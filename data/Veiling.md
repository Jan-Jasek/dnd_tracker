# Veiling

The universe through which the [[Eloran/Cosmology|Veil]] flows. Named for the primal energy that defines it — the Veiling is the space where Veil exists.

## Planes

- [[Eloran/Eloran|Eloran]] — the material plane; largely sealed from the Veil since Orion's working
- [[Fey]] — the fey plane (name TBD); dominated by Erathon; saturated with tainted Veil
- [[The Hollow]] — the shadow plane; a hollow reflection of Eloran, created as a byproduct of Erathon's attempted merger

## Cosmology

See [[Eloran/Cosmology]] for the full account of the Veil, the Accord, the Sundering, and the current state of the Veiling.

## Pantheon

Gods of the Veiling are more symbolic than interventionist, especially on Eloran where the Veil is sealed. The Forgotten Realms pantheon exists as lesser celestial powers; the dominant religion on Eloran is the [[Eloran/Organisations/Church of Orion|Church of Orion]].
