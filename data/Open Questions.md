# Open Questions — Weaving

> Part of: [[Weaving]]

Universe-level unresolved questions. For plane-specific open questions on Eloran, see [[Eloran/Notes/Open Questions]].

## Cosmology

- What caused the Sundering that shattered the Weave and broke the Paths between planes?
- How many pre-Sundering beings exist across the Weaving, and what are they doing?
- Are any pre-Sundering beings benevolent, or does isolation inevitably corrupt them?
- What is the name of the Fey plane?
- What do the pre-Sundering beings in the Fey plane (besides Erathon) want?
- What is the nature of [[The Hollow]] — is it stable, growing, or decaying?
- Does the Weaving have a fixed boundary, or does it extend infinitely through the Paths?

## Pre-Sundering History

- Was the Accord a unified civilization, or simply a state of being?
- Did pre-Sundering beings have names, allegiances, conflicts? Or did the Sundering create the conditions for individuality?

## The Weave

- Can the Weave be restored across the Weaving, or only locally?
- If a plane's seal is broken (as Eloran's partially has been), does the broken plane affect other planes?
