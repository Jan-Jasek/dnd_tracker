# Weaving (Tkaní)

The **Weaving** (_Tkaní_) is the universe through which the [[Eloran/Cosmology#The Weave|Weave]] (_Předivo_) flows. Its name evokes the whole woven from that primal substance.

## Planes

- [[Eloran/Eloran|Eloran]] — the material plane; largely sealed from the Weave since Orion's working
- [[Fey]] — the fey plane (name TBD); dominated by Erathon; saturated with tainted Weave
- [[The Hollow]] — the shadow plane; a hollow reflection of Eloran, created as a byproduct of Erathon's attempted merger

## Cosmology

See [[Eloran/Cosmology]] for the full account of the Weave, the Accord, the Sundering, and the current state of the Weaving.

## Pantheon

Gods of the Weaving are more symbolic than interventionist, especially on Eloran where the Weave is sealed. The Forgotten Realms pantheon exists as lesser celestial powers; the dominant religion on Eloran is the [[Eloran/Organisations/Church of Orion|Church of Orion]].
