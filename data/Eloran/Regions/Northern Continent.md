# Northern Continent

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | See also: [[Places/Regions]], [[Regions/Southern Continent]], [[Places/Dragon Islands]]

The main landmass of the material plane — working name **Northern Continent**, set against the [[Regions/Southern Continent|Southern Continent]] across the sea. The colonists of the [[Places/Dragon Islands|Dragon Islands]] come from here.

## States and Powers

- [[Factions/United Empire]] — Imperial heartland in the south of the continent
- [[Factions/Meredie]] — fortress-kingdom of mountains and rivers
- [[Factions/Triskal]] — coastal merchant federation in the east
- [[Factions/Valdie]] — the historical north
- [[Factions/Grundarmheld]] — dwarf mountain kingdom beyond the Shadow Pass
- **Skeld Isles** (working name) — a northern archipelago belonging to the continent; British-inspired

Geography, the Red River, and borders: [[Places/Regions]].
