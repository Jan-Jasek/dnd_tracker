# Order of the Just (Řád Spravedlivých)

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | See also: [[Organisations/Apostolic Church of Orion]], [[Organisations/Church of Orion]], [[People/Orion]]

The **Templar order** that exists to **eliminate magic**. Also called **the Just Ones** (_Spravedliví_). Co-founded by Ilrusk among the nine disciples — though the coup came long after founding.

They overthrew the **mage oligarchy** inside the [[Organisations/Apostolic Church of Orion|Apostolic Church of Orion]] pre-PS ~155. They did not replace the clergy. They took the church over **unofficially** and left the Apostolics in the vestments. Complementary: Just swords, apostolic words. Same Saint.

Since the Shattering the Marble Fortress is gone. Orestor's templars are divided roughly evenly between those remaining with the Just and **[[Organisations/Orion's Wrath|Orionův hněv]]**, with Tillibor's faction steadily gaining ground. The Just continue their traditional duties, including protection against magical corruption. **[[People/Benedikt|Páter Benedikt]]** leads the local Just from the old temple and believes the Order must adapt to returning magic. The Apostolic clergy is **[[People/Jeroným|Arcibiskup Jeroným]]**.

## Crisis after Ellesmere

Many templars regard the destruction of [[Places/Ellesmere|Ellesmere]] as divine punishment for the Order's abuses. The city's survivors suffer a wasting corruption. Magical creatures appear and gifts awaken among ordinary people as the Veil returns, overwhelming an institution built to suppress magic.

In Orestor, Benedikt questions both the purpose and practicality of indiscriminate interventions; Tillibor seeks exclusive control of magic through Hněv, with recruitment and coercive exploitation of magical people among the methods under discussion. How the templars detect Veil, and how they distinguish danger amid ubiquitous magical activity, remain open. Local reform does not establish a uniform change of policy among continental branches.

## What They Opposed

The **mage-tainted hierarchy** — Maegi pulling the Saint's church. Maegi lineages, institutional magic, the oligarchy behind the apostolic myth. Parish devotion stayed licit.

## Doctrine Under the Just Ones

- Magic in any form is a capital crime
- Necromancy and demon-summoning: kill on sight
- The world's slow withering is the price of the Saint's seal
- Registration and supervision of any surviving Veil talent where states negotiate with the Order (post-Ellesmere reforms vary by realm)

The faithful name the **Just Age** (_Věk spravedlnosti_) — pre-PS ~155 → PS 0, a separate era from the Age of Salvation, when justice rather than mage oligarchy governs the church.

## Structure

### Rank & Symbol

Golden plate gauntlet on a white field. Senior templars bear stars above the gauntlet.

### The Hammer of Orion

Elite militant faction. Symbol: gauntlet crossed with warhammer. Hunts the most dangerous Veil-wielders, Erathon fragments, and Ellesmere survivors.

### Branches & Garrisons

- **Marble Fortress** (_Mramorová pevnost_) — Ellesmere headquarters until the explosion
- Meredic and Imperial garrisons across the continent; paladin remnants in shattered borderlands (e.g. Hlubina)

### Orion's Wrath (Orestor)

Meredian templars who survived the Shattering formed **[[Organisations/Orion's Wrath|Orion's Wrath]]** (_Orionův hněv_) in **[[Places/Orestor|Orestor]]**. Templars remaining with the Just maintain a local presence alongside the old temple. Their military command and relationship to continental leadership remain open. See that file for doctrine.

## Severite / Mlčoun

The Order's signature tool — Veil-suppressing metal for shackles, blades, and armor.

- **Severite** — Imperial / Common (_oddělovač_)
- **Mlčoun** — Meredic (_mlčet_, to silence)

**Hidden truth:** Ilrusk provided the forge-knowledge when the Order was founded. Why he armed the institution that would hunt Maegi — including his own kind — remains contested.

## Key Events

- pre-PS ~155: political control of Ellesmere; **Maegi culling**; coup against Apostolic mage governance; **Just Age** begins
- PS 0: Ellesmere destroyed; Marble Fortress epicenter; Order decimated at city scale
