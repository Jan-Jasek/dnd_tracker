# Brauer & Hess

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | [[Places/Orestor]] | [[Campaigns/Orestor]] | See also: [[Factions/United Empire]], [[People/Jeod Isthar]]

Imperial bank, **established** in Orestor. Letters of credit, cargo, money at the river mouth. Already a client of the **[[Organisations/Legal Consultate|legal consultate]]**.

## Hidden Truth

**[[People/Jeod Isthar|Jeod Isthar]]** owns the bank. The names on the door are a sign. In the [[Campaigns/Orestor|campaign draft]], he commissions recovery of the amethyst through this house once he learns of it. The bank has no knowledge connecting the stone to the ritual attacks.
