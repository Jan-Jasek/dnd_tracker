# Bořiči — Wreckers

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | [[Places/Orestor]] | [[Campaigns/Orestor]]

Street wreckers. Common folk — dock workers, refugees, artisans. Some woke to **Veil-gift** after the Shattering: untrained, wild.

A revolutionary socialist movement. They share opponents and street support with **[[People/Alynn|Alynn]]'s merchant republicans**, whose merchant interests conflict with their economic aims. The two camps have not yet fully recognized the conflict within their alliance. Their precise programme, organisation, and methods remain open. **Bořiči** is also a sub-area of the city.

Detailed programme, named membership, and methods: [[Notes/Open Questions#Orestor]]. Opening job: `ideas/eloran/orestor-opening-job.md`.

## Hidden Leadership

**[[People/The Judge|The Judge]]** is both an Orestor magistrate and the revolutionary leader. His nickname predates his judicial appointment. Common-born and outwardly bureaucratic, he believes democracy requires destroying the entrenched order through **the Great Change**.

His Veil teacher and secret patron is **[[People/Ilrusk|Rewyn Ilrusk]], the Weaver**. Ilrusk cultivates the movement to retain control of Orestor and connects to its leadership through the Judge alone. The Judge believes his patron shares the purpose of his revolution.

Ordinary members do not have a reliable view of the command chain. They may identify different prominent members as the Weaver or believe the Weaver and Judge are the same person. Which leaders deal with recruits, lead secret meetings, and know the patron's role is under development in [the cast preparation](../../../ideas/eloran/orestor-preparation-questions.md).
