# Orionovi Zvěstovatelé — Heralds of Orion

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | [[Places/Orestor]] | [[Campaigns/Orestor]] | See also: [[People/Selith]], [[People/Jeroným]], [[People/Orion]], [[Organisations/Church of Orion]], [[Organisations/Orion's Wrath]]

One of three **power factions** in Orestor, with **[[Organisations/Mlcenlivy|Mlčenliví]]** and **[[Organisations/Orion's Wrath|Orionův hněv]]**.

They believe **[[People/Orion|Orion]] has returned to save us**. Sinners must be **cleansed**.

A figure that looks like Orion has been visiting people at night, convincing them, marking them. The Heralds are those marks. Slogans and ritual sites have appeared. Internal structure and the staging of later deaths: [[Notes/Open Questions#Orestor]].

## Public Face

Heralds of the Saint. Salvation requires a clean city.

## Wants

Save the city. Cleanse sinners.
