# Orion's Wrath (Orionův hněv)

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | [[Places/Orestor]] | [[Campaigns/Orestor]] | Formed from survivors of [[Organisations/Order of the Just|Řád Spravedlivých]]

Independent templar order in **[[Places/Orestor|Orestor]]**, formed after **the Shattering** from Meredian Just Ones who survived Meredie's collapse. They hold **roughly half the templars** in the port; the other half remain with the Just. Tillibor gains the advantage day by day. Both branches claim a duty to protect against magical corruption. **[[People/Benedikt|Páter Benedikt]]** leads the local Just from the old temple and seeks to adapt the Order to returning magic. The temple and Hněv hate each other. Benedikt loses ground every day.

Their name in the city: **Orionův hněv**.

## Schism after Ellesmere

The destruction of [[Places/Ellesmere|Ellesmere]] broke the Order's leadership and confidence. Many templars interpret it as divine wrath for their own abuses. Survivors suffer wasting corruption. The Veil's resurgence brings magical creatures and newly gifted people into ordinary life; the old policy of treating every trace of magic as grounds for suppression becomes increasingly unworkable.

**[[People/Benedikt|Benedikt]]** leads the Just in Orestor toward adaptation. **Lucian Tillibor** answers the same crisis with escalating extremism: he seeks to make Hněv the sole authority controlling magic. Magical users must come under its control, potentially through recruitment or enslavement and use against other magical threats. The balance between these methods and killing those who resist remains for development.

Their followers divide local templar numbers roughly evenly, but Tillibor gains ground daily. The temple and Hněv are rivals for religious authority, men, and political support. Their precise internal command arrangements remain open.

## Doctrine and methods

Hněv claims a holy duty to protect the city by monopolizing the control of magic and suppressing independent magical power. Tillibor's willingness to exploit magical people serves that ambition. Methods of coercion, terms of recruitment, and the limits of enforcement within the city's compact remain open.

Templar patrols investigate suspected corruption, including in the catacombs, and claim authority to act without waiting for civic writs. The means by which they sense Veil, its precision, and its usefulness amid widespread magical resurgence need defining. A magical presence does not by itself distinguish an immediate threat from a newly awakened gift.

## Political position

Tillibor leads Hněv. The faction holds votes in the **Veřejná rada** and seeks influence over closed-council decisions; it has no dedicated Konšel seat. A proposed political representative, Brother František, is being developed in the [schism draft](../../../ideas/eloran/orestor-templar-schism.md). **Jaskell** and **Jimmy** are named templars without established political offices.
