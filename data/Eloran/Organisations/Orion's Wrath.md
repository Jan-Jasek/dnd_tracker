# Orion's Wrath (Orionův hněv)

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | [[Places/Orestor]] | [[Campaigns/Orestor]] | Formed from survivors of [[Organisations/Order of the Just|Řád Spravedlivých]]

Independent templar order in **[[Places/Orestor|Orestor]]**, formed after **the Shattering** from Meredian Just Ones who survived Meredie's collapse. Working name _Orionův hněv_ until a final title is chosen.

## Origin (PS 0)

Meredian **Spravedliví** who regrouped in Orestor faced a choice:

- obey the old **Marble Fortress** doctrine (all magic is capital crime; the seal's slow death is Orion's price), or
- adapt to a world of **scattered Erathon fragments**, returning Veil, and a dead headquarters.

The faction that became **Orion's Wrath** broke from continental command. Templars who insisted on the old rulebook were killed in an internal purge.

## How They Differ from Řád Spravedlivých

|                      | **Řád Spravedlivých** (continent / Triskal)                     | **Orionův hněv** (Orestor)                                                                          |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Enemy**            | Maegi lineages; unsanctioned magic broadly                      | **Fragments and possession** first — Erathon's shards in bodies and stones                          |
| **Magic**            | Capital crime (Branon's Triskal reform: register and supervise) | **Pragmatic:** gifted fighters may serve if they hunt fragments; Maegi oligarchy still hated        |
| **Mission**          | Church-state order; Severite suppression                        | **Finish Orion's work** — he bound Erathon; shards must be found, contained, or destroyed           |
| **Chain of command** | Arch-templars, **Tyův řád** in Triskal, scattered garrisons     | **Lucian Tillibor** (Arci-Templar); no obedience to Marble Fortress or Branon required              |
| **Methods**          | Trials, registration, institutional hunts                       | **Pairs** patrol catacomb mouths; sense corruption; act without waiting for council writs           |
| **Politics**         | Continental institution                                         | **Orestor city faction** — tolerated under the PS 0 compact because they kill things in the tunnels |

## Why the Split (playable logic)

1. **Speed** — fragment hosts corrupt in days; Spravedliví procedure is too slow.
2. **Marble Fortress is gone** — the order's brain died at Ellesmere; Orestor survivors will not wait for orders that never come.
3. **Theology** — Orion imprisoned the demon; leaving shards loose is **desecration of the Saint's act**, not a licensing question.
4. **Rage** — brothers died in the Shattering and the siege; _hněv_ is literal, not branding.
5. **City survival** — Konšelé accept them as sewer-knives against possession; republicans dislike them; monarchists remember they served the crown once.
