# Apostolic Church of Orion (Apoštolská církev Orionova)

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | See also: [[People/Orion]], [[Organisations/Order of Maegi]], [[Organisations/Order of the Just]], [[Organisations/Church of Orion]]

The **founding church** — built by Orion's nine disciples immediately after the betrayal. Public face: Orion as martyr and divine savior. Hidden governance: the disciples themselves, and later Maegi successors, pulling strings from behind the saint's myth.

## Followers — Orionists (Orionisté)

**Orionists** worship Orion as Saint, Martyr, and effectively god. They are not the enemy of the later [[Organisations/Order of the Just|Order of the Just]] — the Just Ones moved against the **mage-tainted institution**, not against devotion to Orion itself.

Most common folk who pray to the Saint are Orionists in practice, whether or not they know the church's political history.

## Public Doctrine

- Orion confronted Erathon and sacrificed himself to seal the corruption
- The Veil is dangerous; only the Saint's sacrifice keeps the world safe
- The Apostolic succession runs from Orion's nine disciples — the "bereaved apostles"

## Hidden Truth

A cover-up from day one. The disciples betrayed Orion during the sealing ritual and needed a religion that made their version of events the only acceptable one.

For most of the **Age of Salvation** (_Věk spásy_), this church was the continental faith — secretly steered by Veil-wielders who knew the truth. It launched the crusade against rival lineages (see _Krvavá válka druidů_ in legend).

## Fall

The [[Organisations/Order of the Just|Order of the Just]] (_Řád Spravedlivých_) overthrew mage control of the church hierarchy ~150 years before campaign start. The Apostolic Church as a governing power ended; what remained was faith without the mage oligarchy — and the Just Ones' military regime on top.

Orionist belief persisted. The institution did not.
