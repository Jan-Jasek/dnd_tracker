# Order of Maegi

An old order of mages, connected to Orion's legacy and active in both the Ellesmere era and Orestor. Members use distinctive rings tied to their school of magic.

## Sibyla Fayen

Road encounter: appears as a harmless old woman. Actually a member of the Order. High-level quest hook.

## Sir Hans

Former templar of the Just. Has taken it upon himself to hunt the mages who escaped Ellesmere before it was destroyed. Those mages were imprisoned for ~150 years and are mostly insane. Sir Hans travels with a company of templars. High-level quest hook.

## Open Questions
