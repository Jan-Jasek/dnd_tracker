# Order of Maegi

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | See also: [[Cosmology]], [[People/Orion]]

An order of Weave practitioners founded on [[People/Orion|Orion's]] complete framework of the Art. The most structured and powerful Weave lineage in existence. Members use distinctive rings tied to their school of magic — a degraded echo of the original nine-power structure.

---

## Nature of a Maegi

Maegi do not die of natural causes. Once a person has been made into a Maegi, they are effectively immortal against age, disease, and slow decay. They can still be killed — by violence, by Weave-related catastrophe, by their own choice — but time alone does not claim them.

This means that the Order's longest-lived members are _deeply_ old. Some of those listed as members may be centuries or millennia in age. The Order's broader membership today consists overwhelmingly of post-founding Maegi made by lineage transmission from the original disciples and their successors.

## How Maegi Are Made

**Open question.** What is canon: the creation ritual is built around **blood**. Living creatures hold a great deal of Weave — Weave concentrates in life — and Maegi creation channels that concentration through blood rituals derived from Orion's earliest framings. The specific mechanics, costs, requirements, and constraints are unspecified.

The earlier "blood mage" lineages from Orion's pre-Maegi attempts may be related — the same root technique, less refined.

## Founding

Orion's final and most complete framework of Weave control: **the Art**, structured as ten distinct powers. His nine inner disciples — each gifted with exactly one of the ten powers — established their own disciples in turn, effectively founding the Order to outlast them.

The nine bound Erathon into the Ellesmere **rift**. Their own students were not in the chamber. They betrayed Orion at the 10th seal. He merged with Erathon. Some of the nine died in that blast; survivors were marked and withered or went mad; some may have been destroyed in the merge.

**[[People/Ilrusk|Ilrusk]]** understood the marking. He sacrificed a student and took the body. He lives in [[Places/Orestor]] at PS 2.

The Church of Orion is the cover-up. Later Maegi were never told the rift existed. See [[People/Orion]].

The ring system (diamond, citrine, etc.) reflects the original nine-power division, though present members no longer know the full structure or that the tenth power exists.

## The Art vs. Other Weave Lineages

The Order of Maegi is Orion's _final_ attempt. Before it, he made cruder, earlier frameworks that produced practitioners with real but divergent and uncontrolled Weave access — blood mages and other lineages. Those lineages persist. They are not lesser in raw terms, just structurally incomplete relative to the Art.

The Art's advantage: it is a _controlled_ relationship with the Weave. Practitioners bend the Weave to their will rather than being shaped by it.

## Sibyla Fayen

Road encounter: appears as a harmless old woman. Actually a member of the Order. High-level quest hook.

## Sir Hans

Former templar of the Just. Has taken it upon himself to hunt the mages who escaped Ellesmere before it was destroyed. Those mages were imprisoned since the pre-PS ~155 culling and are mostly insane. Sir Hans travels with a company of templars. High-level quest hook.
