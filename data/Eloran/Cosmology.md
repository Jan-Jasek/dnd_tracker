# Veiling Cosmology

> Part of: [[Veiling]]

The cosmology of the [[Veiling]] is defined by a single cataclysm — the Sundering — that shattered a harmonious universe into isolated, hungry fragments.

## The Veil

The Veil is the primal energy of the Veiling. It flows through all things and connects all planes through channels called **the Paths**. Before the Sundering, it was unified and abundant. Those who touched it felt calm, powerful, arrogant — a sense of being part of everything, because they were.

On [[Eloran]], the surface expression of the Veil is called **the Art** by the [[Organisations/Order of Maegi]]. Common folk call it magic. Orion's inner circle understood they were working with something much older.

## The Accord — Pre-Sundering Era

Before the Sundering, the Veiling existed in a state called **the Accord**. The Veil flowed freely through the Paths between planes. Pre-Sundering beings inhabited the universe as part of this whole — immense, connected, neither hungry nor desperate. It was not a single civilization but a state of being.

## The Sundering

**Cause: unknown** — whether natural collapse, war between pre-Sundering beings, or outside force is one of the great open questions of the Veiling.

The Sundering shattered the Veil into isolated pockets. The Paths broke or corrupted. Parts of the Veil became hungry — tainted by isolation and desperation.

Two planes were severed together: Eloran and the [[Fey|Fey plane]].

## Pre-Sundering Beings

Pre-Sundering beings were not destroyed — they were trapped in isolated pockets of Veil. Each pocket is finite. Beings that dominate their pockets grow hungry, feeding on whatever Veil remains within reach. Many such beings exist across the Veiling. Most are unknown to Eloran.

## Erathon

One of many pre-Sundering beings. After the Sundering, Erathon found himself trapped in a pocket of Veil overlapping the [[Fey|Fey plane]]. He leeched from it, dominated it, and grew strong enough to begin feeding from Eloran as well.

Attempting to merge Eloran with the Fey plane — and consume both — he created a byproduct: [[../The Hollow|The Hollow]], a dead reflection of Eloran pressed into the gap between the two worlds. The merger failed. The Hollow remains.

His tainted Veil corrupted those on Eloran who could access it. Veil users became hungry for power. Wars followed. Civilizations collapsed. This is the **Age of Darkness**.

## Orion and the Binding

Orion — a mortal born in the Age of Darkness — developed a complete framework for controlling the tainted Veil: **the Art**, structured as ten powers. He made earlier, cruder attempts first, which produced other Veil lineages (blood mages and others). The [[Organisations/Order of Maegi]] was his final and most complete work.

He had nine inner disciples, each gifted with one of the ten powers; he kept the tenth. The nine seals were used to bind Erathon into the crystal — successfully, without consuming the disciples.

When Orion attempted the larger ritual to seal Eloran from the Paths permanently, his disciples betrayed him. Orion sacrificed his own Veil to force a partial completion of the 10th seal alone. The result was an imperfect sealing — enough to cut Eloran off from the Paths, not enough to do whatever Orion's full plan required. The Veil began draining slowly with no way to replenish.

The disciples survived, founded the [[Organisations/Church of Orion|Church of Orion]] as a cover-up, and **launched a crusade against the rival powers of the era** — earlier Veil lineages, druids, and other figures of power who had been near Orion. The _Krvavá válka druidů_ in legend, and the crypt of an old Maegi enemy reportedly held in Temnoles, are echoes of this crusade. Over centuries all but Ilrusk died. The fragments scattered by Jeod Isthar's destruction of the Ellesmere seal carry **Erathon's essence laced with traces of Orion's sacrificed Veil** — not the disciples.

See [[People/Orion]] for the full account.

## The Slow Depletion

Orion's sealing was incomplete. The Veil on Eloran has been slowly draining since — consumed with no way to replenish through the blocked Paths. Over thousands of years, this caused species to wither, civilizations to fade, and the Art to weaken. Orion likely had a different solution in mind — the 10th seal — but never completed it.

## The Templar Era and the Ellesmere Culling

Over centuries the [[Organisations/Church of Orion|Church of Orion]] grew autonomous. The Templars (co-founded by Ilrusk, equipped with Severite / Mlčoun) overtook its leadership and turned anti-Veil enforcement on the Maegi lineages themselves. ~100–150 years before campaign start, the Templars took political control of Ellesmere and culled the Maegi practitioners there.

## Jeod Isthar and the Ellesmere Explosion

[[People/Jeod Isthar|Jeod Isthar]] — a Maegi up to ~500 years old who witnessed the Templar rise and the Ellesmere culling, _not_ a founding-era Maegi — destroyed the seal at Ellesmere ~3 years before campaign start. His **primary goal**: restore the Veil before Eloran finished dying. The catastrophic blast was **collateral**: he knew it would level the city, did nothing to prevent it, and accepted both Templar and civilian deaths as the cost.

He was right that the world was dying. He chose to act with full knowledge of the price and indifference to most of it.

The destruction of the Ellesmere seal shattered the crystal. The soul fragments — Erathon's essence laced with Orion's Veil residue — scattered across the continent. This is the present-day catalyst for the campaigns. Jeod survived.
