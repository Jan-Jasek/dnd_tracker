# Weaving Cosmology

> Part of: [[Weaving]]

The cosmology of the [[Weaving]] is defined by a single cataclysm — the Sundering — that shattered a harmonious universe into isolated, hungry fragments.

## The Weave

The **Weave** (_Předivo_) is the primal energy and underlying substance of the **Weaving** (_Tkaní_). It flows through all things and connects all planes through channels called **the Paths**. Before the Sundering, it was unified and abundant. Those who touched it felt calm, powerful, arrogant — a sense of being part of everything, because they were.

On [[Eloran]], the surface expression of the Weave is called **the Art** by the [[Organisations/Order of Maegi]]. Common folk call it magic. Orion's inner circle understood they were working with something much older.

## The Accord — Pre-Sundering Era

Before the Sundering, the Weaving existed in a state called **the Accord**. The Weave flowed freely through the Paths between planes. Pre-Sundering beings inhabited the universe as part of this whole — immense, connected, neither hungry nor desperate. It was not a single civilization but a state of being.

## The Sundering

**Cause: unknown** — whether natural collapse, war between pre-Sundering beings, or outside force is one of the great open questions of the Weaving.

The Sundering shattered the Weave into isolated pockets. The Paths broke or corrupted. Parts of the Weave became hungry — tainted by isolation and desperation.

Two planes were severed together: Eloran and the [[Fey|Fey plane]].

## Pre-Sundering Beings

Pre-Sundering beings were not destroyed — they were trapped in isolated pockets of Weave. Each pocket is finite. Beings that dominate their pockets grow hungry, feeding on whatever Weave remains within reach. Many such beings exist across the Weaving. Most are unknown to Eloran.

## Erathon

One of many pre-Sundering beings. After the Sundering, Erathon found himself trapped in a pocket of Weave overlapping the [[Fey|Fey plane]]. He leeched from it, dominated it, and grew strong enough to begin feeding from Eloran as well.

Attempting to merge Eloran with the Fey plane — and consume both — he created a byproduct: [[../The Hollow|The Hollow]], a dead reflection of Eloran pressed into the gap between the two worlds. The merger failed. The Hollow remains.

Before the Sundering, Eloran knew the **Age of Harmony** (_Věk harmonie_) — peak civilizations under a freely flowing Weave; dates unknown.

His tainted Weave corrupted those on Eloran who could access it. Weave users became hungry for power. Wars followed. Civilizations collapsed. This is the **Age of Darkness**.

## Orion and the Binding

Orion — a mortal born in the Age of Darkness — developed a complete framework for controlling the tainted Weave: **the Art**, structured as ten powers. He made earlier, cruder attempts first, which produced other Weave lineages (blood mages and others). The [[Organisations/Order of Maegi]] was his final and most complete work.

He had nine inner disciples, each gifted with one of the ten powers; he kept the tenth. He chose Ellesmere because the Paths were strong there. The working bound Erathon into a **floating rift** that consumed anyone who touched it. The rift could not be moved. A fortress was built around it. The nine's own students were not there.

When Orion attempted the larger ritual to seal Eloran from the Paths, his disciples betrayed him. He forced a partial 10th seal by spending his own Weave. He **merged with Erathon** in the rift — not the ending he meant. Some of the nine died in that blast. Survivors were marked by Erathon's influence and withered or went mad. Some may have been pulled into the merge and destroyed. **[[People/Ilrusk|Ilrusk]]** understood the marking, sacrificed a student, and took that body.

The Church of Orion is the cover-up. Later Maegi were never told the rift existed.

**Orion's Seal** held the rift in place. It was a unique working: ten seals combined into one permanent seal, a hovering black hollow in the depths of Ellesmere that held Orion's and Erathon's essence. When [[People/Jeod Isthar|Jeod]] destroyed it, the rift collapsed and the Seal shattered. The essence it held **crystallized** into solid fragments: pure Weave of **Orion and Erathon**, perhaps of some of the nine as well.

See [[People/Orion]] for the full account.

## Seals and Breaches

A **seal** (_pečeť_) is a protective working that closes the barrier between Eloran and the Weave; casting one is **sealing**. Orion's Seal is the one unique creation built from ten of them.

An **inverted seal** (_obrácená pečeť_) is a seal cast in reverse. It opens a temporary, local tunnel through the barrier through which Weave energy can pass. Common speech, witnesses, and templars call the result a **breach** (_průlom_). An ordinary sealing then binds the breach so it stays local. Blood rituals use the pair often. Each of the nine disciples and their lineages guarded their own seals; no one knows the tenth. Exactly how Jeod and those who helped him destroyed Orion's Seal at Ellesmere is unknown.

Opening a breach takes enormous energy. Known sources are the blood of very old lineages, which often carry demon blood from before Orion's days, or royal blood; many ordinary sacrifices; or the caster's own essence, at the cost of a large part of it.

## The Slow Depletion

Orion's sealing was incomplete. The Weave on Eloran has been slowly draining since — consumed with no way to replenish through the blocked Paths. Over thousands of years, this caused species to wither, civilizations to fade, and the Art to weaken. Orion likely had a different solution in mind — the 10th seal — but never completed it.

## The Just Age and the Ellesmere Culling

Over centuries the [[Organisations/Apostolic Church of Orion|Apostolic Church]] grew autonomous — mage-governed behind the Orionist myth. pre-PS ~155 the [[Organisations/Order of the Just|Order of the Just]] (_Řád Spravedlivých_) took political control of Ellesmere, culled Maegi practitioners there, and overthrew apostolic mage governance — beginning the **Just Age** (_Věk spravedlnosti_). They hunted Maegi lineages, not Orionist worship.

## Jeod Isthar and the Shattering

[[People/Jeod Isthar|Jeod Isthar]] — a Maegi up to ~500 years old who witnessed the Templar rise and the Ellesmere culling, _not_ a founding-era Maegi — destroyed Orion's Seal at Ellesmere at **PS 0** (**the Shattering**; _Roztříštění_ / _Rozštěpení_). Goal: restore the Weave. He knew the blast would take the Marble Fortress. He did not know the rift would collapse. He did not expect the scale. He did not care. Fragments of the broken seal — Orion and Erathon, solid — scattered. Jeod survived.

The collapse sent the Weave surging back into Eloran. Magical creatures appear and previously ordinary people awaken to magical abilities. Survivors of [[Places/Ellesmere|Ellesmere]] suffer a wasting corruption; the full nature and progression of that condition remain open.
