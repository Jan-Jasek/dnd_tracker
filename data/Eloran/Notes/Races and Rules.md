# Races and Rules

Player-facing house rules and race notes for the Eloran campaign.

## House Rules

### Character Creation

- Allowed books: all official except Tasha's (case by case); plus Blood Hunter and Revised Ranger
- Starting level: 3
- Standard gear per rules

### Ability Scores

- All races replace normal racial bonuses with **+3 points distributed freely** (max +2 to any one stat)
- Darkvision: Dwarves and Gnomes only (removed from all other races)
- Humans: custom racial abilities matching the character concept instead of the standard feat

### Death and Injury

- No resurrection spells — death is permanent
- On knockdown: **Injuries from DMG** (slightly modified) instead of exhaustion levels

### Magic Restrictions

- No teleportation beyond short range (Misty Step and Dimension Door are fine; nothing further)
- No Leomond's Tiny Hut
- **Identify** = arcane check requiring proficiency (not a spell)
- Detect Magic remains a spell

### Inspiration

Bonus dice instead of advantage:

- 1d4, 1d6, or re-roll — awarded by the DM

## Races

### Humans

Overwhelming majority in civilized lands. Custom racial bonuses matching character concept (instead of standard feat).

### Dwarves

Mountain kingdom **Grundarmheld**. Extremely caste-based. Travel outside kingdom affairs is forbidden. Exile is the common serious punishment — exiled dwarves are permanently branded; their descendants cannot wear clan insignia. Gnomes are the only non-dwarven race tolerated in Grundarmheld.

### Elves

Very rare. Integration with humans historically produced terrible ghettos (misery and exploitation). Most elves withdrew to isolated forest clans and settlements with their own hierarchy. Encountered humans will often be loners or exiles from those clans.

### Gnomes

Rare but well integrated; live normally among humans. Also tolerated in Grundarmheld. The gnomish family Tarkelby in Orestor are jewelers.

### Halflings

Well integrated, live normally among humans.

### Dragonborn

Not a separate race. **Dragonborn** (_drakorození_) is the continental name for Dragon Islands natives taken as slaves — humans carrying draconic blood, mostly the dark-eyed, whose looks are more lizard-like: scaly patches, slit pupils. About 20 years ago, northern Eloran abolished Dragonborn slavery, causing population growth and the formation of isolated settlements. Those settlements are regularly raided by southern slavers. In the southern Empire and Triskal, a well-presented Dragonborn slave is a status symbol. The slave trade with the southern Empire is active.

On the Dragon Islands, free dark-eyed communities hold the inhospitable uncolonized coastline. See [[Places/Dragon Islands#The Dark-Eyed Blessing]].

### Orcs

Mostly pushed to mountains and exterminated. Half-orcs are a rarity in recent decades.

### Tieflings — Not Playable

Demons have no active presence in this world. Anyone visibly resembling demonic heritage is killed by mobs or the Order of the Just. Not a viable ancestry.

### Underdark Races — Not Playable

If an Underdark exists, nothing from it has surfaced. No opportunity to have encountered or learned from these cultures.

## Session Zero Notes

- Characters do not come from the Dragon Islands — they are from Eloran
- Backstory for magic-using classes (druids, warlocks, wizards, clerics, paladins) is discussed individually
- Option A: Define backstory and start at level 3
- Option B: Railroaded prologue through shared earlier experiences (level 1 without abilities); mutual bonds, traits/flaws distributed in-session
- Assumption: party has known each other for just over a year and has a shared goal/ideal
