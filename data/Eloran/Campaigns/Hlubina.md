# Hlubina — The Prologue Arc

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | [[Veiling]] | Precedes: [[Campaigns/Dragon Independence]]

The campaign that precedes Dragon Independence. Players arrive as mercenaries protecting a refugee caravan and end up working for a local crime boss. Ends with imperial advance under **Captain Aemon Whiteoak (Aemon Bělodub)**; **Kate** (Jeod Isthar's apprentice) recruits them into imperial service.

## Setting

Hlubina — a small marshland village built on a water-protected center (moat connected to a river, old palisade). Near Ellesmere (destroyed). Post-war refugee context: the village is filling with displaced people, food is scarce, the imperial garrison left after the army was decimated at Orestor.

## Arrival

Players arrive with a refugee caravan organized by Otto (merchant with no goods; used the players as bodyguards for 1 gold coin). Caravan: ~20 people — three farming families (two human, one halfling; 7 children), Otto and his wife, poacher Držgrešle, former elf blacksmith Austin, and Bardon (real armor, fled Triskal law).

Road events before Hlubina:

- Felled tree with old dried blood traces; following the blood leads to a half-eaten imperial army horse (with an arrow in it). Saddlebags: 5 days' food, tent, blanket.
- Night kobold ambush: kobolds bring captured monsters (2× Grimlock, 2× Gnoll Withering) with half-tied legs as distraction while trying to steal from the wagons. DC 15 Perception to notice.
- Bardon vs. Držgrešle brawl: Bardon attacks Držgrešle for rifling through his things; Držgrešle claims he was looking for a pendant from his (now dead) mother.

## Hlubina

The village is controlled by **Skrček** — a fat, cruel halfling/dwarf crime boss who owned the surrounding marshland before the war (unused then). Now refugees are squatting on his land and he extorts them. The remaining imperial garrison captain was bought off by Skrček's lieutenant Jon Syčák.

### The Opposition

**Hector "Zkurvysyn"** and the Osvoboditeli (Liberators):

- 20 fighters operating from the eastern marshes
- Hector has history with Skrček — he helped Skrček clear out the remaining imperial soldiers after the war, then Skrček tried to kill him; Hector survived by hiding in a chimney for a day

### Ilis

Gnome sorcerer girl (lvl 1), sold by her frightened parents to Skrček for 1–5 silver. Her family fled from near Ellesmere; the infection touched her. Skrček wants her magic for himself.

Her backstory (via Dagobar's farm): family found in the marshes; brother died on arrival; mother died shortly after. Ilis helped on the farm until she tried to leave — was restrained after burning part of the farm. She is not in control of her abilities.

### Dagobar's Farm

Remote farm island in the marshes. Dagobar (~60, long ponytail) and his sons (Alaric, Asger, Calder, Colborn, Dagobar Jr.). Built on ruins of an old necromantic stone temple — undead avoid it due to ancient wards.

The cave near the farm is haunted by **Ariadne** (a spectra — ghost of a woman who died fleeing south after Ellesmere fell; Dagobar stole her silver locket with a picture of a little girl). Destroying or returning the locket banishes the spectra. 3 of Dagobar's grandsons went into the cave and didn't return.

Cave encounter: 1× Ghoul (resistances) + 3× zombie. When the ghoul is killed its essence transfers to the next living zombie.

## Resolution

Skrček stages a night raid on the Liberators' position (using Ilis to set fire to the inn/tavern). A large battle breaks out. Regardless of outcome, the next morning an imperial advance guard arrives under **Captain Aemon Whiteoak (Aemon Bělodub)**. **Kate** recruits the players into imperial service — handoff toward [[Campaigns/Dragon Independence]].
