# Orestor

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | [[Veiling]] | See also: [[Places/Orestor]], [[People/Eirys]], [[Organisations/Mlcenlivy]], [[Organisations/Orion's Wrath]], [[History/Timeline]]

**PS 2 M02–M03.** Late winter / early spring of the second year after the Shattering. City campaign in **[[Places/Orestor|Orestor]]**, the ancient Red River port.

World facts live on the place, org, and people files. This file is the play structure.

## Premise

Orestor is a **tripartite city-republic**: loyalists, monarchists, and republicans locked in a survival compact after the PS 0 siege and coup. An **amethyst fragment** of Erathon has entered the port in the hands of knight **Milbern**. Four powers want it — **[[Organisations/Mlcenlivy|Mlčenliví]]**, **[[Organisations/Orion's Wrath|Orionův hněv]]**, **Orionovi Zvěstovatelé**, and **Bořiči**.

## Starting Situation

The city-republic compact of PS 0–PS 1 still holds. **Konšelé** arbitrate; **Veřejná rada** is the republican façade. **[[People/Eirys|Eirys]]** sits as treasurer and runs the Silents. **Euron Abb'Shiran** speaks for the merchant guild / Bořiči. The purkrabí and family remain monarchist hostages. **Lucian Tillibor** commands Orionův hněv.

See [[Places/Orestor]] for districts, blocs, and the coup sequence.

## Adventure 1 — Searching for Milbern

Opening thread from the Orestor notes:

1. A refugee child finds the amethyst near **Stará tvrz**; the shard drives a massacre; guards deliver it to regent **Stefan Iskelny** (murderer of lord **Harry Broad** — a lowland fortress, not the port coup).
2. Stefan sends knight **Milbern** and a half-dozen men-at-arms to Orestor to have the stone valued and to fence other loot.
3. **Roondar Tarkelby** (gnome jeweler, Orionova čtvrť) finds the stone is not an ordinary amethyst and alerts **Eirys**.
4. Street fight: Silents, Hněv, and Zvěstovatelé collide. City watch arrives. Milbern slips into **Přístavní čtvrť**, disguises as a beggar, hides in a cellar / the catacombs. Two surviving men-at-arms hunt a smuggler who will get him out.
5. **Šprýmař** finds the hide near his smuggling routes. He will sell the location to the Silents in exchange for the death of a rival smuggler boss.
6. Hněv pairs patrol catacomb mouths; they can sense the stone. Harbor gates close. Hunt continues.

**Import variants** (not collapsed): who first hears from Roondar (Eirys vs Euron); order of ambushes on the street. Playable default follows [[Places/Orestor#The Erathon Fragment Plot]] — Eirys is the alert.
