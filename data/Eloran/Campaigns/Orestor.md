# Orestor

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | [[Veiling]] | See also: [[Places/Orestor]], [[People/Eirys]], [[Organisations/Mlcenlivy]], [[Organisations/Orion's Wrath]], [[History/Timeline]]

**PS 2 M02–M03.** Late winter / early spring of the second year after the Shattering. City campaign in **[[Places/Orestor|Orestor]]**, the ancient Red River port.

World facts live on the place, org, and people files. This file is the play structure.

## Premise

Orestor is a **tripartite city-republic**: loyalists, monarchists, and republicans locked in a survival compact after the PS 0 siege and coup. **[[People/Eirys|Eirys]]** holds the treasury and the Silents. Hidden: she is **[[People/Ilrusk|Rewyn Ilrusk]]'s proxy** in the city.

Candidate opening adventure (amethyst / Milbern): `ideas/eloran/orestor-fragment-plot.md`.

## Starting Situation

The city-republic compact of PS 0–PS 1 still holds. **Konšelé** arbitrate; **Veřejná rada** is the republican façade. **[[People/Eirys|Eirys]]** sits as treasurer and runs the Silents. **Euron Abb'Shiran** speaks for the merchant guild / Bořiči. The purkrabí and family remain monarchist hostages. **Lucian Tillibor** commands Orionův hněv.

See [[Places/Orestor]] for districts, blocs, and the coup sequence.

## Where this sits

Meredie's spine is the **Red River** (_Rudá řeka_). See [[Places/Regions#The Red River]]. **[[Places/Gutberg|Gutberg]]** is the south gate (fell PS 0; the metal span did not). **[[Places/Orestor|Orestor]]** sits on the **west** of the delta; Triskal on the **east**. **[[Places/Ellesmere|Ellesmere]]** was the capital in the **[[Places/Ellesmere Region|Ellesmerská kotlin]]** — not yet pinned to a river-mile.

After the Shattering, refugees moved from the crater. The lowlands sit in a cold truce. Orestor is a functioning city-state; Triskal's protectorate and the dead crown both claim it.

**Not placed:** Ellesmere on the river; name of Triskal's east-delta city; name of the west Valdie vassal.
