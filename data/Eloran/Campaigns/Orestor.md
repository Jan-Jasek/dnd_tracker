# Orestor

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | [[Veiling]] | See also: [[Places/Orestor]], [[People/Alynn]], [[People/Oto]], [[People/Lothar]], [[People/Konrad]], [[People/Benedikt]], [[People/Jeroným]], [[People/Selith]], [[People/Ilrusk]], [[Organisations/Mlcenlivy]], [[Organisations/Bořiči]], [[Organisations/Orionovi Zvěstovatelé]], [[Organisations/Orion's Wrath]], [[Organisations/Apostolic Church of Orion]], [[Organisations/Brauer & Hess]], [[History/Timeline]]

**PS 2 M02.** Late winter / early spring of the second year after the Shattering. City campaign in **[[Places/Orestor|Orestor]]**. Same players as **[[Campaigns/Dragon Independence]]** Act I (Ansir).

World facts live on the place, org, and people files. This file is the play structure.

## Premise

Orestor is a **tripartite city-republic**. Six **Konšelé** sit, including **[[People/Jeroným|Arcibiskup Jeroným]]** (Apostolic) and **[[People/Benedikt|Páter Benedikt]]** (Just clergy). The party is a small **[[Organisations/Legal Consultate|legal consultate]]**.

## Play sequence

1. **Opening job** — everyday advocate work gone wrong. Puts the firm among **[[Organisations/Bořiči]]**, **[[Organisations/Orion's Wrath|Hněv]]**, **[[Organisations/Mlcenlivy|Mlčenliví]]**, or several. Details: `ideas/eloran/orestor-opening-job.md`. Candidate: that job helps the **amethyst** into the city.
2. **Amethyst job day** — the stone is in play; **[[Organisations/Brauer & Hess]]** hires the firm (Jeod). The same day: **death 1**, **[[People/Jeroným|Jeroným]]** on the town square (confession, blood, abomination; Hněv cuts the body down). This death is played as a scene. Hunters from that day: the consultate, **Mlčenliví**, **Heralds**, **Hněv**.
3. **Each following day** — the cult works as a unit: Selith picks a target, Heralds bring the person, Selith works the magic, Heralds stage the body. One more found, ritually murdered; some become abominations. People go missing at night. Nine days, nine deaths. Deaths 2–9 are discoveries. Cult details: [[Notes/Open Questions#Orestor]].

After the square the Apostolic flock becomes **Kajícníci** (Orion came to **punish us**; repent). Heralds say he came to **save us** and **cleanse sinners**. Seat 6 is empty. The five remaining Konšelé face replacement of one seat, or a general election.

## Starting Situation

The compact holds. **Six Konšelé** sit: Alynn, Oto, Lothar, Konrad, Benedikt, Jeroným. The purkrabí would preside; he is hostage; the table runs with difficulty. Hněv holds most Just templars in the port and votes in the Veřejná rada. Benedikt holds the old Just temple and waits for Order leadership; he loses ground daily; Hněv and the temple hate each other. Heralds have been marked at night by a figure that looks like Orion; slogans and ritual sites exist. Brauer & Hess is an established Imperial bank (Jeod's, in secret) and already a client of the firm. Ilrusk works at the hospice.

See [[Places/Orestor]] for districts, blocs, and the coup sequence.

## Where this sits

Meredie's spine is the **Red River** (_Rudá řeka_). See [[Places/Regions#The Red River]]. **[[Places/Gutberg|Gutberg]]** is the south gate (fell PS 0; the metal span did not). **[[Places/Orestor|Orestor]]** sits on the **west** of the delta; Triskal on the **east**. **[[Places/Ellesmere|Ellesmere]]** was the capital in the **[[Places/Ellesmere Region|Ellesmerská kotlin]]** — not yet pinned to a river-mile.

After the Shattering, refugees moved from the crater. The lowlands sit in a cold truce. Orestor is a functioning city-state; Triskal's protectorate and the dead crown both claim it.
