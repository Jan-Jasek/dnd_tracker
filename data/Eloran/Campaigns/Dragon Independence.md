# Dragon Independence

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | [[Veiling]] | See also: [[Places/Ansir]], [[Factions/Triple Coalition]], [[People/Snowwhite Carleon]]

Working English title: Dragon Independence. Set in the Dragon Archipelago around Ansir, the capital of the Free Corsair Republic.

The campaign is structured in three acts. The players start as corsairs of the Republic in Act I.

---

## Master Plot

**Hilua** (Vilmaran agent/sorceress) is working to destabilize the Republic and disrupt its truce with Triskal so that Vilmar can take over Ansir. Her tools:
- Captain **Henry Von Telten** (former Imperial admiral) is coerced into weapon smuggling by holding his family hostage in Saltmarsh (through **Xendra**).
- The weapons pass through **Rotgar** (Rotgar is from the Empire; believed killed at the Battle of Corvel; actually operating as a smuggler) and through **Kuliš** in Ansir.
- **Friedrich**, a local underworld figure, acts as intermediary and wants a rival (**Harry**) dead.
- **Tauber dark-eyed** (specifically followers of **Aditya**) were receiving weapons and having cursed bloodlines awakened by Hilua to destabilize the islands from within.
- **Mego** (a Tauber spy sent by Banyu to watch Aditya's faction) was captured near Dirden's farm.

Hilua is killed at some point before Act III. Her second Sending Stone is with Xendra in Saltmarsh.

### Key NPCs (Act I)

**Nastěnka** — Kuliš's girlfriend. Can tell players: Kuliš was closed off about his life; was expecting an important message from Saltmarsh; complained about having to work with "primitives without patience" (the sailors); mentioned Rotgar; received coded messages about weapons deliveries (67 swords and armor pieces in a typical handoff).

**Kuliš** — Weapon smuggler operating in Ansir. Had coded correspondence, most of which is unbreakable. Already dead by Day 1 night. His mistress Nastěnka is the main source of information.

**Friedrich** ("poverty merchant") — Local underworld figure. Knows Kuliš had dealings with Rotgar. Wants Harry killed. Meeting: players are led blindfolded to Friedrich, who makes his request to kill Harry.

**Hemmond** — Commander. Dies evening of Day 3. Cause TBD (assassination ordered by Friedrich? or connected to Harry's ritual?). Found by his young maidservant.

**Captain Kaplan** — Takes command after Hemmond dies.

**Tvígo** — Friedrich's beggar. Noticed Perin investigating. Will approach players in The Local and offer to lead them to where the soldiers went — halfway there, a molotov is thrown from a side alley (Friedrich's attempt to silence him). Later tells players Rotgar took the weapons toward Mokrá Lhota, headed east toward the front.

**Tormund** — Mustachioed leader of the Mokrá Lhota soldiers. Alias. Was heavily drunk by the time the players' side joined; doesn't remember much. Mentioned a wagon left outside the city under a companion's watch; they planned to take a day off before reporting for active duty.

**Prcek** — Arrived with Třesavec and Arryn's men. Led soldiers to the wagon. Drunkenly recruited into the weapon theft.

**Rotgar** — Empire origin; believed dead at the Battle of Corvel. More pirate than soldier. Was actually using Dirden's farm as a smuggling base. Receives the weapons and moves them east toward the front. On the first evening after confrontation, he falls into a coma from blood loss and burns — 3 death saves at disadvantage; dies unless healed.

**Captain Henry Von Telten** — Former Imperial admiral, commanded Rotgar at the southern Imperial border with the Sultanate. His family is being held by Xendra in Saltmarsh. His ship survived a wave on the open sea by chance. On the first stopover after the wave, Hilua came to him and took his family hostage.

**Dalibor** — Hobbit thief. Rotgar's fast courier between Mokrá Lhota and Ansir. Small, unremarkable, very nimble; tries to extract profit from any situation.

**Xendra** — Operating in Saltmarsh. Has Telten's family hostage. Does not know Hilua is dead. Has Hilua's second Sending Stone.

**Hilua** — Vilmaran agent/sorceress. Used Xendra → Gellan Coastal to offer smuggling routes. Wanted to weaken the Republic and disrupt the Triskal truce so Vilmar could take Ansir. Now dead.

### Harry Mansion Encounter

**Harry** — Council member. Not seen publicly for weeks after the coup. His servant makes excuses (he is ill). Family also not seen. Personal guard hasn't left the mansion. Servants act bewitched, refuse to speak. Harry's wife will help the players if they promise to kill Harry and "the Monster."

Harry is ~60 years old in appearance but actually barely forty. Connected to Hedváb (Silky), who has close ties to the Empire — one of few people who could run this scheme. Heard Rotgar is from the Empire and supposedly died at Corvel. Denies ordering the assassination attempt; says his traitorous advisors would otherwise be dead.

**Harry stats:** Resist all except psychic. 24 STR+, 16 DEX+, 20 AC, 10 INT, 16 WIS, 16 CHR. 100 HP. Advantage on STR checks/saves. Legendary Save 2.
- Lair Action: AOE Blood Drain — CON 16 save; nearby enemies take 1d6 force damage, lose that much max HP; Harry heals for damage dealt + temporary HP.
- Actions: 2× Attack (+10, 1d6+7). Close range: Blood Drain (CON 16, 2d6 force + heal) instead of one attack.

**Orion's First Seal** — in the family crypt: a moving pattern of blood and dust rotating on the floor. Interaction requires DC 15 CON save + 2d6 force damage; max HP reduced by damage taken.

**Outcome:** Ritual destroys Harry Mansion — one section of the estate levels, part of a nearby church is also destroyed. A scatter of coins and servant body parts. Hiluino's (Hilua-connected) crushed body regenerates between the rubble. Banker's Sending Stone is on the body.

### Dirden's Farm

Old smuggling base. Built on ruins of a stone necromantic temple; undead avoid the structure (protective wards from the necromancers who didn't want their creations returning to them). Dirden operates here with his numerous family (sons: Alaric, Asger, Calder, Colborn, Dagobar Jr.).

Secret room: 3 bodies covered in blue slime. Small body of Prcek — groaning, begging for mercy. At close range they attack: 3rd-level Inflict Wounds each round + spell slot drain. 80 HP + resistance.

Dirden will cooperate if players are convincing enough. He's just a smuggler in it for profit; allowed Rotgar to use his old hidden cove because it's sheltered by rocks and no one spotted the ships. He's troubled because several of Rotgar's men fought over a crystal (Erathon fragment?) taken from a trade caravan fleeing east from the front; those men were locked in a room containing all his food.

## Act II — Dragon Islands: The Political Struggle

Players travel to the Dragon Islands proper on an imperial ship (Šupina or Leviatan; ~6-week voyage). At sea encounters include a race/game with opponent Dráp (+2 to attack, +3 to acrobatics, advantage on acrobatics).

First Officer Vigo or Agitus — tall, sinewy 40-year-old. Captain is an older stout imperial in ornate clothes.

**Sněhobílý Carleon (Snowwhite)** — receives a fragment containing a shadow lord's soul. Determined to free himself from both Triskal and the United Empire's power. A fragment of ancient power offers a path — but requires acquiring a relic from the indigenous peoples, who want something in return first.

**Banyu** — spiritual/tribal leader. Sent Mego to spy on the dark-eyed who joined Aditya.

**Aditya** — met with Hilua multiple times. Hilua gave her spells, information, and awakened cursed blood in the dark-eyed Taubers. Her faction received weapons from Rotgar's people.

**Mego** — Tauber spy, caught near Dirden's farm. Wants to join the players.

**Urian Ab Nesir** — dark-skinned Vilmaran with glasses, fascinated by elves (never met one). Wealthy optician who wants to open a shop on the Dragon Islands. Fellow passenger.

**Grimír Špinavec (Dirty Grimír)** — owes 7,100–17,100 gold Imperial marks to the bank Brauner & Hess (debt bought from the Grundermwald State Library, originally his grandfather's). Escalating due to interest and fees.

**Aemon Whitecloak (WhiteOak)** — wants to create a strong, respected free democratic republic. One of the key political figures of the Free Republic.

### Indigenous Relic Quest
The indigenous peoples want their desecrated lands cleansed — a son of their spiritual leader gained the power of their ancestors, took their sacred relics, and retreated. The players must reclaim the relics.

## Act III — The Gate and the Endgame

**Erathon** wants to reopen the gate to the Fey world, using Snowwhite as a medium, before his powers are depleted.

**Darkwood** — dark powers are returning or have returned. Connected to the cursed-blood Tauber factions.
