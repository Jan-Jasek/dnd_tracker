# Lady Alynn

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | [[Places/Orestor]] | [[Campaigns/Orestor]]

Head of Orestor's **Merchants' Guild**. She lives in the guild house. Konšel — one of six equals on the closed council. Speaks for the **republicans**.

Husband and sons died in the Third Meredian War.

## Public Identity

A guild mistress with a seat at the table. She champions a merchant-led, plutocratic republic and free trade: wealth and commercial success carry political power. The exact franchise and electoral rules remain open: other ports in the **Red River** delta should form a **coalition of city-republics** — the same move the Dragon Islands made.

**[[People/Konrad|Konrad]]** is master of the docks. Her merchant republicans and the socialist **[[Organisations/Bořiči]]** cooperate against common opponents. Their economic aims will bring them into conflict, but neither camp fully recognizes that divide yet.

## Wants

A league of free delta ports with Orestor as a founding voice. The guild's ships move.
