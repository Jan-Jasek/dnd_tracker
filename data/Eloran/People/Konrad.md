# Konrad

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | [[Places/Orestor]] | [[Organisations/Mlcenlivy|Mlčenliví]] | [[Campaigns/Orestor]]

Konšel. **Master of the docks** (_přístavní mistr_). **[[People/Ilrusk|Rewyn Ilrusk]]'s proxy** in the port.

In the guild he is **Vorlen** — alias and myth. The Silents salute an eternal master. The dock-master under the name is Konrad.

He sits on the closed council as an equal among six. Independent of the three blocs.

## Public Identity

The man who assigns berths, closes the quays, and decides whose cargo lands. The story people tell: a dock-master who kept the harbor working through the siege and bought a council seat with that fact.

## Hidden Identity

He runs the **[[Organisations/Mlcenlivy|Mlčenliví]]** as **Vorlen**. Ilrusk keeps the city library.

What Konrad is, in the flesh: [[Notes/Open Questions#Orestor]].

## Wants

Keep the docks. Keep the compact.
