# Kate

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | [[People/Jeod Isthar]] | [[Campaigns/Hlubina]]

Jeod Isthar's apprentice — currently his only named student in canon. Shadow-magic affinity; not a full Maegi initiate.

## Present Role

Kate was at Ellesmere at **PS 0** — with Jeod when he broke the seal. Mortals smuggled them into the city and the Marble Fortress. She knows the blast was the cost of the working.

She travels in Jeod's name, recruiting Veil-capable people while he pursues the larger restoration of the Veil. She scales with the party and may appear around Hlubina and other imperial staging areas.

At **Hlubina** she recruits the players into imperial service. **Captain Aemon Whiteoak** commands the advance guard; Kate handles recruitment. They are not the same person.

## Appearance & Cost

- ~22 years old
- Black hair streaked with white; unhealthy pale, fragile skin
- Never completed the Maegi admission ritual

Shadow magic costs her far more than it costs Jeod. Her power grew sharply after **Abdelon** (event/name TBD in timeline).

## Relationship to Jeod

Raised from childhood for intelligence and shadow affinity. Acts as his agent on the ground — watching imperial conduct, pulling useful people into his network. Jeod may appear in Hlubina himself during the same recruitment push; Kate is the more visible, recurring face.
