# The Judge

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | [[Places/Orestor]] | [[Organisations/Bořiči]] | [[Campaigns/Orestor]] | See also: [[People/Ilrusk]]

An Orestor magistrate of ordinary, common-born origins. **The Judge** was his nickname for settling disputes and judging people before he obtained judicial office. His full name and detailed career are [[Notes/Open Questions#Orestor|open questions]].

## Public Face

An apparently conventional bureaucrat whom the legal consultate can approach through the courts. His judicial work gives him a place in the city's public life.

The city says he can tell whether someone is guilty simply by looking at them. This is a public reputation; its supernatural basis is not established.

## Convictions

He believes in democracy and considers destructive revolution necessary to remove the entrenched rulers. He leads Bořiči toward **the Great Change**, the movement's working slogan for breaking the existing order and creating a new one. He believes those holding power will not surrender it voluntarily. His precise programme, methods, and limits remain to be developed.

## Hidden Relationships

**[[People/Ilrusk|Rewyn Ilrusk]], the Weaver**, is his Veil teacher and secret patron. The Judge believes their shared purpose is his revolution. Ilrusk cultivates the movement as one of several means to keep control of Orestor.

Ilrusk connects to the Bořiči leadership through the Judge alone. Ordinary members can identify different prominent members as the Weaver, or believe the Judge and Weaver are the same figure. The Judge's knowledge of his teacher's ancient identity and the other leaders' exact knowledge remain open.
