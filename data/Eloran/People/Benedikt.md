# Páter Benedikt

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | [[Places/Orestor]] | [[Organisations/Order of the Just]] | [[Campaigns/Orestor]] | See also: [[Organisations/Orion's Wrath]]

Konšel. Orionist priest of the **old Just temple** in Orestor. He sits beside **[[People/Jeroným|Arcibiskup Jeroným]]** of the **[[Organisations/Apostolic Church of Orion|Apostolic Church]]**.

After the Shattering the Just lost their leaders. Benedikt holds the temple and waits for someone to take the Order. **[[Organisations/Orion's Wrath|Orionův hněv]]** took most of the Just templars in the port. The temple and Hněv hate each other. Benedikt loses ground every day.

## Public Identity

Parish voice of the Just Saint on the Konšelé. Old temple, old manners. He says the Just rites.

## Wants

Wait it out. Someone to take the Order. Keep the temple on the council.
