# Páter Benedikt

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | [[Places/Orestor]] | [[Organisations/Order of the Just]] | [[Campaigns/Orestor]] | See also: [[Organisations/Orion's Wrath]]

Konšel. Orionist priest of the **old Just temple** in Orestor. He sits beside **[[People/Jeroným|Arcibiskup Jeroným]]** of the **[[Organisations/Apostolic Church of Orion|Apostolic Church]]**.

After the Shattering the Just lost their leaders. Benedikt leads the Just in Orestor from the old temple. He believes the Order must change to serve a world in which magic is returning everywhere. **[[Organisations/Orion's Wrath|Orionův hněv]]** and the templars who remain with the Just each account for roughly half the port's templars. The Just continue their traditional duties, including protection against magical corruption; Hněv gains the advantage day by day. The temple and Hněv hate each other. Benedikt loses ground every day.

## Public Identity

Parish voice of the Just Saint on the Konšelé. Old temple, old manners. He says the Just rites.

## Crisis of purpose

The destruction of [[Places/Ellesmere|Ellesmere]] shattered the templars' confidence. Many fear divine punishment for the Order's abuse of power. Survivors suffer a wasting corruption, while magical creatures appear and gifts awaken among ordinary people.

Benedikt faces repeated interventions by men using old methods against an abundance of Weave they cannot meaningfully distinguish. He doubts that these actions fulfil the Order's purpose. A report of sensed Weave alone is insufficient reason to treat every person or shipment as a threat. He can call off an investigation because its grounds are weak, its demands disproportionate, or more urgent needs require his men.

How the templars detect Weave, and what establishes a concrete danger, remain open. The proposed ship intervention and favor are [campaign preparation](../../../ideas/eloran/orestor-opening-job.md).

## Wants

Reform the local Order for the returning magical world. Keep its place on the council and prevent Tillibor's escalating persecution from becoming the city's answer to the crisis.
