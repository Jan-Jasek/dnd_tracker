# Hanser (Jednonohý Hanser)

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | [[Places/Saltmarsh]] | [[Factions/Triple Coalition]] | [[Campaigns/Dragon Independence]]

Dwarf veteran of the corsair republic, known as **Jednonohý Hanser** (One-Legged Hanser). Speaks a thick, mixed dialect peppered with Meredian-German words. He and the Dragon Independence party share a special bond from the war years.

## War Record

- Went through the recruit camp under Bezútěšný vršek with **Jimmy Štístko**, sharing a tent, then served with him aboard the **Vznešená** under Kliďas Tom and later under young Charlie.
- Lost his leg when the Shattering's wave swept the ship at **Bezútěšný vršek**.
- At **Covel**, unable to fight on one leg, he worked a ballista from the watchtower. He saw Jimmy's last stand from there.

Once very bloodthirsty, he has grown calmer since losing the leg. He stays thoroughly pragmatic.

## Present — Saltmarsh

Hanser is a Saltmarsh public figure:

- Puts out a weekly newsletter for the town.
- Stands as a candidate for the town council.
- Makes money selling **Lucky Jimmies** — good-luck charms in memory of Jimmy Štístko.

## Jimmy Štístko

**Jimmy Štístko** (Lucky Jimmy), born **Jimmy Danta**, was a jinxed halfling the party met early on. Things always happened around him, to other people, and he walked away unharmed. When magic returned with the Shattering, he was one of the newly awakened talents. At **Covel**, the battle where first consul Torsten died, Triskal troops were closing in on Hanser's group. Jimmy charged them with a soprano war cry, hurling flame from his bare hands. A misplaced fireball blew him and the whole squad apart and opened the way out. Hanser's people found only smouldering pieces.

His father, a tanner, lives alone and mourns two dead sons. He has asked after his third son, **Billy**, who lives in Červánkov, and offers Jimmy's ring as reward.
