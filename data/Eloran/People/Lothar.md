# Sir Lothar

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | [[Places/Orestor]] | [[Campaigns/Orestor]]

Knight and commander of Orestor's **city guard**. Konšel — one of six equals on the closed council. Speaks for the **loyalists**.

## Public Identity

The prominent loyalist in the room: low nobles, artisans, and townfolk who still hold to the **purkrabí**. The guard is his strength. The compact holds because he can put men on the streets.

## Wants

The purkrabí's city standing. The protectorate signature honoured.

## Connections

- **Konšelé** — sits with [[People/Alynn|Lady Alynn]], [[People/Oto|Lord Oto]], [[People/Konrad|Konrad]], [[People/Benedikt|Páter Benedikt]], and [[People/Jeroným|Arcibiskup Jeroným]]
- **City guard** — his command
- **Purkrabí Jan** — the locked man his people are loyal to
