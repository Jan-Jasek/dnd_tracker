# Rewyn Ilrusk

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | [[Places/Orestor]] | [[Organisations/Order of Maegi]] | [[Campaigns/Orestor]] | See also: [[People/Orion]]

Last of Orion's nine inner disciples. Given name **Rewyn**. Public epithet in legend: **Rudý Ilrusk**. Lives in **[[Places/Orestor|Orestor]]** at PS 2. **[[People/Eirys|Eirys]]** is his proxy in the city.

## Public Face (Orestor)

Solitary. Looks old, wandering, unthreatening. Ruby ring. Conjurer, transmuter, alchemist. He has spent centuries making the body he wears nearly indestructible and resistant to magic.

He has told other Maegi that **Orestor stays out of their wars**, and that he will not suffer their unannounced presence in the port.

Most of the city has no idea what sits in that room. The Konšelé see Eirys. They do not see him.

## Hidden Truth

He anticipated the disciples' betrayal of Orion. Before the sealing ritual he projected his mind into one of his own students' bodies. The original flesh died. He has worn stolen bodies since. See [[People/Orion]].

He co-founded the **[[Organisations/Order of the Just|Order of the Just]]** and gave them **Severite / Mlčoun**. Why he armed the order that hunts Maegi is contested.

**Azolar** is the name he hates. Identity open.

## Wants (this season)

Orestor quiet and his. Eirys holds the compact. Maegi wars stay outside the port. **Lucian Tillibor** and Orionův hněv in the same city are a collision waiting for a name.
