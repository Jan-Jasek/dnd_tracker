# Sněhobílý Carleon — Snowwhite

Full name: Sněhobílý Carleon. Carleon is an Empire location (his origin city or family name).

## Role

Central figure in Act II/III of Dragon Independence. Receives a fragment containing a shadow lord's soul and becomes determined to free himself from both Triskal and the United Empire's control.

## Situation

- Received (or will receive) an Erathon/shadow lord soul fragment
- Wants to use a pradávná relikvie (ancient relic) of the indigenous people to break free of imperial power
- The indigenous people will give access to the relic — but only if their desecrated lands are cleansed first

## Connection to Orestor

Mentioned in the Orestor notes as "Sněhobílý Carleon" alongside the thieves' guild faction names — suggesting he has a prior history in Orestor, possibly as a guild-adjacent figure or young criminal.
