# Selith

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | [[Places/Orestor]] | [[Organisations/Orionovi Zvěstovatelé]] | [[Campaigns/Orestor]] | See also: [[People/Orion]], [[People/Jeroným]]

The marked call Selith **Orion**.

An **ancient being** [[People/Orion|Orion]] made while shaping the Veil on Eloran — an attempt to create more Veil-users to help him secure the world.

By night it walks looking like Orion. It visits people, convinces them, marks them as **Heralds**. It has done this for some time. Slogans and ritual sites exist in the city.

How it looks in its own shape, and what it wants now: [[Notes/Open Questions#Orestor]].

## Campaign

The [[Campaigns/Orestor|campaign draft]] gives the Heralds an independent campaign of rites against perceived oath-breakers, with **[[People/Jeroným|Jeroným]]** as the proposed square victim. Means, targets, and timing are developed in [the Herald-rites preparation](../../../ideas/eloran/orestor-herald-rituals.md). The rites operate without requiring the fragment's arrival or possession. Selith's pursuit of the fragment is a separate thread; [motives and nature](../../../ideas/eloran/orestor-selith-motives.md) remain draft.
