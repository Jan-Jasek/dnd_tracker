# Lord Oto

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | [[Places/Orestor]] | [[Campaigns/Orestor]] | See also: [[Factions/Meredie]]

War-refugee high lord. He fled to **[[Places/Orestor|Orestor]]** with other Meredian lords, his own army of men, and his household. Konšel — one of six equals on the closed council. Speaks for the **monarchists**.

## Public Identity

The loudest high-noble voice in the port. The monarchists mean to crown hostage **Purkrabí Jan** as king of Meredie. Two other contenders hold elsewhere in Meredie; Oto's camp is the one in this city.

## Wants

Jan on a Meredian throne. His lords fed, his armed household kept in the city, his seat on the Konšelé used to block a free-port league and a Triskal protectorate.

## Connections

- **Konšelé** — sits with [[People/Alynn|Lady Alynn]], [[People/Lothar|Sir Lothar]], [[People/Konrad|Konrad]], [[People/Benedikt|Páter Benedikt]], and [[People/Jeroným|Arcibiskup Jeroným]]
- High lords in exile in Orestor — his following
- **Purkrabí Jan** — the throne-piece his camp holds
