# Eloran

> Part of: [[Eloran]]

## The World at a Glance

The playable material plane has **two major landmasses** plus the **Dragon Archipelago** in the **Azure Sea**:

| Landmass                                               | Powers                                                         | Campaign role                   |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| **[[Regions/Northern Continent\|Northern Continent]]** | Empire, Meredie, Triskal, Valdie, Grundarmheld                 | Hlubina, Orestor, Meredian Wars |
| **Southern continent** (_jižní kontinent_)             | Empire vs **Vilmar** across the sea; **Gibesh Desert**, Akbesi | Border lore, necromancer rumors |
| **Dragon Isles**                                       | **Triple Coalition** (since PS 0)                              | Dragon Independence             |

Colonial activity on the archipelago is a phenomenon of the last few decades. Main campaign: **Dragon Isles**.

Magic exists but is rare, feared, and actively suppressed by the Order of the Just (the Just Ones). The average person has heard stories of witches and strange circus performers but has never personally witnessed anything undeniable. Necromancy and demon-summoning are the most condemned acts imaginable.

## The Dragon Archipelago

Subtropical/tropical — Caribbean or Indonesian in feel, culturally Eastern-inspired. Colonial activity is a phenomenon of the last few decades. The dominant colonial power is now the **[[Factions/Triple Coalition]]** of Free Corsair Republics, which broke away during the last war.

Key island hub: **[[Places/Ansir]]**.

### Indigenous Peoples

- **Natives** (name open; _Tauberové_ in old notes) — one human people of many regional groups, carrying the dragon blood of Ilimox. The **light-eyed** (_světloocí_) look somewhat elvish, with a wood-elf, Vistani feel, and form the nobility. The **dark-eyed** (_temnoocí_) look more lizard-like and draconic and are the servant caste. Speak their own Draconic dialect, minimal Common. Avoid colonizers; retreat inland and toward [[Places/Darkenwold|Darkenwold]]. See [[Places/Dragon Islands#The Dark-Eyed Blessing]].
- **Dragonborn** (_drakorození_) — the continental name for the natives, above all the dark-eyed, as slaves. Free dark-eyed communities hold the inhospitable uncolonized coasts and are frequent targets of slavers.
- **Sanguine / Lizardfolk** — "Fishers"; withdrawn to southern swamps and waterways.

## Magic

The world is effectively non-magical from a player perspective at campaign start:

- Players have met circus performers doing unexplained things; heard rumors of forest witches
- The **[[Organisations/Order of the Just|Order of the Just]]** (_Řád Spravedlivých_) hunts and destroys magical practitioners — fire and sword, no trial
- **Teleportation** beyond short range (Misty Step, Dimension Door) does not function in this world
- **Resurrection spells** do not exist — death is permanent
- No other planes the players would know of or can reach
- **Identify** is an arcane check requiring proficiency, not a spell; Detect Magic remains a spell
- Leomond's Tiny Hut does not exist here

Hidden truth: magic is suppressed and feared. The Erathon soul-fragment crisis, the fey gate Erathon is seeking, and the sealed dragon blood of the islands' natives all suggest older forces are waking. After the Shattering some **common folk spark** — untrained Veil-gift. In [[Places/Orestor|Orestor]] that street includes the **[[Organisations/Bořiči]]**.

## Religion

Continental faith centers on **Orion** as Saint and Martyr — worshippers are **Orionists** (_Orionisté_). The Forgotten Realms pantheon exists in the background as lesser celestial powers (Latander and others), but the Saint's cult is supreme on Eloran.

Two institutions, one history — see [[Organisations/Church of Orion]]:

- **[[Organisations/Apostolic Church of Orion|Apostolic Church of Orion]]** (_Apoštolská církev Orionova_) — founded at the betrayal; secretly mage-governed for most of the Age of Salvation
- **[[Organisations/Order of the Just|Order of the Just]]** (_Řád Spravedlivých_) — Ellesmere culling pre-PS ~155 marks the **Just Age** (_Věk spravedlnosti_, pre-PS ~155 → PS 0), a separate era from Salvation. Opposed the mage church, not Orionist devotion.

The Order teaches that the Veil is corrupting; suppression of Veil-wielders is sacred duty. Necromancy and demon-summoning are killed on sight. Tools: **Severite** / **Mlčoun** — gauntlet on white field; Hammer of Orion adds crossed warhammer.

## Races

See [[Notes/Races and Rules]] for full player-facing detail.

- **Humans** — overwhelming majority in civilized lands
- **Dwarves** — mountain kingdom **Grundarmheld**; extremely caste-locked; exile with permanent branding is the common punishment; exiled dwarves and their descendants cannot wear clan insignia
- **Elves** — very rare; withdrew to forest clans; elf ghettos were notorious for misery
- **Gnomes** — rare; well integrated with humans; only race tolerated by dwarves in Grundarmheld
- **Halflings** — well integrated, live normally among humans
- **Dragonborn** (_drakorození_) — not a separate race: the continental name for enslaved Dragon Islands natives, mostly dark-eyed. Freed in northern Eloran ~20 years ago; elsewhere still a valuable slave commodity; northern settlements are raided by southern slavers
- **Orcs** — pushed to mountains and largely exterminated; half-orcs are rare
- **Tieflings** — not playable (demons have no presence here; anyone resembling demonic heritage ends up on a pyre)
- **Underdark races** — not playable

## Legends and Old History

Things everyone knows as myth:

- **Orion** — master of magic; founded his order and the city of Orestor
- **Erathon** — great demon imprisoned by Orion; the Ellesmere explosion scattered fragments across the continent
- **Krvavá válka druidů** — the Bloody War of the Druids
- **Ilimoxi** — the dragon god
- **Vrognir** — King of Giants
- **Fey world** — exists in legend; Erathon seeks to reopen a gate to it

## Current Situation

**Bořivoj I** unified **Meredie** about three centuries before the Shattering. The three **Meredian Wars** (pre-PS ~55, ~45, and **PS 0**) are later Imperial attempts to take that fortress-kingdom. In the Third, Gutberg falls; then **Ellesmere** is destroyed in the Erathon explosion, not by a completed siege.

Present aftermath:

- Meredian **main line** ended at Ellesmere; cadet branches compete; the kingdom has no unified crown
- Valdie fell to Triskal
- Orestor (Meredian crown port) is a **city-republic compact** at PS 1 — six Konšelé as equals, purkrabí locked up; loyalists, monarchists, and republicans; not a popular democracy
- The Triple Coalition broke away during the chaos and controls the Dragon Islands
