# Eloran

> Part of: [[Eloran]]

## The World at a Glance

The world splits between **Eloran** (the main continent, four colonial powers) and the **Dragon Archipelago** around the **Azure Sea** — a subtropical island chain those powers have been colonizing for decades. The campaign is set in the Archipelago.

Magic exists but is rare, feared, and actively suppressed by Tyr's Order (the Just Ones). The average person has heard stories of witches and strange circus performers but has never personally witnessed anything undeniable. Necromancy and demon-summoning are the most condemned acts imaginable.

Common is the trade and administrative language across all of Eloran and the colonies.

## The Dragon Archipelago

Subtropical/tropical — Caribbean or Indonesian in feel, culturally Eastern-inspired. Colonial activity is a phenomenon of the last few decades. The dominant colonial power is now the **[[Factions/Triple Coalition]]** of Free Corsair Republics, which broke away during the last war.

Key island hub: **[[Places/Ansir]]**.

### Indigenous Peoples

- **Tauber tribes** — half wood-elf, half Vistani in feel. Light-eyed (higher social status) and dark-eyed variants. Speak their own Draconic dialect, minimal Common. Avoid colonizers; retreat toward [[Darkwood]].
- **Dragonborn** — inhabit inhospitable uncolonized coastline; frequent targets of southern slavers.
- **Sanguine / Lizardfolk** — "Fishers"; withdrawn to southern swamps and waterways.

## Magic

The world is effectively non-magical from a player perspective at campaign start:

- Players have met circus performers doing unexplained things; heard rumors of forest witches
- **Tyr's Order (the Just Ones)** hunts and destroys magical practitioners — fire and sword, no trial
- **Teleportation** beyond short range (Misty Step, Dimension Door) does not function in this world
- **Resurrection spells** do not exist — death is permanent
- No other planes the players would know of or can reach
- **Identify** is an arcane check requiring proficiency, not a spell; Detect Magic remains a spell
- Leomond's Tiny Hut does not exist here

Hidden truth: magic is not truly gone — it is suppressed and feared. The Erathon soul-fragment crisis, the fey gate Erathon is seeking, and the cursed bloodlines of the Taubers all suggest older forces are waking.

## Religion

The dominant religion of the continent is the **[[Organisations/Church of Orion|Church of Orion]]** — Orion as Saint and Martyr who saved the world from Erathon's corruption. The Forgotten Realms pantheon exists in the background as lesser celestial powers (Tyr, Latander, etc.), but the Saint's cult is supreme on Eloran. Gods are more symbolic here — they do not intervene the way they do in FR.

The Church teaches that the Veil (magic) is a corrupting force that destroys those who wield it; suppression of Veil-wielders is a sacred duty. Necromancy and demon-summoning are universally condemned; practitioners are killed on sight. The militant arm — **the Templars** (also called _the Just Ones_) — hunts Veil-wielders with fire, sword, and **Severite** (Imperial term) / **Mlčoun** (Meredic term) — a Veil-suppressing material.

Symbol of the Templars: a golden plate gauntlet on a white field. Senior templars have stars above the gauntlet marking rank. Members of the **Hammer of Orion** (elite militant faction) have the gauntlet crossed with a warhammer.

The Church is a lie built by Orion's surviving disciples to cover up their betrayal of him. See [[Organisations/Church of Orion]] for the full account.

## Races

See [[Notes/Races and Rules]] for full player-facing detail.

- **Humans** — overwhelming majority in civilized lands
- **Dwarves** — mountain kingdom **Grundarmheld**; extremely caste-locked; exile with permanent branding is the common punishment; exiled dwarves and their descendants cannot wear clan insignia
- **Elves** — very rare; withdrew to forest clans; elf ghettos were notorious for misery
- **Gnomes** — rare; well integrated with humans; only race tolerated by dwarves in Grundarmheld
- **Halflings** — well integrated, live normally among humans
- **Dragonborn** — historically enslaved; freed in northern Eloran ~20 years ago; elsewhere still a valuable slave commodity; northern settlements are raided by southern slavers
- **Orcs** — pushed to mountains and largely exterminated; half-orcs are rare
- **Tieflings** — not playable (demons have no presence here; anyone resembling demonic heritage ends up on a pyre)
- **Underdark races** — not playable

## Legends and Old History

Things everyone knows as myth:

- **Orion** — master of magic; founded his order and the city of Orestor
- **Erathon** — great demon imprisoned in crystal by Orion; his soul was shattered by the Ellesmere explosion and fragments scattered across the continent
- **Krvavá válka druidů** — the Bloody War of the Druids
- **Ilimoxi** — the dragon god
- **Vrognir** — King of Giants
- **Fey world** — exists in legend; Erathon seeks to reopen a gate to it

## Current Situation

The Third Meredian War (roughly 3 years before campaign start) shook the continent:

- Meredie fought the Empire for independence; the capital (name TBD) was destroyed in the Erathon explosion
- Valdie fell to Triskal
- Orestor (successor city in Meredie's territory) is now a fractured city-state with competing criminal and political factions
- The Triple Coalition broke away during the chaos and controls the Dragon Islands
