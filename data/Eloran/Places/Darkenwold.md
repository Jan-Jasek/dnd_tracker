# Darkenwold (Temnoles)

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | [[Places/Dragon Islands]] | See also: [[Campaigns/Dragon Independence]]

A forest on the Dragon Islands where the Tauber tribes retreat from the colonists. Labelled **Darkenwold** on the [[Maps/Dragon Islands Map|archipelago map]]; _Temnoles_ in Czech notes. Stub — details open.

## What Is Known

- Dark powers are said to be returning here, or to have returned already
- Linked to the dark-eyed bloodlines and the faction around **Aditya**
- In legend, after the Orionist crusade: the supposed crypt of an old enemy of the Order of Maegi (see [[People/Orion]], [[Cosmology]])
- Possibly a thin place between Eloran and the fey plane (see [[Fey]])
