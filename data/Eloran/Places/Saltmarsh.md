# Saltmarsh (Saltmarsh)

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | [[Places/Dragon Islands]] | [[Campaigns/Dragon Independence]]

Port town on the Dragon Islands (_Slanobažina_) and capital of [[People/Birger Aderen|Birger Aderen's]] federal state in the [[Factions/Triple Coalition|Triple Coalition]]. Founded by settlers from the **Skeld Isles** and taken over by [[Factions/Valdie|Valdie]] somewhat over thirty years ago. Under the coalition, migration has mixed every nation of the [[Regions/Northern Continent|Northern Continent]] through the town; who founded it matters little today. One of the first harbors to declare independence and join the Free Republic coalition.

## Government

A single elected **council** (_rada_) of six holds executive power over both the town and [[People/Birger Aderen|Birger Aderen's]] federal state. Seats are won by vote, not reserved for groups, but each councillor stands on a declared focus, and the six roughly follow the town's preferences:

| Focus                       | Councillor                                                          |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Trade, wealth, and commerce | **Gellan Primewater** (_Gellan Přímořský_), merchant; mayor         |
| War and defence             | **[[People/Birger Aderen\|Birger Aderen]]** (_Sněhobílý_)           |
| Mining and the smithies     | **Manfred Kupferbart** (_Manfréd Měďovous_), dwarf; Dwarven Company |
| The old families            | **Edwin Kester** (_Edvín Kestr_), of the Skeld founders             |
| The newcomers               | **Ondřej Andr**, former Meredian officer                            |
| Health and quality of life  | **Xendra**, physician — seat empty since she left town              |

- **Gellan Primewater** (_Gellan Přímořský_) is the elected **mayor** (_starosta_) of the town.
- **Sněhobílý** governs the state and sits in the triumvirate.
- Both town and state are meant to have separate executives one day. In these dangerous times the council still serves both, and Gellan is negotiating with Sněhobílý over how the powers would be split.
- **Xendra's** seat stands empty since she left town. [[People/Hanser|Jednonohý Hanser]] is running for it.

### Councillors

- **Manfred Kupferbart** (_Manfréd Měďovous_) — exiled dwarf, branded and barred from clan insignia, who built the **Dwarven Company** (_Trpasličí společnost_): mines and smithies east in the Ganner Mountains toward Kanbar. His ore feeds the town's smithies, whose weapons went to the Republic — among them the arms stolen from Ansir's fortress. He knows the mountain paths and mining villages.
- **Edwin Kester** (_Edvín Kestr_) — a naive young nobleman, descendant of the Skeld founders whom Valdie pushed aside thirty years ago. Speaks for the old families and resents a Valdian bastard ruling his town.
- **Ondřej Andr** — former Meredian officer who speaks for the refugee camps and the newcomers.

## Public Opinion — Xendra's Fall

**Official account:** Xendra was a [[Factions/Vilmar|Vilmaran]] agent behind the smuggling of the weapons stolen in Ansir, and held Von Telten's family hostage. Von Telten testifies to it: the Požehnaný who watched him aboard the _Sea Ghost_ spoke by magic with someone he called Xendra and passed on messages from Telten's wife, and Telten learned at landings that Saltmarsh's councilwoman Xendra had come to town with a woman and two children matching his family. She vanished from town at night with a hooded woman and two children.

**Contested:** Hanser and a part of the town believe Sněhobílý had her removed and framed. In their telling, she was growing too popular, and the council needed someone to blame for the weapons stolen in Ansir.

**Hidden truth:** Xendra is innocent, but Sněhobílý did not frame her. Her assistant, the "refugee widow", is **Marie von Telten**, a Požehnaná. After the incident with the rider's crystal, Marie fed Xendra rumours that accusations were gathering against her and that Sněhobílý meant to use them to eliminate her as opposition — and may have staged something to make it convincing. She proposed escaping to the mountains, where she knew someone who would hide them. On the second night after the incident, Xendra fled with her. A dead Xendra in town would have raised questions; a vanished one confirms her guilt.

## Places

Saltmarsh is still recovering from the wave, but in the six weeks or so the party spent away in Ansir, repairs have moved on.

- **Council House** — the town hall, half torn down by the wave, under repair.
- **Market** — destroyed by the wave and being rebuilt. The **flea market** (_bleší trh_) is gone.
- **Šarkan's Bridge** (_Šarkanův most_) — survived the wave.
- **Crab Cliffs** (_Krabí útesy_) — the poor quarter.
- **Cemetery**
- **Xendra's hospital and hospice** (_Xendros'_ on the map) — her house and surgery joined to a hospice, where she treated refugees, the wounded, and the dying. Its physician and her assistant are both gone; the staff keep it running. Several nurses trained under Xendra are nearly physicians themselves — she taught everyone whatever she could. With them work a grave digger and the orderlies (_zřízenci_) who set bones, hold patients down, cut, pry, amputate, and prepare bodies for dissection. Among the patients lies the unconscious general **Eliander**.
- **The Anchor** (_U Kotvy_; The Empty Net on the map) — [[People/Hanser|Hanser's]] harbour tavern.
- **The Wicker Goat** (_U Proutěné kozy_) — Kreb's inn, rebuilt with its own brewery.
- **The Snapping Line** (_U Přetrženého lana_) — sailors' tavern and lodging house, decorated with planks from fishing boats. **Burned** by the Ashen Brotherhood after the incident, with everyone inside.
- **The church** (Temple of Procan on the map) — of the [[Organisations/Apostolic Church of Orion|Apostolic Church of Orion]].
- **The Grove** (_háj_; Grove of Obad-Hai on the map) — where the old seer lives.
- **Tyrol's smithy and manufactory** — in the building of an executed bandit.
- **Nesir's All-Seeing Shop** (_Nesirovo všezórium_) — optician's shop on the hill above the ruined town hall.
- **Jail** — kept by Kraddok.
- **Hoolwatch Tower** (_Strážní věž_), **Mariner's Guild**, **Carpenter's Guild**, **Fishmonger's**, **Winston's Store** — as on the map.
- **The Ashen Brotherhood's camp** — outside town. The squadron arrived a few weeks after the party left for Ansir and camped in the wild. A few days before the incident they offered Sněhobílý their services and he refused them; the day after the incident he sent for them himself.

Saltmarsh made weapons for the Republic: ore from the Dwarven Company's mines and Tyrol's manufactory made it the Republic's arsenal, and the arms stolen from Ansir's fortress were largely made here.

## People

Besides the council (above):

- **[[People/Hanser|Jednonohý Hanser]]** — dwarf veteran; The Anchor; newsletter; runs for Xendra's seat.
- **Kreb** (_Šenkýř Kreb_) — keeper of The Wicker Goat. The wave flattened his old pub and smashed him against the bridge; he survived with a crushed nose and talks through it. Hanser's rival.
- **Skerrin Chytač** — kept The Snapping Line until it burned. Whether he was inside is open.
- **Tyrol Železňák** — dwarf smith who came to the islands on a ship full of machines to build a great manufactory. Works with his lads (_ogaři_). The workers of the drowned smith **Jóža** went to him.
- **Wellgar Slanoruký** (_Wellgar Brinehand_) — the Apostolic cleric; a drunk.
- **The old seer of the Grove** — known in town as the weirdo by the grove (_pošuk od háje_). An old dark native, one of the few who remember the truth of the blessing of Ilimox.
- **The preacher of the Cleansing Tide** (_Očistná vlna_) — a doomsday preacher of the wave and leader of its cult.
- **Urian Ab Nesir** — Vilmaran optician, fascinated by elves; runs Nesir's All-Seeing Shop: _„Navštivte Nesirovo všezórium, kouzelná nasazovátka pro všechny špatně vidící, poznejte konečně, koho potkáte na ulicích! Již od 2 zlatých!“_
- **The librarian** — awakened as a mage after the wave. Name open.
- **Mára** — the hospice grave digger, a man. Handles the dead every day and fears nothing.
- **Kraddok** (_Bachař Kraddok_) — the jailer. Holds a looter in whose ruined house a pile of stolen goods was found.
- **Kiorna** (_Koželuh Kiorna_) — the tanner.
- **Jíra** (_Tesař Jíra_) — the carpenter; survived the wave, his workshop did not.
- **Eda** — owned several fishing boats and with them all the town's fishing. The wave destroyed her house, and secret passages were found in its ruins. Whether she is alive is open.
- **Eliander** — a former general, swept away by the wave in his own house and unconscious ever since. Lies in Xendra's hospice.
- **Torvald Hallsson** — the guard captain; a former gladiator and soldier who leads the town watch. Honest and stubborn, loyal to Sněhobílý since the war; he takes the warrant as an order, not as politics, and dislikes bounty hunters for the mess they make in his town.

## The Incident

1. **Night.** Three men — Marie's hirelings — mugged a well-liked young newcomer, **Matyáš**, in the street. He surprisingly fought all three off. They seemed suddenly sick and wandered off into the dark, and ended the night drinking together at **The Snapping Line**. Matyáš fled south toward [[Places/Oros|Oros]] that same night.
2. **Morning.** Several people at the tavern showed symptoms — flesh darkening and dying, like necrosis. **Xendra** was called and was mortified.
3. **That day.** Sněhobílý called in the Ashen Brotherhood camped nearby, whom he had turned away a few days before. The Brotherhood closed off the tavern and burned it with everyone inside.
4. **Second night.** Xendra fled town, with Marie and the children.

The town knows Matyáš was mugged and disappeared the same night. What he did in his first weeks in Saltmarsh is open.

## Court

[[People/Jeod Isthar|Jeod Isthar]] came to Saltmarsh a few days after the incident with the rider's crystal, on the trail of the archdruid Finnigan. He offered Sněhobílý his services in this era of erupting magic and became the triumvir's advisor, eager to shape the coalition his way. Nobody in town knows he is a mage. He found the Ashen Brotherhood, the men who hunted Maegi for centuries, already serving the man he advises.

## Role in Act I–II

- **Marie von Telten**, a Požehnaná, lived here under a false name with her children while a Požehnaný aboard the _Sea Ghost_ told her husband, **Captain Henry Von Telten**, that his family was held hostage by someone called Xendra. She has fled the town.
- The party set out from Saltmarsh at the start of Dragon Independence and met the lone rider on the road into town.
- Coalition survivors regrouped here after the wave at Bezútěšný vršek (Bleak Hill)

## Geography

Sheltered by nearby mountains and a dwarven community, whose mines and smithies make it the Republic's arsenal.
