# Saltmarsh (Saltmarsh)

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | [[Places/Dragon Islands]] | [[Campaigns/Dragon Independence]]

Port town on the Dragon Islands (_Slanobažina_ in Czech sources). One of the first harbors to declare independence and join the Free Republic coalition.

**Town map:** [[Maps/Saltmarsh Map]]

![[saltmarsh-town-map.jpg]]

**On the archipelago map:** port label **Saltmarsh** on the [[Maps/Dragon Islands Map]]

## Role in Act I–II

- **Xendra**, a town councilwoman and [[Factions/Vilmar|Vilmaran]] agent, held **Captain Henry Von Telten's** family hostage here, coercing the former Imperial admiral into the weapon-smuggling chain. Through her council seat, distributed weapons and supplies were stolen from the town's caches. She has fled the town.
- The party set out from Saltmarsh at the start of Dragon Independence and met the lone rider on the road into town.
- Coalition survivors regrouped here after the wave at Bezútěšný vršek (Bleak Hill)

## Geography

Sheltered by nearby mountains and a dwarven community — enough stored weapons that Stoupač's men could steal from caches without immediate shortage.

## Playable Hooks

- Investigate missing arms bound for the front (Ausiv supply line)
- Pressure on council finances and imperial banking connections
- **[[People/Hanser|Jednonohý Hanser]]** — war veteran, weekly newsletter, council candidate, seller of Lucky Jimmies
- Mego (Tauber spy) rescued and brought here after the convoy ambush
