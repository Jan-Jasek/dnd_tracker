# Saltmarsh (Saltmarsh)

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | [[Places/Dragon Islands]] | [[Campaigns/Dragon Independence]]

Port town on the Dragon Islands (_Slanobažina_) and capital of [[People/Birger Aderen|Birger Aderen's]] federal state in the [[Factions/Triple Coalition|Triple Coalition]]. Founded by settlers from the **Skeld Isles** and taken over by [[Factions/Valdie|Valdie]] somewhat over thirty years ago. Under the coalition, migration has mixed every nation of the [[Regions/Northern Continent|Northern Continent]] through the town; who founded it matters little today. One of the first harbors to declare independence and join the Free Republic coalition.

## Government

A single elected **council** (_rada_) of six holds executive power over both the town and [[People/Birger Aderen|Birger Aderen's]] federal state. Seats are won by vote, not reserved for groups, but each councillor stands on a declared focus, and the six roughly follow the town's preferences:

| Focus                       | Councillor                                                          |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Trade, wealth, and commerce | **Gellan Primewater** (_Gellan Přímořský_), merchant; mayor         |
| War and defence             | **[[People/Birger Aderen\|Birger Aderen]]** (_Sněhobílý_)           |
| Mining and the smithies     | **Manfred Kupferbart** (_Manfréd Měďovous_), dwarf; Dwarven Company |
| The old families            | **Edwin Kester** (_Edvín Kestr_), of the Skeld founders             |
| The newcomers               | **Ondřej Andr**, former Meredian officer                            |
| Health and quality of life  | **Xendra**, physician — seat empty since she left town              |

- **Gellan Primewater** (_Gellan Přímořský_) is the elected **mayor** (_starosta_) of the town.
- **Sněhobílý** governs the state and sits in the triumvirate.
- Both town and state are meant to have separate executives one day. In these dangerous times the council still serves both, and Gellan is negotiating with Sněhobílý over how the powers would be split.
- **Xendra's** seat stands empty since she left town. [[People/Hanser|Jednonohý Hanser]] is running for it.

### Councillors

- **Manfred Kupferbart** (_Manfréd Měďovous_) — exiled dwarf, branded and barred from clan insignia, who built the **Dwarven Company** (_Trpasličí společnost_): mines and smithies east in the Ganner Mountains toward Kanbar. His smithies supplied the weapons that drained out of the caches. He knows the mountain paths and mining villages.
- **Edwin Kester** (_Edvín Kestr_) — descendant of the Skeld founders whom Valdie pushed aside thirty years ago. Speaks for the old families and resents a Valdian bastard ruling his town.
- **Ondřej Andr** — former Meredian officer who speaks for the refugee camps and the newcomers.

## Public Opinion — Xendra's Fall

**Official account:** Xendra was a [[Factions/Vilmar|Vilmaran]] agent who drained the weapon caches and held Von Telten's family hostage. Letters signed "X" prove it, and she was seen leaving town with a cloaked woman and two children in her cart.

**Contested:** Hanser and a part of the town believe Sněhobílý had her removed and framed. In their telling, she was growing too popular, and the council needed someone to blame for the lost weapons.

**Hidden truth:** Xendra is innocent, but Sněhobílý did not frame her. Her assistant, the "refugee widow", is **Marie von Telten**, a Požehnaná. After the incident with the rider's crystal, Xendra treated the victims and came close to the truth. Marie told her that the letters named her and that Sněhobílý's men were coming that night, and proposed escaping to the mountains, where she knew someone who would hide them. Xendra fled with her. A dead Xendra in town would have raised questions; a vanished one confirms her guilt.

## Role in Act I–II

- **Marie von Telten**, a Požehnaná, lived here under a false name with her children while letters signed "X" told her husband, **Captain Henry Von Telten**, that his family was held hostage. Through her council seat, distributed weapons and supplies were stolen from the town's caches. She has fled the town.
- The party set out from Saltmarsh at the start of Dragon Independence and met the lone rider on the road into town.
- Coalition survivors regrouped here after the wave at Bezútěšný vršek (Bleak Hill)

## Geography

Sheltered by nearby mountains and a dwarven community — enough stored weapons that Stoupač's men could steal from caches without immediate shortage.
