# Ansir

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | [[Places/Regions|Dragon Archipelago]]

The main colonial city of the Dragon Archipelago and the seat of the Free Corsair Republic (Triple Coalition). Also referred to as Saltwater in some sources.

**Map:** `imports/onenote/images/dra-nezavislost-one-on-26-04-2026-img004.png`

## Political Status

Capital of the Triple Coalition of Free Corsair Republics. Broke away from Empire/Triskal control during the Third Meredian War. [[People/Carleon Snowhite|Carleon Sněhobílý]] is the key political figure visible in Ansir — preaching against war and great powers before taking a harder line in Act II.

External threats:

- **Triskal** — wants the islands under its control or influence
- **Vilmar** — Hilua's plan was specifically to disrupt the Triskal truce so Vilmar could take Ansir
- **United Empire** — still has interests via the banking house Brauner & Hess and merchant connections

## Key Locations

- **The Local (Lokál)** — main tavern/gathering place for soldiers and corsairs; where the weapon theft plot begins
- **The Fortress** — military garrison; oiled leather-wrapped weapons were lowered from its walls to the river during the theft
- **Harry Mansion** — site of the Act I climax; partially destroyed by Orion's First Seal detonation
- **Orion's Temple (Orionův chrám)** — reorganized by soldiers after players' visit; connected to Harry's ritual
- **Temple of Umberlee** — on a small square; rune inscription: "Křič s námi" ("Scream with us")
- **The Brothel** — used as distraction during the weapon theft
- **Brauner & Hess banking house** — newly constructed; Imperial bank operating in the city

## Sword of Screaming (found in Ansir)

+2 sword, +1d8 psychic damage, once per day: Hunger of Hadar.

- Each round it fails to hit an organic creature: 1d8 damage to the bearer.
- If bearer is killed this way: dies instantly, soul absorbed into the sword.
- Other weapons lose their attribute bonuses while the bearer carries it.
