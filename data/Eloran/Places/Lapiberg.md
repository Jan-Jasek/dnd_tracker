# Lapiberg

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | [[Factions/Meredie]] | [[Places/Regions|Eloran Continent]]

Dwarven **ground city** in western Meredie — one of three surface holdings of **[[Factions/Grundarmheld|Grundarmheld]]** that sit in a circle around the mountain kingdom (the others: unnamed cities in Valdie and in the west Valdie vassal). Formally Meredic; the halls lie beyond the **Shadow Pass** (_průsmyk Stínů_).

## History

- **Bořivoj I** (pre-PS ~300) grants Lapiberg **free-city status** at unification — still part of the Meredian Kingdom, self-governing. **Lapibergské doly a hutě** arm that unification
- Strong friendship with Meredie until **the Shattering** (PS 0) destroyed Ellesmere and the main royal line

## Campaign Window

The **Lapiberg campaign** took place in winter, approximately **PS 0 M11–M12**, immediately after the Shattering.

## Present (PS 1)

- **Neutral** — refuses Triskal and Meredian successor politics alike; proverbial answer to diplomats: _„Sral pes vašu politiku, Lapiberg zůstává neutrální.“_
- An elite Triskal host of the [[Organisations/Order of the Just|Order of the Just]] (~500 paladins) moved to besiege Lapiberg after PS 0, facing orc incursions from the **Shadow Pass** (_průsmyk Stínů_) on the western lowlands
- Champion duel (import): **Eolian Šílený** (Order of the Just) vs **Skagnok** (dwarven champion, rune-forged gear); king **Norwik** at time of challenge

## Religion

The [[Organisations/Order of the Just|Order of the Just]] maintains a presence — **Andujar**, Dragonborn cleric, described as a ruthless fanatic and the order's current representative in Lapiberg (import).
