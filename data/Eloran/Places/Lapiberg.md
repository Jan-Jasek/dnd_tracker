# Lapiberg

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | [[Factions/Meredie]] | [[Places/Regions|Eloran Continent]]

Dwarven mountain city in western Meredian territory. **Old import name _Zarvilar_ is retracted** — same settlement, current canon name **Lapiberg**.

## History

- ~200 years old at campaign present — trpaslíci se semkli v horské komunitě
- **First Meredian War** (pre-PS ~55): **Lapibergské doly a hutě** played a major role in Meredie's liberation from the Empire
- **Behemot I the Liberator** granted Lapiberg **free-city status** — formally still part of the Meredian Kingdom, but self-governing
- Strong friendship with Meredie until **the Shattering** (PS 0) destroyed Ellesmere and the royal line

## Campaign Window

The **Lapiberg campaign** took place in winter, approximately **PS 0 M11–M12**, immediately after the Shattering.

## Present (PS 1)

- **Neutral** — refuses Triskal and Meredian successor politics alike; proverbial answer to diplomats: _„Sral pes vašu politiku, Lapiberg zůstává neutrální.“_
- **Tyrovo kladivo** (_Tyr's Hammer_) — Triskal's elite paladin host (~500) — moved to besiege Lapiberg after PS 0, facing orc incursions from the **Shadow Pass** (_průsmyk Stínů_) on the western lowlands
- Champion duel (import): **Eolian Šílený** (Tyův řád) vs **Skagnok** (dwarven champion, rune-forged gear); king **Norwik** at time of challenge

## Religion

**Tyův řád** maintains a presence — **Andujar**, Dragonborn cleric, described as ruthless fanatic and current Tyr's representative in Lapiberg (import).
