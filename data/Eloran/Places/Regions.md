# Regions

> Part of: [[Eloran]]

## Eloran Continent

### Spojené císařství — United Empire
Rome/Byzantine inspiration. Home of Common, the dominant language. Expansionist. Fought Meredie to keep it as a territory. Allied with Triskal. Still active power; slavers in the south still deal in Dragonborn.

Symbol: Red eagle on yellow field.

### Triskal
Dutch inspiration. Language: Triskal. Allied with the Empire in the Meredian war; conquered Valdie. Has interests in the Dragon Islands. One of the pressures that could absorb the Free Republic.

Symbol: Red-white field with black wolf.

### Meredie
Czech/Prussian inspiration. Language: Meredic. Former Empire holding that fought for independence. Won the war militarily but lost the capital Ellesmere in the Erathon explosion. Political successor is the fractured city-state of Orestor. Eirys Dustenhorn rose to power in the chaos.

Symbol: White apple tree on green field.

### Valdie
British inspiration. Language: Veildic. Fell to Triskal during the last war. Location of Nialmar (mentioned in notes).

### Vilmar
Sultanate. Language: Vilmaran. Southern power. Hilua — a Vilmaran agent — was working to weaken the Free Republic and disrupt its truce with Triskal so Vilmar could take Ansir. Active antagonist power in the Dragon Islands arc.

References: Vilmarský sultanát, Nebeský Vilmarion (Heavenly Vilmarion), necromancers from Vilmar reportedly have power over life and death (rumored in Gibeška desert).

### Grundarmheld
Dwarf mountain kingdom. Extremely caste-locked. Exiled dwarves and their descendants are permanently branded and barred from clan insignia. Gnomes are the only non-dwarf race tolerated inside.

## Dragon Archipelago

### Azurové moře — Azure Sea
The sea surrounding the Dragon Islands. The Corsair Republic controls its trade lanes.

### Ansir
Main colonial city of the Dragon Islands. Seat of the Free Corsair Republic. See [[Ansir]].

### Saltmarsh (Saltwater)
Port town. Xendra operates here; was holding Captain Von Telten's family hostage. Custom map available: `imports/onenote/images/dra-nezavislost-one-on-26-04-2026-img005.jpg`.

### Mokrá Lhota
Village of significance in the weapon-smuggling plot. Weapons stolen from Ansir were moved here, then east toward the front.

### Dřevolhota
Village where early Act I events occurred. Prcek and Třesavec arrived here with Arryn's men.

### Darkwood
Forest/region where Tauber tribes retreat. Dark powers described as returning from here. Connected to Aditya and the cursed dark-eyed bloodlines.

### Dirden's Farm (Dirdenův statek)
Smuggler's hideout; built on ruins of an old necromantic stone temple. Dagobar-style farm with multiple buildings, palisade, fields. Rotgar used it as a smuggling base. A ghost (Ariadne) appears on the 3rd/4th night after arrival.
