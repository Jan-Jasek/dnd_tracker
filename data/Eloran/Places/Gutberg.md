# Gutberg

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | [[Factions/Meredie]] | [[Places/Regions]]

Meredian mountain fortress on the **Red River** (_Rudá řeka_). The **south gate** of Meredie. Fell to the Empire in the [[History/Timeline#The Third Meredian War|Third Meredian War]] (PS 0) — the shock of that war. The bridge did not fall with it.

## The path

The river here is canyon and fierce. There is one army road into Meredie from the south: the fortress, and the **single-arc metal bridge** it guards. The span is old-age work, fused into the mountain stone. No one has ever destroyed it.

Apart from that path, the south is closed.
