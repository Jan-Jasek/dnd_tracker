# Hlubina

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | [[Places/Ellesmere Region]] | [[Campaigns/Hlubina]]

Marshland village on the right bank of the Klikatice (Klikatice), ~40 km southeast of ruined Ellesmere. The prologue campaign opens here.

## Layout

The original village center sits on raised ground surrounded by a moat tied to the river channel — dry crossing only by bridge. A weathered palisade rings the core; beyond it spread the **Hlubinná blata** (Hlubina marshes): muddy paths, reed huts, and refugee shanties.

## Present Situation

Post–Third Meredian War. Imperial garrison decimated at Orestor; local captain bought off by crime boss **Skrček**. Refugee influx strains food and order. Crime boss controls extortion; **Hector's Liberators** oppose him from eastern marshes.

## Key Sites

- **Spící panoš / Mrtvý Písař** — inn run by Norton půlruký
- **Dagobar's farm** — remote marsh island; built on ruins of a necromantic stone temple (undead avoid it)
- **Ruined Orion temple** — east of town; magical creature encounter
- **Haunted cave near Dagobar's** — Ariadne spectra; locket tie-in

## Playable Hooks

- Skrček's night raid using **Ilis** (gnome sorcerer) to burn the inn
- Handoff: **Aemon Bělodub (Kate)** arrives with imperial advance guard and recruits players toward Dragon Independence
