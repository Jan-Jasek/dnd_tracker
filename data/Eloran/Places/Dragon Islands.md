# Dragon Islands (Dračí souostroví)

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | [[Places/Regions]] | [[Factions/Triple Coalition]]

The Dragon Archipelago in the **Azure Sea** (Azurové moře) — subtropical island chain, colonial frontier of the last few decades. Primary setting for [[Campaigns/Dragon Independence]].

## Political Situation

Home of the **Triple Coalition** (Trojkoalice) of Free Corsair Republics. Broke from Empire/Triskal control during the Third Meredian War. External pressure from Triskal, Vilmar (via Hilua's plot), and lingering Imperial commercial interests (Brauner & Hess).

## Key Ports & Settlements

- [[Places/Ansir]] — coalition capital
- [[Places/Saltmarsh]] — port town; Von Telten hostage plot
- [[Places/Mokrá Lhota]] — inland village on the weapon-smuggling route
- **Dřevolhota** — village tied to early Act I events (Prcek, Třesavec)

## Peoples

- **Tauber tribes** — wood-elf/Vistani feel; light-eyed vs dark-eyed castes; retreat toward Darkwood
- Colonial corsairs, merchants, and imperial holdouts

## Terrain & Threats

Mountainous interior on some islands; smuggling coves; Darkwood's returning powers; weapon caches left over from the war
