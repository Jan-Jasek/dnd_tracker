# Dragon Islands (Dračí ostrovy)

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | [[Places/Regions]] | [[Factions/Triple Coalition]]

An archipelago in the **Azure Sea** (_Azurové moře_) — a subtropical chain of islands and the colonial frontier of recent decades. Main setting of [[Campaigns/Dragon Independence]].

![[dragon-islands-archipelago.png]]

Regional map: [[Maps/Dragon Islands Map]]. The image keeps its original English labels; canon may use other names (e.g. **Ansir** for the map's Anstr).

## Political Situation

Seat of the **[[Factions/Triple Coalition|Triple Coalition]]** of Free Corsair Republics. The three founding ports — **Ansir**, **Saltmarsh**, and **Oros** — broke away from the Empire and Triskal during the [[History/Timeline#The Third Meredian War|Third Meredian War]]. Outside pressure: Triskal, [[Factions/Vilmar|Vilmar]], and Imperial commercial interests ([[Organisations/Brauer & Hess]]).

## Founding Ports and Settlements

- [[Places/Ansir]] — coalition capital
- [[Places/Saltmarsh]] — founding port; Xendra, Von Telten hostage plot
- [[Places/Oros]] — founding port
- [[Places/Mokrá Lhota]] — inland village on the smuggling route
- [[Places/Darkenwold]] — forest where the Taubers retreat

## Peoples

- **Taubers** — half-elves; light-eyed and dark-eyed, the light-eyed ranking higher; retreating into Darkenwold
- **Dragonborn** — on the inhospitable, uncolonized coasts beyond the islands; frequent targets of slave raids
- **Lizardfolk and other "fishfolk"** — withdrawn into the swamps and river mouths of the south
- Colonial corsairs, merchants, and Imperial remnants

## Terrain and Threats

Mountainous interior, smugglers' coves, dark powers returning from Darkenwold, wartime weapon caches.
