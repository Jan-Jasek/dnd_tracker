# Mokrá Lhota (Mokrá Lhota)

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | [[Places/Dragon Islands]] | [[Campaigns/Dragon Independence]]

Inland village on the Dragon Archipelago — a waypoint on the weapon-smuggling route from [[Places/Ansir|Ansir]] toward the eastern front.

## Role in Act I

After the Ansir fortress theft, oiled-leather-wrapped weapons traveled by barge and wagon to Mokrá Lhota overnight. **Tormund** commands the local soldiers (operating under an alias); his unit was drunk when players' side linked up. A wagon waited outside the town under a companion's watch before reporting for active duty.

**Dalibor** — hobbit thief and Rotgar's fast courier between Mokrá Lhota and Ansir.

## Connections

- **Rotgar** receives weapons and moves them east toward the front
- **Tvígo** (Friedrich's beggar) tells players weapons went toward Mokrá Lhota after the Ansir theft
- Dirden's farm smuggling base lies further along the coastal route
