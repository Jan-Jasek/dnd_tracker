# Ellesmere (Ellesmere)

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | [[Places/Ellesmere Region]] | See also: [[Cosmology]], [[History/Timeline]]

Former capital of the **Meredian Kingdom** since **Bořivoj I** unified the country (pre-PS ~300) — the **City of Mages** (Město mágů), older than that crown. Destroyed at **PS 0** — **the Shattering** (_Roztříštění_ / _Rozštěpení_) — when [[People/Jeod Isthar|Jeod Isthar]] broke the seal that held the rift.

## Public History

The Third Meredian War ended here. Imperial siege under Marshal Von Edgeira was about to begin when a thunderous blast tore the city apart — epicenter at the **Marble Fortress** (Mramorová pevnost), headquarters of the Templars of the Just (Spravedliví). Tens of thousands died; all **male direct descendants** of the Meredian house (name TBD) are dead. Female-line and cadet branches remain. The purkrabí of [[Places/Orestor|Orestor]] is **Jan** — distant blood through his mother, a marshal's name.

## Hidden Truth

The Marble Fortress was a cage around a **floating rift** — Path-wound, immovable, secret even from later Maegi. Jeod broke the **seal** that held it. He meant to restore the Veil and to blow the templar fortress. He did not know the rift would collapse. He did not expect the size of the blast. The broken seal crystallized into fragments of Orion and Erathon.

## Survivors & Aftermath

- Maegi prisoners held since the pre-PS ~155 culling escaped or died in the collapse; many insane
- Refugees spread south — including families that reached Hlubina and Dagobar's farm
- Templar **Sir Hans** hunts escaped mages as a high-level quest hook
