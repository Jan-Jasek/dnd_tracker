# Ellesmere Region (Ellesmerská kotlin)

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | [[Places/Regions|Meredie]]

The lowland heartland of former Meredie — once the most fertile territory on the continent. Centered on the ruined capital [[Places/Ellesmere|Ellesmere]] and the marsh approaches around [[Places/Hlubina|Hlubina]].

## Geography

- **Klikatice** river and **Hlubinná blata** (marsh lakes north of Hlubina)
- Imperial army disaster: General Von Nedur drowned fleeing through the marshes after a crushing defeat near Hlubina
- Von Edgeira's siege camp faced Ellesmere from the south before the explosion ended the war

## After the Blast

Refugee streams south and east. Templar hunts for surviving Maegi. Political vacuum that fed Orestor's fracture and Skrček's rise in the marshes.
