# Orestor

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | [[Places/Regions|Eloran Continent — Meredie]] | [[Places/Ellesmere Region]]

Ancient city reputedly founded by Orion (Prastaré město). A **delta city** on the **west** mouth of the **Red River** (_Rudá řeka_) — Venice-like, harbors reaching the open sea. **Triskal** holds the **east** of the same delta. One of the continent's greatest ports. At **PS 2** a **city-republic**: a survival compact among three blocs.

See [[Places/Regions#The Red River]] for the river from **Hor stínů** to this mouth.

**pre-PS ~500+:** Orestor, the Triskal east-delta city, and the Imperial island-fort at the end of the marches were a **river-trade coalition**. The city remembers.

## Before the Third War

- Meredian **crown port** while **[[Places/Ellesmere|Ellesmere]]** was capital
- Orion's city; Meredic administration via a **Lord Warden of the Crown Port** (_purkrabí korunního přístavu_) — **Lord of Orestor**
- The warden is **Jan** — distant **female-line** blood. His great-grandfather would have been king; that man's daughter (Jan's mother) was married off to a marshal. Jan takes the lord's name. Male direct descendants of the house are dead. The marshal (Jan's father) died in the previous war.

## PS 0 — War, Siege, and Coup

1. **Third Meredian War** — **[[Places/Ellesmere|Ellesmere]]** destroyed in **the Shattering**
2. Imperial **eastern column** besieges Orestor
3. **[[Factions/Triskal|Triskal]]** massacres the eastern column (same pattern as Covel)
4. The **purkrabí** negotiates Triskal's **protectorate** over Meredie. Triskal leaves the port standing
5. Word of the deal reaches the city after the Imperial army is gone
6. **Monarchists** depose the purkrabí — they refuse Triskal occupation after Triskal attacked **[[Factions/Valdie|Valdie]]**
7. Purkrabí and family taken **hostage**
8. **Republicans** (Merchants' Guild) push councils and a **free-port league** on the delta
9. **Loyalists** (low nobles, artisans, townfolk, city guard) back the protectorate deal
10. **[[Organisations/Mlcenlivy|Mlčenliví]]**, **[[Organisations/Bořiči]]**, **[[Organisations/Orionovi Zvěstovatelé]]**, **[[Organisations/Orion's Wrath|Orionův hněv]]** broker a compact

**PS 1–PS 2:** Orestor is a de facto independent city-state. Protektorát and crown both claim it.

## The Three Blocs

| Bloc            | Goal                                                                                 | Strength                                               | Face                              |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------- |
| **Loyalists**   | Honour the protectorate deal; keep the purkrabí's city standing                      | Low nobles, artisans, townfolk; the **city guard**     | **[[People/Lothar\|Sir Lothar]]** |
| **Monarchists** | Crown **Jan** as king of Meredie                                                     | High lords in the port; **Lord Oto**'s armed household | **[[People/Oto\|Lord Oto]]**      |
| **Republicans** | A **coalition of merchant-led city-republics** on the delta — free ports, free trade | Merchants' guild; contracts; ships                     | **[[People/Alynn\|Lady Alynn]]**  |

**Konšelé** (closed council) sit as equals. The **purkrabí** would preside; **Jan** is a monarchist hostage. **Veřejná rada** (open council): guild and district seats; templars hold votes. After Ellesmere the city treats the blast as the **gods' revenge**.

Three **power factions** sit beside the blocs: **[[Organisations/Mlcenlivy|Mlčenliví]]**, **[[Organisations/Orion's Wrath|Orionův hněv]]**, **[[Organisations/Orionovi Zvěstovatelé]]**.

## Districts

- **Orionova čtvrť** — jewelers, mages, Order of Maegi connections; Tarkelby gnome family
- **Přístavní čtvrť** — smugglers, catacombs; Fenris Tichošlápek
- **Orion's Library** — [[People/Ilrusk|Ilrusk]] works here as librarian; its collection is being developed in [the library brief](../../../ideas/eloran/orestor-orion-library.md)
- A **town hospital** — Ilrusk volunteers with its physicians
- **Nebeská čtvrť** — nobles; guild representatives; hostage household
- **Nové město**
- Sub-areas: Tři řeky, Soutěska, Bořiči, Pašeráci
