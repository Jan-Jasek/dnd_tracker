# Orestor

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | [[Places/Regions|Eloran Continent — Meredie]] | [[Places/Ellesmere Region]]

Ancient city reputedly founded by Orion himself (Prastaré město — the Ancient City). Largest port on the **Red River** (_Rudá řeka_). At **PS 1** a fractured **city-republic** — not a ideological democracy, but a survival compact between three failed power grabs.

## Before the Third War

- **Meredian**, not Imperial — the kingdom's **crown port** while **[[Places/Ellesmere|Ellesmere]]** was capital
- Older than the crown: Orion's city; Meredic administration layered on top via a **Lord Warden of the Crown Port** (_purkrabí korunního přístavu_) — commonly called **Lord of Orestor**
- The warden was cadet-branch kin to the ruling house (royal house name **TBD**; **Behemot I the Liberator** was a personal title of the founder-king, not the dynasty name)

## PS 0 — War, Siege, and Coup

Sequence agreed for canon:

1. **Third Meredian War** — lowlands fought over; **[[Places/Ellesmere|Ellesmere]]** destroyed in **the Shattering**
2. Imperial **eastern column** besieges Orestor (cut the Red River, take the port after the capital fell)
3. **[[Factions/Triskal|Triskal]]** approaches the Imperial siege camp as an ally — **massacres the eastern column** (same betrayal pattern as at Covel)
4. The **purkrabí** negotiates Triskal's **protectorate** over Meredie. Part of the deal: Triskal **clears new holdings of Imperial influence** — so Triskal does **not** assault Orestor after the massacre; the port is left standing under negotiated terms
5. Word of the protectorate deal reaches the city **after** the Imperial army is gone
6. **Monarchist** faction deposes the purkrabí — refuses Triskal occupation; Triskal had attacked **[[Factions/Valdie|Valdie]]** and cannot be accepted as liberator
7. Purkrabí and family taken **hostage**, not executed — highest surviving royal kin in the region after Ellesmere
8. **Republican** faction (guilds, harbor interests, democratic rhetoric) **exploits** the monarchist coup — pushes councils and elections as propaganda, not as a spontaneous popular revolution
9. **Loyalist** faction (veterans, garrison, officers) holds the **largest military force** — backs the purkrabí's protectorate deal as the only order left
10. None of the three can hold the whole city alone; war refugees and existing powers (**[[Organisations/Mlcenlivy|Mlčenliví]]**, **Bořiči**, **Zvěstovatelé**, **Spravedliví**) broker a **compromise** so the port does not burn

**PS 1:** Cold truce in the **Ellesmere lowlands** (north Triskal / south Empire / centre contested). Orestor is a **de facto independent city-state** on paper; protektorát and crown both claim it, neither controls it fully.

## The Three Power Blocs (PS 1)

| Bloc            | Goal                                                                       | Strength                                                  | Weakness                                                            |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Loyalists**   | Honour the protectorate deal; restore order under Triskal-backed stability | Largest **military** force                                | No street legitimacy after the coup; seen as Triskal's sword        |
| **Monarchists** | Meredian **sovereignty** — no Triskal protectorate                         | **Hostages** (purkrabí + family = royal hope of the port) | Cannot take the harbor by force without civil war                   |
| **Republicans** | Port belongs to the port; **democracy as rhetoric**                        | **Common folk**, refugees, dock workers                   | No army, no crown — exploited monarchist chaos to seat guild voices |

The resulting **city-republic** is a **tripartite ceasefire**, not a sovereign people's state. **Konšelé** (closed council) arbitrate; **Veřejná rada** (open council) is the republican façade.

## Mediators (not rulers)

Brokers who forced the compact when all three blocs would otherwise destroy the harbor:

- **[[People/Eirys|Eirys]]** — treasurer; **[[Organisations/Mlcenlivy|Mlčenliví]]**; money, informants, leverage on violence
- **Euron Abb'Shiran** — **Bořiči** / merchant guild; republican at the table, needs trade peace
- **Orionovi Zvěstovatelé** — ideological cover (_Orion founded a free city, not a throne_)
- **Harbor guilds** — can close the quays and starve every faction

## Districts

- **Přístavní čtvrť** (Harbor District) — smugglers, catacombs; Fenris Tichošlápek
- **Nebeská čtvrť** (Heavenly Quarter) — upper-class, guild representatives; hostage household likely secured here
- **Nové město** (New Town)

## The Erathon Fragment Plot

Orion imprisoned Erathon in crystal; **the Shattering** scattered fragments. Contact corrupts handlers.

**In Orestor:** amethyst fragment via refugee child → **Stefan Iskelny** (regent who murdered lord **Harry Broad** at a lowland fortress — separate from the port coup) → knight **Milbern** → jeweler **Roondar Tarkelby** → Eirys. Milbern hides in harbor catacombs. **Šprýmař** knows the tunnels; will sell intel to the Silents for a rival's death.

## Catacombs

Silents move via rooftops and canal rings (steel cables, black rope below the waterline).
