# Orestor

Ancient city reputedly founded by Orion himself (Prastaré město — the Ancient City). Political successor to Ellesmere after the Third Meredian War. Now a fractured city-state controlled by overlapping criminal and political factions.

## Districts

- **Orionova čtvrť** (Orion's Quarter) — home to jewelers, mages, Order of Maegi connections; where the Tarkelby gnome family operates
- **Přístavní čtvrť** (Harbor District) — smugglers, pashers, catacombs beneath; Fenris Tichošlápek operates here
- **Nebeská čtvrť** (Heavenly Quarter) — upper-class, guild representatives
- **Nové město** (New Town)
- **Orionova čtvrť** also has: Tři řeky (Three Rivers), Soutěska, Bořiči, Pašeráci (Smugglers)

## Government

**Closed Council (Uzavřená rada / Konšelé):** real power.
**Open Council:** nobility, guild representatives, district representatives.

Key council members:
| Position | Person | Faction |
|----------|--------|---------|
| Treasurer | Eirys Dustenhorn | Mlčenliví (Silents) via Zvěstovatelé |
| Merchant Guild | Euron Abb'Shiran | Bořiči (Demolishers) |

## Factions

### Mlčenliví (the Silents)
See [[Organisations/Mlcenlivy]].

### Orionovi Zvěstovatelé (Orion's Heralds)
Magic-positive faction. Wants to restore Orion's legacy. Will pursue Erathon fragments for their own reasons.

### Tyrův hněv (Tyr's Wrath)
Paladin faction. Can sense Erathon fragment power from catacombs. Patrols in pairs near catacomb entrances. Two named templars: **Jaskell** (huge blonde man, masterwork two-handed sword) and **Jimmy** (short, stocky, bald with black beard; shield, axe, mace). Arci-Templar: **Lucian Tillibor**.

### Bořiči (Demolishers)
Led by Euron Abb'Shiran (Merchant Guild). Wants council power. Will pursue Erathon fragments.

### The Just Ones
Surviving paladin order from Meredie's destruction. Set up a new branch in Orestor for those who survived the Meredie massacres. Also want the fragments.

## The Erathon Fragment Plot

**Background:** Orion imprisoned the great demon Erathon in a crystal at the beginning of ages. The Ellesmere explosion shattered the crystal and scattered fragments across the continent. Each fragment contains a piece of Erathon's soul. Contact corrupts and influences anyone who handles one.

**In Orestor:** A fragment (amethyst) came via a refugee child, then to "lord" Stefan Iskelny (self-proclaimed regent of an old fortress; murdered his lord Harry Broad after Meredie's fall), then to his knight Milbern, who brought it to jeweler Roondar Tarkelby for valuation. Roondar alerted Eirys. Milbern fled into the harbor district and is hiding in the catacombs. Multiple factions are hunting him.

**Šprýmař (the Jester)** — key NPC in the harbor; knows the catacombs; found Milbern near his smuggling routes. Will sell the information to the Silents in exchange for the killing of a rival smuggling boss.

## Catacombs

Beneath the city. The Silents use them for movement. Steel cables are strung between laundry lines on rooftops; iron rings with black rope are set into canal walls below the waterline for fast movement.
