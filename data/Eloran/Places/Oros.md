# Oros

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | [[Places/Dračí ostrovy]] | [[Factions/Triple Coalition]]

**Founding port** of the **[[Factions/Triple Coalition|Triple Coalition]]** on the **[[Places/Dračí ostrovy|Dragon Isles]]** (_Dračí ostrovy_) — one of three co-founding corsair republics, with [[Places/Ansir|Ansir]] and [[Places/Saltmarsh|Saltmarsh]].

**Archipelago map:** labeled **Oros** on `imports/onenote/images/dragon-islands-archipelago.png` (north-central Azure Sea coast, south of Rečistě).

## History

During the **Third Meredian War**, island ports nominally under Triskal refused to join the continental fight. **Oros**, **Ansir**, and **Saltmarsh** were among the first to declare independence — former **[[Factions/Valdie|Valdie]]** coastal holdings that broke away and formed the coalition.

## Role Today

Member republic of the Free Corsair coalition. Less documented in play than Ansir (capital) or Saltmarsh (Act I–II scenes). Treat as a peer port with its own council and corsair captains until expanded.
