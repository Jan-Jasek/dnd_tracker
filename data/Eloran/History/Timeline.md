# Timeline

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | See also: [[Cosmology]] for universe-scale history

This file is the **Eloran plane chronology** — ages, wars, and campaign events. Universe history (the Accord, the Sundering, pre-Sundering beings) lives in [[Cosmology]] and [[Weaving]].

**Campaign-era anchors** span **PS 0–PS 2**. Campaigns sit in approximate seasonal/month windows; relative timing is canon, not exact calendar dates.

### Dating Convention — PS (Post-Shattering)

**The Shattering** — the night Ellesmere fell, Orion's Seal, which held the rift, was destroyed, and fragments of Orion and Erathon scattered. Marks **PS 0** and opens the **Age of Fragments** (_Věk úlomků_).

**Czech:** _**Roztříštění**_ (chronicles, scholars — stresses fragments) or _**Rozštěpení**_ (common speech, sermons — shorter, more immediate). Both are canon; _Katastrofa_ survives as raw grief-talk, not the epoch name.

**Wiki / agent notation:** **PS** = _Post-Shattering_ / _po Roztříštění_ — years counted from the Shattering.

**Month notation:** `M01`–`M12` are approximate calendar-month positions within a PS year. They are seasonal anchors, not exact dates and not a count of months since the Shattering.

| Notation         | Meaning                                                                                  |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **PS 0**         | Year of the Shattering — Orion's Seal destroyed, city destroyed, Third Meredian War ends |
| **PS 1**         | First full year after the Shattering; Hlubina and Ansir campaign windows                 |
| **PS 2**         | Second year after the Shattering; Orestor campaign window                                |
| **PS N M01–M12** | Approximate months within a Post-Shattering year                                         |
| **pre-PS (~N)**  | Roughly _N_ years before the Shattering                                                  |

Deep world ages still use approximate **pre-PS** spans (e.g. pre-PS ~5,000 for Orion's rise). Calibrate within ranges.

**Other aliases** (same event, not alternate calendars): _Katastrofa_ (Meredian grief, pre-dates formal _Roztříštění_ usage), _the Convergence_ / _Konvergence_ (Maegi — Weave and Paths stirring). Imperial scare-preachers may say _cataclysm_, but that risks confusion with the **Sundering** at universe scale.

## Detailed History

### Age of Harmony — Creation to Sundering

Eloran's first named era. Civilizations reach their peak while the Weave flows freely through the Paths. No reliable calendar survives; treat all dates as mythic order, not arithmetic.

The **Accord** at universe scale and the **Age of Harmony** on Eloran describe the same pre-Sundering world from different scopes — see [[Cosmology]].

### pre-PS ~5,000 — Age of Darkness → Orion's Rise

The Weave on Eloran is tainted following the Sundering. Pre-Sundering beings (Erathon chief among them) feed on it. Weave users become corrupted and hungry for power. Civilizations are built and destroyed in cycles of war.

**Orion rises.** A mortal born in this era. Develops the Art through multiple attempts — earlier frameworks produce other Weave lineages (blood mages and others) before he achieves the complete ten-power structure. Founds Orestor (Prastaré město — the Ancient City). See [[People/Orion]].

**The Binding of Erathon.** Orion chooses Ellesmere for Path-access. He and the nine bind Erathon into a **floating rift**. Their students are not in the chamber. A fortress is raised around the rift.

### pre-PS ~2,000 — Sealing Ritual; Start of the Age of Salvation

Orion attempts the 10th seal. The nine betray him. He forces a partial completion. He **merges with Erathon** in the rift. Some of the nine die in the blast; survivors are marked; Ilrusk takes a student's body and lives.

The surviving marked disciples found the **Apostolic Church of Orion** (_Apoštolská církev Orionova_) as cover-up — the faith that names this era the **Age of Salvation** (_Věk spásy_). Worshippers are **Orionists** (_Orionisté_). They launch a crusade against rival powers (the Bloody War of the Druids — _Krvavá válka druidů_ — is a likely ripple effect of this era). Later Maegi are never told the rift exists.

The Order of Maegi continues to grow through later generations of blood-ritual creation. Members visible in present-day records include Theodor Eon (evocation, diamond ring), Roderik Loon (abjuration, citrine ring), Sibyla Fayen, Garcilaso von Vega, Josias Simmler.

### The Long Depletion (~2,000 years)

With Eloran's Paths sealed and the Weave unable to replenish, magic slowly fades. Species wither. Civilizations that once practiced the Art lose access to it. The process takes thousands of years.

The Apostolic Church grows autonomous — mage-governed in secret. The **Order of the Just** (_Řád Spravedlivých_), co-founded by Ilrusk and equipped with **Severite / Mlčoun**, grows into the force that will overthrow mage control of the church hierarchy.

### pre-PS ~500+ — River cities

Three cities on the Red River form a **trade coalition** and hold the water for a long time: **[[Places/Orestor|Orestor]]** (west delta), the Triskal east-delta port (name TBD), and the island-fort at the end of the marches (now Imperial; name TBD). All three still remember it. This is older than Bořivoj's kingdom and older than the United Empire.

### pre-PS ~300 — Bořivoj unifies Meredie

For centuries **[[Factions/Meredie|Meredie]]** is the prize of the continent — split, vassalized, fought over by **[[Factions/Valdie|Valdie]]** from the north and the river states south of **Gutberg** (now Imperial; name TBD). **Bořivoj I the Liberator** (_Bořivoj 1. Osvoboditel_) unifies it into a nation that can hold its mountains and rivers. **[[Places/Ellesmere|Ellesmere]]** is the capital. **[[Places/Lapiberg|Lapiberg]]** becomes a free city.

That northern peace is breathing room for the south. Contenders there stop feeding a Meredian war and start founding the **[[Factions/United Empire|United Empire]]**. The coastal merchant patchwork that will become **[[Factions/Triskal|Triskal]]** federates when that Empire rises.

### pre-PS ~155 — End of Salvation; Start of the Just Age

The Order of the Just takes political control of Ellesmere and conducts the **Ellesmere culling** — a massacre of Maegi practitioners in the city. Many Maegi are killed. Jeod Isthar — at this point already a Maegi, perhaps a few centuries old — witnesses this. The grievance is personal, not generational.

The culling marks the templar coup and ends the **Age of Salvation**. The **Just Age** (_Věk spravedlnosti_) begins — a separate era, not a sub-period of Salvation. Continental military dictatorship replaces apostolic mage rule. Orionist devotion is not suppressed; the mage-tainted institution is.

### PS 0 — The Shattering (Age of Fragments begins)

[[People/Jeod Isthar|Jeod Isthar]] destroys Orion's Seal, which holds the rift. He means to restore the Weave. He knows the Marble Fortress will blow. He does not know the rift will collapse. He does not expect the scale. He does not care. The essence it held crystallizes into fragments of Orion and Erathon. Ellesmere is leveled. Jeod survives.

The catastrophe shifts the land and seabed. A great earthquake sends a tsunami against exposed eastern shores of the continent and across the Dragon Archipelago to its far eastern islands. Many coastal ships are destroyed. Vessels sheltered farther upriver or in protected waters have better chances of surviving; the actual damage depends on local geography. Trade and shipping remain disrupted long afterward.

**Weave resurgence:** Magic strengthens worldwide. Forgotten creatures awaken — orcs, dryads, gith bands, and others press borders that had been quiet for centuries. The Templars of the Just reform under Branon Vznešený; magic becomes registered and tolerated under supervision in some states, hunted in others.

**Aftermath in Orestor:** survivors of [[Places/Ellesmere|Ellesmere]] suffer wasting corruption. Many [[Organisations/Order of the Just|templars]] fear divine punishment for their abuses as magic awakens among ordinary people. [[People/Benedikt|Benedikt]] seeks adaptation; Lucian Tillibor leads [[Organisations/Orion's Wrath|Hněv]] toward escalating persecution. By PS 2 the local templars are divided roughly evenly, with Hněv gaining ground.

The [[#The Third Meredian War|Third Meredian War]]'s closing battles unfold in the same catastrophe window — see below. Fragments begin finding hosts.

### Meredian Wars

The **named** wars are three Imperial campaigns against independent **templar-Meredie**. Bořivoj unified the kingdom pre-PS ~300. The Empire is the Maegi pole; the Order has no authority there. Taking Ellesmere would mean destroying the templars. Geography makes seizure a grind. Decades of that effort, then the Shattering, leave the Empire spent.

| War        | When       | Outcome                                                                                               |
| ---------- | ---------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **First**  | pre-PS ~55 | Imperial campaign on the fortress-kingdom. Fails.                                                     |
| **Second** | pre-PS ~45 | Reconquest fails. Meredie comes out harder.                                                           |
| **Third**  | **PS 0**   | Empire with **[[Factions/Triskal\|Triskal]]**. Gutberg falls. Then Ellesmere is destroyed. See below. |

The house (name **TBD**) runs from **Bořivoj I** to the Shattering. All **male direct descendants** are dead. The purkrabí of **[[Places/Orestor|Orestor]]** is **Jan** — female-line blood, a marshal's name, a contender.

See [[Notes/Open Questions#Meredian Wars]].

### Recent History (pre-PS ~10–30)

**Dragonborn emancipation in northern Eloran.**
~20 years before PS 0, the northern states (likely Meredie's sphere) abolished the slavery of Dragonborn — enslaved Dragon Islands natives. This caused population growth, the formation of isolated Dragonborn settlements, and retaliatory slave raids from southern powers. The slave trade with the southern Empire continues.

### The Third Meredian War

Culminates at PS 0.

The third Imperial attempt to take **Meredie** — independent since Bořivoj (pre-PS ~300), templar heartland since the culling. Triskal joins as an ally of convenience. The sequence:

1. Triskal (allied with the Empire) simultaneously attacks Valdie. Valdie falls quickly.
2. The Empire invades Meredie. The mountain fortress **[[Places/Gutberg|Gutberg]]** — the south gate, the one army path — falls. Shocking. The old-age metal bridge is not destroyed.
3. Meredie mobilizes fast, maneuvers the Empire into splitting forces.
4. One Empire column under Marshal **Von Edgeira** takes **Dol Andor** after a short siege, leaving a minimal garrison.
5. The other column under General **Jenryr Von Nedur** — acting against orders — engages Meredie directly and is decisively defeated. Von Nedur dies.
6. Meredian forces, overconfident after the victory, attack Von Edgeira's regrouped army and are repelled. They retreat to **Ellesmere** (capital; the Marble City; templar seat).
7. Von Edgeira secures flanks and supply lines and prepares a proper siege.
8. Before the siege begins: **Ellesmere is destroyed overnight in a massive explosion**. The epicenter is the Marble Fortress of the Templars of the Just Ones. The central district is a crater. Much of the city collapses.

**The Ellesmere explosion** is Jeod destroying Orion's Seal. The rift collapses. Fragments of Orion and Erathon scatter. Anyone who handles a fragment is psychically influenced — violence, corruption, obsession. This is the hidden root of the Shattering.

**Archipelago closing (same PS 0 window):**

9. Naval battle at **Bezútěšný vršek** — simultaneous with the Ellesmere blast; the tsunami interrupts the fight.
10. Survivors regroup at **Covel**. Triskal and the Empire press a combined assault.
11. **Battle of Covel** — Torsten (first corsair consul, **[[People/Birger Aderen|Birger Aderen]]**'s nephew) dies defending the northern republic. Triskal betrays its Imperial allies mid-battle (as at Orestor). Coalition retreats; free-republic borders shrink back toward the Ansir peninsula.

**Immediate aftermath (PS 0):**

- The war ends without a clean victory for either side. **Meredie's** noble class is decimated, the royal line is effectively ended, and political power begins shifting toward merchant, criminal, and military networks.
- At **[[Places/Orestor|Orestor]]**, Triskal massacres the Imperial eastern column, then negotiates a **protectorate** with the purkrabí. When the deal becomes known, monarchists seize the purkrabí and his family. Loyalists, republicans, refugees, and mediators force a city-republic compact rather than allowing the port to fall into another full-scale battle.
- In the **Dragon Archipelago**, the battle at **Bezútěšný vršek** (_Bleak Hill_) is interrupted by the Shattering's tsunami. Survivors regroup at **Covel**. Triskal turns on its Imperial allies, **Torsten** dies, and the corsair forces retreat toward the Ansir peninsula.
- The Weave surges back into Eloran. Forgotten creatures and hostile powers begin reappearing, and the first Erathon fragments begin finding hosts. Refugee streams move south and east from the ruined Ellesmere region.

**Following weeks to months (late PS 0):**

- Triskal retains **Valdie** on the continent and regains most of the Dragon Islands, while the Empire remains weakened but intact. The betrayal at Orestor and Covel fractures the former alliance.
- Former Valdie island holdings consolidate into the **[[Factions/Triple Coalition|Triple Coalition of Free Corsair Republics]]**. After Torsten's death no new consul is chosen. The coalition settles into three federal states — Saltmarsh under **Birger Aderen**, Ansir under **Stoupač**, Oros under **Hedvábný** — whose leaders act as a triumvirate with equal votes on foreign matters. Its surviving core holds into PS 1.
- Triskal's military-religious order reforms under **Branon Vznešený**. The Empire blames the **Order of the Just** and outlaws it; Triskal continues using the Just Ones as state enforcers. An elite Just host of ~500 paladins moves against neutral **[[Places/Lapiberg|Lapiberg]]**, while orc incursions pressure the western lowlands.
- The former Meredian heartland remains politically fractured. Refugee movement, the disappearance of central authority, Templar hunts for surviving Maegi, and local military strongmen create the conditions seen in **[[Places/Hlubina|Hlubina]]** and the Ellesmere lowlands.

### PS 1 — First Post-Shattering Year

**PS 1 M05–M06 — Hlubina** — Late spring. Players arrive in Hlubina, a marshland village near ruined Ellesmere, as mercenaries with a refugee caravan. Post-war context: displaced people, scarce food, decimated imperial garrison after Orestor. **Captain Aemon Whiteoak**; **[[People/Kate|Kate]]** (Jeod's apprentice) recruits them. See [[Campaigns/Hlubina]].

**PS 1 M10–M12 — Dragon Independence, Act I (Ansir)** — Autumn into winter; the party is away from Saltmarsh about six weeks. The party sets out from Saltmarsh as corsairs of the Free Republic, meeting a lone rider with an Orion–Erathon fragment on the road. Weapon smuggling, death of Commander Hemmond, Harry Mansion. See [[Campaigns/Dragon Independence]].

**PS 1 M12 — Alastor's return to Saltmarsh** — A plague incident tied to the rider's crystal has drawn a squadron of the Just to Saltmarsh. Alastor sets out after Xendra, publicly named as the Vilmaran agent. In preparation.

### PS 2 — Second Post-Shattering Year

**PS 2 M02–M03 — Orestor campaign** — Late winter / early spring. Starting situation: the city-republic compact of PS 0–PS 1. See [[Campaigns/Orestor]].

**After PS 2 M03 — Dragon Independence, Acts II–III** — A few months after Act I, following the Orestor campaign.

### Beyond PS 2

Undated long-term pressures: spread of Erathon fragments, surviving Maegi and the Order of the Just, political rebuilding of Meredie and the Dragon Islands, Erathon's attempt to reopen the Fey gate.
