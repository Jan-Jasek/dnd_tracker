# Orestor Schematic

> Part of: [[Places/Orestor|Orestor]]

## Purpose and status

Two editable DM schematics support discussion of city movement and the Shoalrunner incident: [city layout](orestor-city-schematic.svg) and [regional rivers](orestor-river-system.svg). They are not measured maps. Geography supplied by the DM is recorded below; coastline shapes, bearings, district boundaries, building positions, and boat routes remain proposals.

## Geography

**Klidná** rises in the northwestern mountains separating Valdia and Meredia. It follows a broad curve through Ellesmere and northern Meredia to Orestor. **Klikatic** rises near Gutberg in the mountains beside the Red River in southern Meredia, winds through southern and eastern Meredia, and joins Klidná a few tens of kilometres upstream of Orestor.

The **Red River** is a separate drainage. It rises in **The Spine** far west, passes through mountains separating Meredia and Union, and flows east before turning north. Its northern arm reaches the sea serving Orestor. Its eastern arm reaches a broad bay separating Union and Triskal. **Blackwater Sea** and **Blackwater Bay** are working names. Legend attributes the eastern passage from the bay to the ocean to Orion breaking through the land. Whether inhabitants call that passage a canal remains open.

The city drawing places the sea north and Klidná flowing locally north, making Ores the western, left bank and Tor the eastern, right bank when facing downstream. This orientation is a proposal, not a regional bearing established by the DM. The Klikatic confluence lies outside the city drawing. The Red River lowlands also lie beyond it to the south.

Mělčinář moors directly alongside a quay at the sea port, with gangplank access, because its rig cannot pass beneath the city bridges. Small local freight boats, fishing craft, ferries, and transfer barges use the river harbour and canals. Marker 1 in the SVG still indicates the earlier river-quay proposal and needs relocation once the sea-port quay is placed; the illustrated raid approach is consequently pending revision.

Ores occupies both a defensible cliff and adjacent low ground. Marshes and small channels extend around the city; solid ground for major quays is unevenly distributed. River channels, drainage canals, and tidal waterways can coexist near an estuary; their relative importance and depth need defining. See [NOAA's estuary overview](https://coast.noaa.gov/nerrs/about/what-is-an-estuary.html) for the physical analogy.

### Source conflicts to reconcile

- [[Places/Orestor]] and [[Campaigns/Orestor]] place Orestor on the western mouth of the Red River. The DM's current geography places it on Klidná, with the Red reaching the coast separately.
- [[Places/Regions]] names the Red River's source mountains Hor stínů. Their relationship to The Spine is unresolved.
- Union's relationship to the existing Imperial geography needs checking; the schematic retains the DM's name without assuming they are interchangeable.
- The relationship between **Tři soutěsky** and the existing district names Tři řeky, Soutěska, Bořiči, and Pašeráci is unresolved.

## Zones and districts

- **Ores:** the older, smaller settlement on the left bank. Orion's house and Nebeská čtvrť occupy the cliff above the lower city. **Přístavní čtvrť** lies east below the Sky District. The River district also occupies lower ground, with several river and coastal harbours.
- **Tor:** the larger right-bank settlement, developed across numerous small rivers and connected by bridges. It houses the Merchants' Guild and much of the city's workshop activity, extending south toward the Red River's wet lowlands.
- **Mokřiny:** marshy slums north of Orestor beyond the mountain route. Unstable shores discourage a major formal port but offer opportunities for smugglers.
- **Tři soutěsky**, colloquially **Tři děvky:** a three-branched gorge running north from below Přístavní čtvrť through the mountains to Mokřiny. It provides a mountain route a few kilometres long. The precise branching pattern and the connections between its branches remain open.

The first bridge united Ores and Tor; later bridges connect their expanding neighbourhoods. No bridge names, wall circuit, street widths, or travel times are fixed here.

### Major coastal ports

The three major trading cities of Blackwater Sea and Bay are **Orestor** (Meredian), **Vallenza** (Triskalan), and **Caldris** (Union). Caldris lies near the delta of the Red River's eastern arm, farther inside the bay; Mělčinář sails from there. Vallenza's exact site remains open. The Dragon Isles, including Ansir, are a separate archipelago far to the east.

**Skardvik** is Valdia's large port to the northwest, outside Blackwater Bay. Valdia lies beyond the high mountains and marshes north of Orestor's slums. Its exact coastline and the routes across that terrain remain open.

The city SVG labels Mokřiny separately from Tři soutěsky. Its compressed northern terrain needs redrawing to show the several-kilometre mountain corridor; the gorge annotation does not establish a measured route.

## Faction control

The port is the jurisdiction disputed by [[People/Konrad|Konrad]], [[People/Lothar|Sir Lothar]], and [[People/Alynn|Lady Alynn]]. The Merchants' Guild's Tor headquarters is an agreed district association; its exact building site is proposed. The Just's arrival at the detained ship adds another claim of authority without establishing permanent templar control over the harbour.

Bořiči use personal contacts and merchant storage access. Their proposed warehouse refuge is hidden within ordinary guild activity. Smugglers operate around the marsh margins; specific territories for Zbožní and the Silent Ones require a later pass.

## Secrets and hidden layer

The refuge's everyday guild ownership conceals its use by Bořiči. The visible investigation follows Jindra's home, Lyanna's house, the guild doorman, and the warehouse keeper; it does not require a secret tunnel.

The brothers' refuge and the cargo handoff remain separate decisions. The dashed boat route illustrates an option, not the raid's settled course. No asset establishes that the explosive is stolen: the raid targets Rauk's equipment and other valuables while the actual consignment remains concealed aboard.

## Encounter density

The customs berth concentrates rival patrols, detained passengers, crew, and anxious traders. Bridges concentrate traffic and make visible checkpoints plausible. The riverside market offers witnesses among ordinary crowds. Warehouse back channels and marsh edges support discreet boat movement, but navigability, lighting, patrols, and tides need defining before a raid route can be timed.

The cliff separates prestigious upper districts from lower quays. Stairs and approaches are useful social and movement constraints; no hidden access is established.

## Generation Prompt

Create a north-up schematic of Orestor, an old river and coastal city formed by joining Ores on the left bank of Klidná with larger Tor on the right. Show an ancient cliff-top Sky District and Orion's house above Ores, a dominant library below it, lower river and port neighbourhoods, multiple bridges, Tor's merchant headquarters and workshops amid branching small rivers, and the northern Mokřiny slums reached by Tři soutěsky, a three-branched gorge route extending several kilometres north through mountains from below Přístavní čtvrť, east beneath the Sky District. Distinguish natural channels from proposed canals. Include a separate regional inset with Klidná from northwestern mountains through Ellesmere, winding Klikatic joining upstream, and the independent Red River from the western Spine dividing into northern sea and eastern bay arms. Mark uncertain geography and adventure sites as candidates; avoid invented walls, fixed distances, and unconfirmed landmarks.

## Open questions

### Layout discussion order

Develop one item at a time. Agreed geography above is the foundation; this queue concerns unresolved shape, access, and placement. Day 1 preparation remains open in the [campaign discussion queue](../../../ideas/eloran/orestor-preparation-questions.md).

1. **Coastline and orientation.** Where does Klidná meet the sea relative to Ores, Tor, and the Sky District cliff? Confirm the local bearings before redrawing the schematic.
2. **Scale and terrain.** How far do the main districts extend, and how do the cliff, northern mountains, and several-kilometre Tři soutěsky route fit together? Define its three branches and where they reach Mokřiny.
3. **Waterways and crossings.** Which small channels are natural or excavated, and which are navigable by cargo boats? Place the principal bridges, ferries, and routes between the banks; define useful depth and tidal constraints without requiring an engineering survey.
4. **District character and boundaries.** Place the River district and the remaining named neighbourhoods around the established Ores/Tor structure. Reconcile Orionova čtvrť, Nové město, and the older sub-area names with this layout.
5. **Sea port and customs quay.** Locate Mělčinář's berth, customs premises, warehouses, passenger holding space, and shore approaches. Distinguish these from the small-craft river harbour.
6. **Civic and religious sights.** Place the councils, court, guardhouse, Jan's household, and the establishments of Jeroným, Benedikt, and Tilibor. Establish their visible presence and how visitors reach them; organizational histories remain separate follow-ups.
7. **Orion's Library.** Choose its prominent site below the Sky District, approaches, public rooms, restricted collections, and Antonín's everyday working space. Develop the existing library brief in more detail.
8. **Everyday meeting places.** Place and name the firm's regular tavern, useful inns, market, hospice, and working waterfront. Describe enough ordinary activity to give the city a life beyond faction business.
9. **Investigation addresses.** Review Jindra's house and rope workshop, Eti's distant home, Lyanna's house, the guild, warehouse refuge, and Riedel's forge. Trace Hans's journey and plausible witnesses against the agreed streets and waterways.
10. **Criminal waterfront.** Choose the brothers' refuge and Zdenda's receiving site, then establish the cargo boat, approach, escape, and handoff route. This supplies the geography needed to resume the Day 1 raid questions.

Regional source conflicts, the final Blackwater names, Vallenza's exact site, and mountain names remain a separate reconciliation task. They need not block placing the opening scenes within Orestor.
