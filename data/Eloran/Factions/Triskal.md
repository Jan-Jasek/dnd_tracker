# Triskal

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | See also: [[Factions/United Empire]], [[Factions/Triple Coalition]]

French, Italian, and Spanish cultural inspiration. **Triskal** is the federation's common language; its coastal states have distinct local speech and naming traditions. A federation of small wealthy coastal and merchant states that **grouped as Triskal** when the **[[Factions/United Empire|United Empire]]** rose in the south. Before that the map here was ever-changing.

Northern expansionist once federated. Ally of convenience with the Empire in the **Third Meredian War** — then betrayal at Orestor and Covel.

The **Red River** is Triskal's west line: triple-border islands with Empire and Meredie, then the Triskal–Meredie stretch, then the **east bank of the delta** facing **[[Places/Orestor|Orestor]]**. That east-delta city is unnamed.

**pre-PS ~500+:** that city, Orestor, and the Imperial island-fort at the end of the marches were a **river-trade coalition**. The memory is still live. The island-fort is what stops Triskal hitting the Empire from the north.

**Symbol:** red-white field with black wolf.

## Third Meredian War

- Attacked **[[Factions/Valdie|Valdie]]** simultaneously with the Empire's Meredian invasion; Valdie fell quickly
- After the Shattering: betrayed Imperial forces besieging **[[Places/Orestor|Orestor]]**; **protectorate** deal with the purkrabí includes **clearing Imperial influence** from new holdings — Triskal does not assault the port after the massacre; coup follows inside the city when the deal is known
- Declared Meredie a **protectorate** on paper; **Orestor** remains a contested city-republic at PS 1
- Fought Von Edgeira to a stalemate; war dragged on until Veil resurgence forced both sides to withdraw

## Dragon Archipelago

- Had nominal control over island ports during the Third War
- **[[Factions/Triple Coalition|Triple Coalition]]** broke away when ports refused to fight
- Wants annexation or protectorate status over the archipelago — primary external threat to the Free Republic

## Religion

Hosts the [[Organisations/Order of the Just|Order of the Just]] as state enforcers; reformed under **Branon Vznešený** after the Shattering. An elite host of ~500 paladins besieges neutral **[[Places/Lapiberg|Lapiberg]]** post-PS 0 (import).
