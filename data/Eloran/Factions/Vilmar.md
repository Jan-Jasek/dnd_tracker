# Vilmar (Vilmarský sultanát)

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | See also: [[Regions/Southern Continent]], [[Factions/United Empire]]

Sultanate. Language: **Vilmaran**. Southern power on the **southern continent** — **not** a neighbor of Meredie on the Eloran mainland.

## Border with the Empire

The **[[Factions/United Empire|United Empire]]** and Vilmar face each other **across the sea** on the **[[Regions/Southern Continent|southern continent]]** — desert coast, **[[Places/Gibešská poušť|Gibešská poušť]]**, ports like **[[Places/Akbesi|Akbesi]]**.

Imperial military careers on this frontier are common (e.g. **Captain Henry Von Telten** on the Sultanate border before archipelago events).

## Dragon Archipelago

Separate theater — Vilmar projects power by sea:

- **Hilua** (_Hilua_) — ancient agent of Saybar in the body of a bloodless girl of about thirteen. Sent to weaken the **[[Factions/Triple Coalition|Triple Coalition]]** and break the Triskal truce so Vilmar could seize **[[Places/Ansir|Ansir]]**. Led the ritual at Harry's mansion that used Orion's First Seal to break through the Veil. Presumed dead; her crushed body was seen regenerating in the rubble.
- **Xendra** — Saltmarsh councilwoman and Vilmaran agent. Held Captain Von Telten's family hostage to force him into the smuggling chain, and let the town's weapon caches drain away through her council seat. Holds Hilua's second Sending Stone. Escaped Saltmarsh after the conspiracy was exposed.

## Prophet Saybar

**Prophet Saybar** (_Prorok Saybar_) is the hidden head of the Vilmaran church and the being the Sultanate worships as its religious symbol.

**Hidden truth:** Saybar is one of the first **Prastarí** — a vampire-like being from one of [[People/Orion|Orion's]] early frameworks of the Art, before the Maegi. After Orion's fall, **[[People/Ilrusk|Ilrusk]]** found and studied him as part of his own attempt to remake Orion's work. When Ilrusk decided to get rid of him, Saybar fled south to Vilmar. Ilrusk is his sworn enemy.

Saybar builds an army of followers through blood rituals of sacrifice. The changed gain unnatural life span, regeneration, speed, resistance to harm, and magic. The ritual does not always take; those it half-changes are left marked and withered.

## Reputation

- **Blood mages** (_krvaví mágové_) and necromancers — feared in desert rumor; **Sayid Azolar** whispered at Akbesi market
- **Nebeský Vilmarion** (Heavenly Vilmarion) in old notes
- **Urian Ab Nesir** — Vilmaran optician seeking trade on the Dragon Isles
