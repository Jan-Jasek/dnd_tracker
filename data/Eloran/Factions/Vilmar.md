# Vilmar (Vilmarský sultanát)

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | See also: [[Regions/Southern Continent]], [[Factions/United Empire]]

Sultanate. Language: **Vilmaran**. Southern power on the **southern continent** — **not** a neighbor of Meredie on the Eloran mainland.

## Border with the Empire

The **[[Factions/United Empire|United Empire]]** and Vilmar face each other **across the sea** on the **[[Regions/Southern Continent|southern continent]]** — desert coast, **[[Places/Gibešská poušť|Gibešská poušť]]**, ports like **[[Places/Akbesi|Akbesi]]**.

Imperial military careers on this frontier are common (e.g. **Captain Henry Von Telten** on the Sultanate border before archipelago events).

## Dragon Archipelago

Separate theater — Vilmar projects power by sea:

- **Hilua** (_Hilua_) — agent sent to weaken **[[Factions/Triple Coalition|Triple Coalition]]** and break the Triskal truce so Vilmar could seize **[[Places/Ansir|Ansir]]**
- Hilua served **Prophet Saybar** (_Prorok Saybar_) — hidden head of Vilmaran church (import: possibly a **Prastarý** / pre-Sundering fragment-beings plot)

Hilua is dead before Act III; **Xendra** in Saltmarsh still holds her second Sending Stone.

## Reputation

- **Blood mages** (_krvaví mágové_) and necromancers — feared in desert rumor; **Sayid Azolar** whispered at Akbesi market
- **Nebeský Vilmarion** (Heavenly Vilmarion) in old notes
- **Urian Ab Nesir** — Vilmaran optician seeking trade on the Dragon Isles

## Open Questions

- Sultan, court, and war aims beyond Ansir
- Saybar / Saybara — church, necromancy, and Prastarý lore (contested in imports)
- Formal peace or trade with the Empire on the southern coast
