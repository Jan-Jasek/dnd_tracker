# Vilmar (Vilmarský sultanát)

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | See also: [[Regions/Southern Continent]], [[Factions/United Empire]]

Sultanate. Language: **Vilmaran**. Southern power on the **southern continent** — **not** a neighbor of Meredie on the Northern Continent.

## Border with the Empire

The **[[Factions/United Empire|United Empire]]** and Vilmar face each other **across the sea** on the **[[Regions/Southern Continent|southern continent]]** — desert coast, **[[Places/Gibesh Desert|Gibesh Desert]]**, ports like **[[Places/Akbesi|Akbesi]]**.

Imperial military careers on this frontier are common (e.g. **Captain Henry Von Telten** on the Sultanate border before archipelago events).

## Dragon Archipelago

Separate theater — Vilmar projects power by sea:

- **Hilua** (_Hilua_) — century-old blood mage and agent of Saybar in the body of a bloodless girl of about thirteen. Sent to weaken the **[[Factions/Triple Coalition|Triple Coalition]]** and break the Triskal truce so Vilmar could seize **[[Places/Ansir|Ansir]]**. Led the ritual at Harry's mansion that inverted the first of Orion's seals to open a breach in the Veil and imbue Harry. Died in the ritual's collapse.
- **Xendra** — Saltmarsh councilwoman and Vilmaran agent. Held Captain Von Telten's family hostage to force him into the smuggling chain, and let the town's weapon caches drain away through her council seat. Escaped Saltmarsh after the conspiracy was exposed. The smuggling was only the opening of Vilmar's plans on the islands, and she carries them on.

## Prophet Saybar

**Prophet Saybar** (_Prorok Saybar_) is the hidden head of the Vilmaran church and the being the Sultanate worships as its religious symbol.

**Hidden truth:** Saybar is a vampire-like being from one of [[People/Orion|Orion's]] early frameworks of the Art, before the Maegi. After Orion's fall, **[[People/Ilrusk|Ilrusk]]** found and studied him as part of his own attempt to remake Orion's work. When Ilrusk decided to get rid of him, Saybar fled south to Vilmar. Ilrusk is his sworn enemy. Saybar and the other surviving creations of that age call themselves **Prastarí** (the Ancients), the common name for beings out of the age of legends; Saybar's blood mages are not Prastarí.

### Požehnaní

Saybar's blood mages are the **Požehnaní** (the Blessed). He has been making them for centuries through blood rituals of sacrifice. The changed gain unnatural life span, regeneration, speed, resistance to harm, and magic. Each one is costly and difficult to make. The ritual needs enormous energy, drawn from one of several sources: the blood of very old lineages, which often carry demon blood from before Orion's days, or royal blood; many ordinary sacrifices; or the caster's own essence, at the cost of a large part of it. Hilua needed Harry for his old Triskal noble line. The ritual does not always take; those it half-changes are left marked and withered. Since the Shattering, the Požehnaní feel their power grow.

### Aims on the Dragon Islands

Vilmar means to wake the curse in the blood of the dark-eyed Taubers and use them to take control of the Dragon Islands, then launch an invasion of the northern continent. The weapon smuggling in Ansir was only the opening move.

## Reputation

- **Nebeský Vilmarion** (Heavenly Vilmarion) in old notes
- **Urian Ab Nesir** — Vilmaran optician seeking trade on the Dragon Isles
