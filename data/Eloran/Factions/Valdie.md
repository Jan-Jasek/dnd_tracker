# Valdie

> Part of: [[Eloran]] | See also: [[Factions/Triskal]], [[Factions/Meredie]]

Scandinavian cultural inspiration. Language: **Veildic** (_Valedický_). The historical north. Geopolitical analogue: a state no one really conquers — depth, winter, distance. **[[Factions/Meredie|Meredie]]** was contested from this side for centuries before Bořivoj.

The Meredie border is mountainy and more open than the south (Krkonoše-like). An **inner vassal** sits west of Meredie and north-abuts Valdie (name TBD). One of **[[Factions/Grundarmheld|Grundarmheld]]'s** ground cities sits in Valdie (name TBD); another in that vassal.

## Third Meredian War

Fell to **[[Factions/Triskal|Triskal]]** (with northern **Ioth** raider allies) in the opening phase of the [[History/Timeline#The Third Meredian War|Third Meredian War]] — weeks, not months. The first time a southern neighbour overruns the accessible kingdom. What remains of the deep north is open.

## Dragon Archipelago

Former **Valdie coastal holdings** on the islands were among the first to declare independence during the Third War, seeding the **[[Factions/Triple Coalition|Triple Coalition]]** (with **[[Places/Saltmarsh|Saltmarsh]]**, **[[Places/Ansir|Ansir]]**, **[[Places/Oros|Oros]]**).
