## Archived sequence from the imported Orestor notes

1. A refugee child finds the amethyst near **Stará tvrz**; the shard drives a massacre; guards deliver it to regent **Stefan Iskelny** (murderer of lord **Harry Broad** at a lowland fortress — not the port coup).
2. Stefan sends knight **Milbern** and a half-dozen men-at-arms to Orestor to have the stone valued and to fence other loot.
3. **Roondar Tarkelby** (gnome jeweler, Orionova čtvrť) finds the stone is not an ordinary amethyst and alerts **Eirys**.
4. Street fight: Silents, Hněv, and Zvěstovatelé collide. City watch arrives. Milbern slips into **Přístavní čtvrť**, disguises as a beggar, hides in a cellar / the catacombs. Two surviving men-at-arms hunt a smuggler who will get him out.
5. **Šprýmař** finds the hide near his smuggling routes. He will sell the location to the Silents in exchange for the death of a rival smuggler boss.
6. Hněv pairs patrol catacomb mouths; they can sense the stone. Harbor gates close. Hunt continues.

**Import variants** (not collapsed): who first hears from Roondar (Eirys vs Euron); order of ambushes on the street.
