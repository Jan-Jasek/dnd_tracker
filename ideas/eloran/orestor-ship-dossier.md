# Orestor — detained trader and passenger dossier

> Part of: [[Campaigns/Orestor]]
> **Ship design draft.** Use with [the bank's opening job](orestor-opening-job.md) and [the custody timeline](orestor-sacred-timeline.md). Names, numbers, cargo lots, and individual histories are proposals until discussed with the DM.

The [opening cast sheet](orestor-opening-cast.md) turns these backgrounds into immediate requests, testimony, and reactions during the first three days.

## Vessel and history

**Working type:** a shallow-draft coastal and lower-river trader. Sail is its main propulsion along the coast and with favorable winds. Two long sweeps help with turning, berthing, and gentler reaches. Against a strong current, the crew poles in shallows or hires a shore team and towline where the bank allows it. Neither method makes a laden ship fast upriver; depth, wind, current, and labor set the pace. It has no paddlewheel.

The captain and this ship have worked the delta ports for roughly two decades. The Shattering's earthquake and tsunami destroyed many vessels on exposed eastern coasts, including several in his small trading concern. This ship survived PS 0 in a sheltered side channel farther upriver. It now circles the main Red River delta ports, including Orestor and Triskal's east-delta port, and makes opportunistic trips upriver. A working route for this voyage is **Imperial island-fort → Meredian landing on the safer western side of the river → Orestor**, skirting Triskal-held waters and taking on passengers in Meredie. The particular Meredian stop, previous sea leg, next port, and sailing deadline remain open.

**Ship:** **_The Shoalrunner_**, called **_Mělčinář_** in Czech, a familiar sight at the three old trade-coalition ports: Orestor, the Triskal east-delta city, and the Imperial island-fort. This opening case is **The Shoalrunner Incident**. **Candidate captain:** Varek, an independent and sharp trader from the Empire's southern maritime frontier facing Vilmar across the sea. He smokes cigars made of rolled tobacco leaves or another setting-appropriate herb, but forbids flames below deck. He knows the bank's lot is undeclared and knows which passenger will take it from him, but **does not know the final recipient**. He does not personally move the hidden lot or supervise every passenger's baggage. His surname, home port, and ownership arrangement remain open.

The Empire has Roman/Byzantine roots and many subject cultures. A Hungarian-inspired community on its southern maritime frontier is a possible fit, not established canon. **Váradi Bálint** (family name first in that culture; **Bálint Váradi** in a foreign manifest) is a candidate full name for the captain; **Varek** can remain his current working name until the DM chooses. This needs a named province or port before promotion to canon.

**Working scale for play:** ten crew including Varek, eleven proposed individual passengers including a bounty hunter, and the Novotný family. Jakub, Alena, Běla, and Ondřej are named so far; they may have more children, so the passenger total is open. A short enclosed passenger space and a covered deck area make for a crowded voyage; the family is not secretly involved in the freight. The bank has chartered space for its lot, which lets its representative call this “my employer's ship” without making Varek a bank employee or the bank the hull's owner. Final tonnage, berth plan, and which crew members know about the concealed lot remain open.

The bank orders the advocates to release the ship and cargo without searches or seizure and to ensure delivery to its original beneficiary. Varek can identify Matěj as the authorized handoff, but cannot name that beneficiary. Matěj controls the next link; his full briefing and knowledge of the final recipient remain open. In the [three-day working version](orestor-opening-cast.md), he knows the lot is powder and has a next contact, but **does not know the hiding place**; Varek can verify the kegs without a structural search. The bank representative does not tell the party. Alynn knowingly arranges the reserve as protection against an unknown attempt to seize power or a collapse of the compact; she and other republicans are arming against a loyalist or monarchist takeover and strengthening their bid for a capitalist republic. What she knows of individual containers and agents remains to be set.

**Magical explosive — candidate under discussion:** a compact Imperial shipment could contain a magical explosive with a proposed total destructive power comparable to a tonne of TNT. Its substance, trigger, Veil signature, and exact potency remain open. A concealed compartment in the captain’s cabin is a candidate storage location; ordinary customs inspection, searches of private belongings, and thorough searches need distinct scopes. The first-night raiders take Rauk’s equipment and selected captain’s belongings, while this sensitive lot remains hidden aboard in the baseline.

**Powder in this setting — working proposal:** southern Imperial powder-making is a recent craft. Small charges can serve mining, demolition, or crude siege weapons; neither common firearms nor a settled Orestor trade regime follows from its existence. Possession and sale are not expressly forbidden. Concealing a hazardous imported lot and omitting it from the manifest would still create a serious customs and safety dispute if discovered. Whether any particular duty, declaration rule, or wartime decree applies remains to be written. The city's rumor calls it a regime-changing weapon; that is a political interpretation, not proof of what Alynn intends to do with it.

**Hold layout:** the main hatch opens to a central cargo space stacked with Varek's grain sacks. Cloth, iron fittings, and medicines occupy clearly marked smaller lots; crew keep personal chests outside the freight space, while a separate shipboard baggage locker holds bulky passenger cases such as Rauk's tools. Behind a removable section of the dry side lining, above the damp bilge and away from the galley and lamps, sits a compact concealed magazine for the sealed powder kegs. Grain hides the access panel from a glance, but powder is not mixed into the food or packed in ordinary grain sacks. The captain controls the hatch and compartment; who else aboard knows how to open it remains open. This design makes the concealed lot difficult to find during a manifest check, while a close structural search can find it.

**Intended transfer:** normal unloading begins with the grain. At an agreed point, Varek would bring the concealed lot out and hand it to Matěj, who arranges a covered wagon and onward delivery. Matěj need not know where it was stored before the handoff; the captain knows no final recipient. The exact wagon, helper, cover story, and what Matěj has been told remain open. If the cordon prevents unloading, the kegs remain aboard.

**First-night attempt — to develop:** Zdenda's three hired Skeld brothers board while most crew sleep and at most two patrol aboard. A Bořiči senior guard officer coordinates coverage intending no guards be hurt; Captain Hans approaches Zdenda to commission the theft and coordinates the watch opening without boarding himself. The captain alone holds the cargo-hold key. The brothers take Rauk’s equipment and selected captain’s belongings; leave bulk grain and the concealed bank lot. Other passenger valuables remain optional targets. Rauk's tools, Claire's apothecary equipment, Piere's hunting gear, and other passenger valuables need an inventory with weights, locks, locations, and carrying requirements. The brothers interrogate the captain for the key and weapons; he deliberately directs them toward Rauk's inventions to protect the bank's lot. They do not know the powder compartment. After taking his key and obtaining the misleading answer, the brothers kill Varek to eliminate a witness. This is the baseline outcome unless player intervention changes the raid. Zdenda receives the stolen haul from the brothers; their temporary storage, receiving location, raid boat, route, and handoff timing remain open. Track actual theft separately from Matěj’s belief that powder was stolen. See [the preparation questions](orestor-preparation-questions.md).

The city guard's rumor, actual powder, and Just Veil trace are separate facts. “Tupoun” Jožka, an arrested chewing addict outside Bořiči, overheard messenger Jindra “Šňůra” and Lyanna, Alynn's Imperial assistant in public. He recognizes Jindra and notices the hooded woman, but cannot identify Lyanna. He hears only fragments about a weapon and an Imperial ship, missing its name. Lyanna tells Jindra the ship's name; Jindra reports to Captain Hans, who approaches Zbožný Zdenda. Hans later warns Jindra, who hides. Torture of Jožka adds nothing. Alynn and Severin Varro know the technical description through private correspondence; Lyanna hears only the political offer and delivery discussion. See [the opening leads](orestor-opening-side-quests.md).

## Mára “Bery” Beran — stone carrier

**Origin and former life:** Mára is Meredic, born in a village on the road to Ellesmere. He drove a small goods cart into the capital and was at its edge when the Shattering struck. He survived, spent the following days hauling injured people toward improvised shelters, and now shares the wasting illness visible in many survivors. Friends call him **Bery**.

**How the stone reached him:** he acquired the wrapped violet crystal only **a few weeks before the opening**, while moving among the post-Ellesmere refugee routes. Its previous holder, the exchange, and any earlier custody are open. He recognizes its value but knows nothing of Orion, Erathon, Selith, or Jeod. The working custody chain begins at the unresolved acquisition, then runs **Bery → Varek's ship**; pin down the transfer before play.

**Why he is here:** he means to stay with family in Orestor, then quietly find someone willing to buy the stone. A sister, **Eva Beranová**, is a working family contact; her home, livelihood, and willingness to shelter him remain open. He has no arranged buyer or fixed handoff. Treatment and cash also matter. Weeks of carrying the fragment have made him watchful and possessive; he worries that officials or fellow travelers have guessed what he has. How much of that is the stone's influence remains open.

**What the players can meet:** **Mára “Bery” Beran** is a gaunt, friendly cart driver who jokes with children, remembers names, and carries a small brass harness bell. A small violet fragment, wrapped in cloth, sits in a cut-out beneath a removable boot inner sole near the arch. The uncomfortable boot makes him limp. He adjusts it privately, refuses to remove it, and is defensive when someone handles it. Ordinary baggage inspection can miss the stone; careful inspection of the removed boot can find it. He repeatedly seeks lawful release and can ask the firm for help. If freed, his first intended stop is family; address and next move remain open.

## Search and movement

Map the gangway, passenger space, cargo hold, hatch and seals, waterside access, and route from berth to land. Record who has keys and which areas city guard, harbor customs, merchants, templars, crew, and passengers can reach under each claimed authority. Test any Bořiči approach, retreat, theft, or attempt on Varek against the actual quay layout. Bery's stone changes hands only after a personal search, voluntary handover, theft of his effects, or another recorded event.

If a search finds the hidden powder, the city's political evidence changes, though possession alone proves no statutory ban was broken. If it finds the fragment, track the actual finder and custodian and use the early-discovery branch. Finding one does not automatically reveal the other. If Matěj escapes with the lot, follow his handoff; if detained, the chain stalls. If the vessel sails, record the captain's next port and whether passengers remain aboard.

## Česky — podklady pro hru

**Loď:** _The Shoalrunner_, česky **_Mělčinář_**. S Varkem brázdí říční deltu zhruba dvacet let. Jde o obchodní loď s malým ponorem pro pobřeží a dolní tok Rudé řeky. Na moři a po proudu používá plachtu. Dlouhá vesla pomáhají při manévrování; proti silnějšímu proudu posádka používá bidla nebo si najímá lidi či tažná zvířata na břehu a loď táhne lanem. Plně naložená loď tak proti proudu nepluje rychle. Při katastrofě přečkala ve chráněném říčním rameni výše proti proudu. Kapitán přišel o několik jiných lodí, které zastihla vlna na otevřeném východním pobřeží. Pro tuto cestu se počítá se zastávkami u císařské ostrovní pevnosti a v Meredii před Orestorem; loď se vyhýbá triskalským vodám. Na palubě je deset členů posádky, jedenáct navržených jednotlivých pasažérů a rodina Novotných. Zatím jsou pojmenovaní rodiče a dvě děti; dalších dětí může být víc, takže celkový počet pasažérů zůstává otevřený.

**Kapitán Varek:** samostatný, ostrý obchodník z jižního pobřeží Císařství, naproti Vilmaru přes moře. Kouří doutníky ze svinutých listů, ale pod palubou zakazuje otevřený oheň. Do Orestoru veze vlastní zásobu obilí; ve městě je po válce a přerušení lodní dopravy vzácné a dobře placené. Zná Matěje Šimka jako pasažéra, který má převzít nepřiznanou zásilku, ale **nezná konečného příjemce**. Matěj má zařídit další předání. Ví, že přebírá prach, ale nezná jeho skrýš ani konečného správce zásoby. Varek není zaměstnavatelem zbrojíře ani účastníkem krádeže jeho učednice. Pro kapitána je ve hře i jméno **Váradi Bálint**; zda zůstane Varek, ještě není rozhodnuto.

**Náklad a podpalubí:** Banka si na lodi pronajala místo pro svou zásilku; Varek podniká samostatně. Veřejný soupis uvádí jeho obilí a zásilky sukna, kování a léčiv od různých vlastníků. Hlavní poklop vede do prostoru s pytli obilí a označeným zbožím. Za odnímatelnou částí bočního obložení, nad vlhkou stokou a dál od kuchyně i lamp, je malá suchá skrýš se zapečetěnými soudky nového prachu z jihu Císařství. Obilí kryje přístup, ale prach není nasypaný do pytlů s jídlem. V Orestoru není jeho držení ani prodej výslovně zakázán; problémem by bylo zatajení nebezpečného nákladu před celníky. Alynn a další republikáni hromadí zbraně proti převzetí města loajalisty nebo monarchisty a pro posílení své snahy o kapitalistickou republiku. Přesné znění předpisů, množství, vlastník, správce zásoby a podmínky použití zůstávají otevřené. Beryho fialový krystal je jeho osobní věc, nikoli součást nákladu banky.

**Vykládka:** Nejdřív by šlo z lodi běžné obilí. Ve smluvenou chvíli by Varek vyzvedl skrytou zásilku a předal ji Matějovi, který zajistí krytý vůz a další předání. Matěj nemusí vědět, kde byla ukrytá. Varek konečného příjemce nezná. Pokud hlídka vykládku zastaví, soudky zůstanou na lodi.

**Pokus první noc — podrobnosti k projednání:** Zbožný Zdenda najme tři bratry ze Skeldu, dezertéry z valdijského námořnictva. Kapitán Hans pod Lotharem domluví akci se Zdendou a koordinuje městskou stráž, sám na loď nejde; chce zabránit zranění strážných. Většina posádky spí, na palubě hlídají nejvýše dva muži. Klíč od nákladového prostoru má kapitán. Bratři získají klíč, kapitán je nasměruje k Raukovu vybavení a potom ho zabijí, aby nemohl svědčit. Podrobnosti kořisti a dopravy zůstávají otevřené; bratři neznají skrýš prachu.

**Posádka (10):** Varek velí; Irena Sol vede záznamy o nákladu; Boran Kett je lodník a sjednává tahouny; Dalia Fen vaří a hlídá zázemí. Olek přišel o rybářskou loď, Petra Voss zná přístav, Radan zná říční mělčiny, Sorel je nový plavčík, Tavin přežil katastrofu v říčním rameni a Ysra nastoupila až v posledním přístavu. Kromě Vareka nikdo z nich nemusí znát pravý účel bankovní zásilky.
