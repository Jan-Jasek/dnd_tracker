# Orestor — opening cast and pressure

> Part of: [[Campaigns/Orestor]]
> **Draft, not canon.** Use with the [ship dossier](orestor-ship-dossier.md) for origins and secrets, the [street-level city sheet](orestor-opening-city.md) for venues and commoners, and the [no-firm first three days](orestor-sacred-timeline.md) for a counterfactual sequence. This sheet describes motives and information at the start of Day 1. In actual play, update it after every meaningful exchange.

The seizure puts several unrelated secrets in one confined place. A frightened passenger's concealment is not evidence that the guard's weapon rumor is true. Give each person a request the advocates can act on and a reason their account might be incomplete. Do not grant powder knowledge beyond an actor's explicit entry. Among the shipboard cast, Bery alone knows he carries the fragment until someone actually sees or finds it.

Alynn's defensive intention does not make a hidden powder stockpile harmless. Lothar's concern can be sincere even if his legal claim is too broad and his report is dishonest about its source. Konrad can defend lawful harbor procedure while privately maintaining a clandestine network. Oto can distrust both the Empire and Lothar's emergency precedent. These tensions keep a council hearing from becoming a simple vote for or against the ship.

**Day 1 field officers — working names:** **Captain Jaromír Zima** commands Lothar's city guards at the gangway. He demands the search on a report of an unnamed Imperial river trader; how much of Jožka's raw testimony he has seen is open. **Chief Customs Officer Anežka Koutná** handles the hail, manifest, and levy under Konrad. She refuses to give Jaromír custody of the hold, summons Konrad, and can still insist on lawful customs examination herself. Neither is merely a mouthpiece: Jaromír has to decide whether he will enforce a doubtful emergency claim, and Anežka will not protect undeclared hazardous cargo if she finds it.

## Decisions required before running Day 1

1. Vet Jaromír Zima and Anežka Koutná as the field officers. Name the Just patrol's leader and detector, and the Bořiči raiders. They are interfaces for established leaders, not extra factions.
2. Develop the magistrate and Bořiči core, then the public meeting “Tupoun” Jožka overheard, the senior officer's warning, and Jindra's reachable shelter. Follow [the numbered questions](orestor-preparation-questions.md).
3. Draw a berth plan and assign Day 1 locations, keys, seals, watch rotations, and who can reach Varek and passenger baggage. Passengers remain detained while Varek and the full crew stay aboard; decide exactly where passengers are held and how each force watches them and the ship.
4. Set the provisional review authority and hour, and the next useful sailing or unloading window. Publicly announce the deadline through notices or officials.
5. Confirm or revise Matěj's proposed knowledge, choose his next contact and instruction route, and set what would prove final delivery to the firm without the bank naming the beneficiary upfront. Choose Eva Beranová's address. Keep Alynn's reserve custodian and the final beneficiary open until the handoff is prepared.
6. Choose the Just patrol's sensing limits and Benedikt's actual concession. If the DM prefers a Hněv patrol instead, replace the Just's Day 1–2 actions and update Tillibor's knowledge; do not run both ship searches by default.
