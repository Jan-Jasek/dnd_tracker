# The Shoalrunner Incident — opening job and the firm's debt (WIP)

> Part of: [[Campaigns/Orestor]]
> **Draft, not canon.** Debt history follows the DM's account. The ship dispute, decrees, terms, and faction actions below are proposals for review.

## The firm's debt

The firm solves difficult problems for clients and is willing to take work involving coercion or unlawful interference. Its advocates decide how to handle each assignment. Public legal work and discreet services both belong to its place in Orestor.

The **[[Organisations/Legal Consultate|legal consultate]]** began on borrowed money. Establishing the business and maintaining its network of contacts and assets cost more than its early work brought in.

During the siege of **[[Places/Orestor|Orestor]]**, paying work collapsed. While **[[Factions/Triskal|Triskal]]** and the **[[Factions/United Empire|Empire]]** were allies, Triskal's naval blockade left little prospect of help arriving by sea. The firm's expenses continued: premises, staff, retainers, and the cost of keeping its network available. Interest accumulated even when clients could not pay.

The firm borrowed wherever it could to survive. Its obligations are scattered among private lenders, commercial houses, and personal acquaintances. War, political upheaval, and the destruction of **[[Places/Ellesmere|Ellesmere]]** killed or scattered many of those creditors. Some claims have surviving holders; others have uncertain ownership or incomplete records. The firm faces debts it cannot reliably total and demands it cannot easily verify.

**[[Organisations/Brauer & Hess]]**, already a client, offers to consolidate those obligations into one documented debt, with one creditor and an agreed repayment schedule. For the firm, this means breathing room and predictable expenses. For the bank, it secures an ongoing relationship with capable local advocates who know the city.

### Proposed opening offer

The bank acquires or settles claims it can substantiate, then agrees a consolidated balance with the firm. Unverified claims remain a named exception to resolve; a missing creditor's signature cannot simply be assumed. The paperwork, surviving claimants, and the bank's willingness to absorb disputed claims are possible future hooks.

Bank commissions pay a stated fee: part in cash to keep the firm operating, part credited against principal. Refusing one job leaves the agreed repayment obligation in place; it does not automatically trigger immediate ruin. Amount, interest, collateral, and the treatment of later claimants remain to be set with the DM.

>

>

The representative gives no warning of theft and does not identify the recipient. The captain knows Matěj Šimek, the passenger charged with the handoff, but not the final recipient. The advocates must ensure delivery; they are not instructed to transport the goods themselves.

The representative calls it “my employer's ship” because the bank has chartered freight space and has a financial stake in the voyage. Varek operates the vessel independently and owns its grain cargo; the bank does not command his crew.

### DM scope of the original bank contract

The assignment covers release of the ship and specified consignment, followed by ensuring delivery to the intended recipient. Release from the harbor hold is an intermediate objective. If the Bořiči steal the cargo, locating and recovering it remains part of the same assignment; there is no new hiring. This contingency belongs in DM preparation, not in the opening speech.

The bank presents the shipment as commercial business and explicitly forbids search and seizure. The working hidden cargo is new southern Imperial powder, supplied through Jeod's connections. Alynn means to stockpile it against a collapse of the city's compact or an attempt to seize power; she and other republicans are arming against a loyalist or monarchist takeover and strengthening their bid for a capitalist republic. Orestor has no express ban on its possession or sale; concealment from harbor customs creates the immediate legal and safety dispute. Define the consignment, ownership, reserve custodian, applicable declaration rules, and proof of completed delivery. Varek knows the lot is undeclared and that Matěj is to take it from his custody, but does not know where Matěj will ultimately deliver it. The [three-day working version](orestor-opening-cast.md) gives Matěj knowledge of the powder and a next contact, but not its hiding place or final custodian; confirm this before play. A possible false alarm after the first-night raid works if he does not know the hiding place.

The fragment is a passenger's personal possession, outside this contract. **Jeod learns of its arrival later** and then issues the separate fragment commission through the bank.

Precise deployment remains open. Imperial assistance weakens Triskal without requiring Imperial control of Orestor.

The merchant republicans and socialist Bořiči share opponents but have conflicting long-term economic aims. Lyanna, Alynn's assistant, is loyal to Bořiči, fancies Alynn, and uses that access to learn of the shipment. Information passes through Jindra. They know of a war-changing weapon, not powder or its location. Lyanna's origin, history, briefing, and message destination remain open.

Alynn's participation does not establish that the entire guild, Konrad, or crew knows. Decide each participant's knowledge separately. The working [ship dossier](orestor-ship-dossier.md) gives Varek his own grain cargo, a separate concealed dry compartment for the powder, and named crew and passengers, including Matěj; the final beneficiary remains open. ## Council powers — proposed model

The **Veřejná rada (open council)** makes the city's standing laws, including commercial rights and the jurisdictions of its institutions. The **Konšelé (closed council)** govern and execute those laws, with powers to issue administrative decrees and emergency declarations. Closed-council members also influence open-council votes through their guilds, clients, and political followings.

An executive decree can order action within those powers. Whether an emergency declaration permits an exception to a standing law is the contested boundary. Sponsors within the closed council claim that the ship requires immediate action; their opponents argue that the conditions for using emergency powers have not been met. Who reviews that claim, and how quickly, remain prep decisions.

The **city guard** is Lothar's police force. The **harbor guard** is Konrad's customs and port service: it administers admission, manifests, duties, cargo inspections, and harbor enforcement. The two have separate commands. These roles and the legislative/executive division are draft setting proposals.

## Four instruments behind the dispute

Working wording for player-facing extracts; titles and exact legal limits remain subject to review.

**Search powers — current discussion:** the stalemate needs a credible distinction between ordinary harbor inspection and a city-guard security investigation. Tariff exemptions alone do not establish immunity from inspection. Decide what ordinary customs has already examined, what additional powers the guard demands, why its weapon rumor does or does not justify that intrusion, and the limits of the guild's objection. A possible model is that customs checks the manifest and accessible declared freight but finds no discrepancy, while the guard demands a comprehensive security search and control of cargo and passengers. This model is a proposal; review the legal instruments and Day 1 sequence together before adopting it. Discuss the first inspection decision before expanding the legal details.

1. **Closed council — decree on Imperial commerce.** “Imperial-owned cargo is subject to an extraordinary enemy-trade levy. Goods and their carrying vessel may be held pending declaration of ownership and security for payment.” This penalizes commerce without automatically making every passenger a criminal. The levy, scope of Imperial ownership, and treatment of existing contracts need setting.
2. **Open council — harbor jurisdiction law.** “Within the harbor precinct, vessel admission, customs examination, and cargo custody belong to the harbor service. The city guard may not assume these functions under its ordinary authority.” This identifies who performs port duties; it does not authorize every possible search.
3. **Closed council — emergency substitution decree.** “Where an immediate threat to the city requires action, the city guard may temporarily assume the duties of another civic service to contain that threat.” The guard must identify the claimed threat and record its assumption of authority. Who can invoke this power and how it is reviewed remain open; the declaration is the document the party can challenge.
4. **Open council — guarantee of free commerce and lawful possession.** “Peaceful commerce and lawful possession stand under the city's protection. Beyond ordinary customs formalities, compulsory search or seizure requires stated grounds relating to an offense or immediate danger. Travelers may not be compelled to submit to questioning or detention solely by reason of foreign origin.” A levy on goods does not itself establish grounds for a security search or detention of their carriers.

Routine customs checks of manifests, declared goods, and duties remain possible. Opening sealed consignments for a security investigation is the disputed additional intrusion. The free-trade guarantee gives merchants grounds to resist arbitrary interference; it does not exempt them from all customs duties or investigation.

**Proposed passage history, for discussion:** The open council passed the harbor-jurisdiction and free-commerce laws after the coup, with Alynn's merchants and Konrad's port interests as their chief sponsors. An earlier closed council, still presided over by Jan during the siege, issued the emergency-substitution decree to let the guard assist when another service could not contain a threat; the decree has never been repealed. The current six Konšelé passed the Imperial-commerce levy shortly before _The Shoalrunner_ arrived. Lothar is a possible sponsor and Oto a possible supporter, but the votes remain open. Konrad administers the levy as dock-master regardless of his vote, and resists Lothar's separate attempt to command his officers. The sponsors, votes, dates, and the decree's precise levy remain proposals.

## The ship dispute — proposed situation

An Imperial-linked vessel receives harbor admission. The new commercial decree passed during its voyage; delayed notice to the harbor is an available complication. The exact Imperial connection could be registration, ownership, or ownership of particular cargo and needs choosing.

The **city guard identifies the vessel as an enemy-linked threat** from “Tupoun” Jožka's overheard rumor. Jožka is outside Bořiči and knows no ship name. He recognizes messenger Jindra; the other speaker is Lyanna, Lady Alynn's assistant, unknown to Jožka. The guard officer warns Jindra before he is found. Torture yields nothing more. The watch matches the arriving Imperial trader to the rumor and invokes emergency substitution to demand cargo access and questioning.

The **harbor customs service retains the berth and vessel access** under its standing jurisdiction. **Chief Customs Officer Anežka Koutná** receives the papers and begins ordinary customs inspection in the morning, before the city watch arrives. The declared freight is straightforward, and much of it is tariff-exempt; the urgently needed grain has an exemption. Assess any remaining Imperial levy on non-exempt declared trade. The bank asks the Merchants' Guild to act as its proxy, pay the remaining assessment, and insist that free passage be honored. This does not declare the sensitive concealed consignment.

**Captain Jaromír Zima**, Lothar's officer at the quay, arrives after customs has begun and demands the vessel and all cargo be confiscated into city-guard custody, the passengers prevented from leaving, and the ship subjected to an exhaustive search including dismantling where needed. Anežka refuses to surrender harbor custody and calls Konrad. Normal customs inspection continues under his authority; its completion or tariff payment does not make Lothar withdraw the cordon. Lothar regards Imperial sabotage as a genuine risk after the recent war and also seeks a precedent for extending guard control into the harbor. He invokes the emergency decree to justify powers that conflict with ordinary harbor jurisdiction and passage protections. Konrad rejects that takeover, while Alynn presses the passage guarantee and fears the damage to trade and the guild's position. A joint inspection requires agreement on scope, custody, detention, and what would secure release; those terms are the dispute.

The **Merchants' Guild represents affected owners and travelers**. Konrad offers a witnessed harbor-customs search as a voluntary compromise if the guild agrees; its advocate refuses because the guard has supplied no specific grounds to open private goods. Konrad will authorize an extraordinary security search only with the guild's consent or a closed-council execution order applying to the harbor. He continues ordinary customs formalities under his standing authority. Its members refuse consent to an extraordinary search; its advocates challenge both services under the free-trade guarantee. It demands concrete grounds for compulsory passenger questioning or detention and for opening sealed goods. The guild's practical resistance consists of owners withholding access, masters retaining keys and seals, and representatives filing a formal protest. It does not command either force; overriding that resistance would require coercion under contested authority.

Passengers are **held in practice** while the guild challenges the legality of their confinement. They have not all been arrested or individually accused. By the end of Day 1, city guards maintain the landward cordon, harbor customs holds the berth and ship, and Just templars watch for corruption; all three also watch one another. Varek and his crew remain aboard. Their presence complicates any waterside theft or abduction. Harbor admission, restraint of the vessel, cargo inspection, and personal detention are separate questions, which the competing claims attempt to combine.

The city guard claims **necessity**; harbor customs claims **jurisdiction**; the guild demands **lawful grounds and property protection**; the Just claim a **separate duty against magical corruption**. Harbor and guild cooperate against the guard's search, but are not one party: customs may inspect declared goods and seize undeclared hazardous material under its own rules, while the guild can challenge the scope of that inspection and any detention of passengers. The guild's payment on the bank's behalf answers only the Imperial-commerce decree. No single actor's success resolves every other claim.

**Council politics and actionable requests:** Konrad is defending procedure, not a political program. He says the closed council first sits **on Day 3 at noon**; Day 2 is available for separate petitions and vote gathering, with no formal council meeting. It could rule that the guard's extraordinary situation does not displace harbor jurisdiction. Alynn and Konrad support that ruling; Lothar opposes it. If four of the six Konšelé must agree, the advocates need **two of Oto, Benedikt, and Jeroným**, but none has promised a vote. Oto wants a credible response to Imperial influence without handing Lothar permanent authority over the port; he may support a narrowly limited customs order if the evidence warrants one. Jeroným presses to free unaccused passengers even if ship and freight remain held. Benedikt will not withdraw the Just's separate claim until a petitioner completes the favor he demands. Konrad wants an execution order or the guild's consent before an extraordinary harbor search. Alynn asks the firm to make Lothar withdraw his cordon; Lothar asks it to make Alynn withdraw the guild objection and may direct it toward Jindra or another witness who knows more about the shipment. All three want the templars' competing claim withdrawn and direct the advocates to Benedikt. These are separate requests, and success for one side need not settle the others. Lothar's loyalists can justify restrictions through Jan's protectorate agreement, which includes clearing Imperial influence. Lothar commands the city guard; Oto's armed household is a separate force.

The cordon is a temporary coercive fact, not a settled legal outcome. The guard can try to board, customs can obstruct it, and merchants can refuse access; none wants to take responsibility for the resulting clash. One councillor receives petitions each office day, but cannot issue a council order alone. **Jeroným receives them on Day 2 morning.** The bank, through its guild advocate in the no-firm baseline or with the firm's help in play, petitions over the blocked shipment and detained passengers. Jeroným has not been briefed by the guard or Benedikt's patrol; he asks why he is first hearing of it from a merchant petition. This gives the firm a direct audience before the Day 3 hearing. A negotiated settlement or that hearing can break the impasse.

**Three movable council votes — working terms:** Oto wants the underlying threat tested and any search restricted to named goods, harbor officers, witnesses, and a stated end time. Proof that the weapon report was inflated weakens Lothar's case with him; credible evidence of undeclared dangerous freight makes an unrestricted release harder to sell. Benedikt distinguishes his civic vote from the Just's standing claim: he can consider a narrow jurisdiction order, but will not call off the templars until his separate favor is completed. Jeroným wants a written reason for each passenger still held, prompt medical access, and a council order deciding personal liberty separately from freight. Those terms might win his vote on a limited ruling; he does not automatically endorse cargo release. The exact proposal before the council determines whether any of the three votes yes.

### The emergency — proposed political provocation

**Working rumor chain:** “Tupoun” Jožka, a chewing-addicted petty thief outside Bořiči, overhears messenger Jindra and Alynn's assistant discussing a war-changing weapon in public. He offers the rumor after a theft arrest; subsequent torture adds nothing. He recognizes Jindra but not the assistant. Captain Hans, a western Meredian officer under Sir Lothar, warns Jindra, who hides. Neither speaker knows it is powder. The familiar patrol takes Jožka to the guardhouse for a beating, where he offers the rumor. Set exact theft, patrol members, receiving officer, guardhouse location, warning route, shelter, and official report. See [the detailed questions](orestor-preparation-questions.md).

**Jindra investigation — to develop:** establish the messenger's shelter and several traces by which the guard or firm can reach him. He can identify Alynn's assistant and explain his message, but does not know the powder or compartment. The guard officer's warning and the leadership response require discussion.

A faction within the closed council wants a precedent for overriding the republicans, Merchants' Guild, and harbor service, whose interests converge around free trade. **Alynn and Konrad themselves sit on the closed council**; this is a struggle among its members and their supporters. The sponsor and participating councilors remain open.

**Working trigger:** an arrested petty thief outside Bořiči offers the weapon rumor for leniency on a theft charge. He overheard Jindra speak with an unidentified person; the guard cannot find Jindra before the ship arrives. Jožka knows neither the weapon's contents nor the intended recipient and cannot identify the arriving ship from firsthand observation. The patrol knows Jožka and takes him to the guardhouse for a beating. He offers the rumor there to bargain for relief; subsequent interrogation yields nothing more. It can claim an imminent Imperial-backed attempt to change the government without evidence that any passenger is an Imperial soldier. The report may conceal the rumor's distance from its source or exaggerate Jožka's firsthand knowledge. The closed-council sponsor sees a chance to expand the guard's emergency powers; the rumor can be exploited politically even if the sponsor did not invent it.

Distinguish the torturer and recorder of the statement, the official who endorses the emergency, and the officers who enforce it. Lothar's involvement is open: he might see the raw statement, receive only a cleaned-up intelligence report, or know how much uncertainty has been removed. Ordinary guards need not know the source or who benefits.

Give the claim evidence the advocates can examine: the runner's detention record, a polished guard report that calls hearsay “intelligence,” a possible witness to the interrogation, and the chain of Bořiči members who repeated the rumor. A suspiciously prepared demand for lasting city-guard control over port security can reveal political exploitation. Following the chain may lead toward the attempted theft. Finding gunpowder validates one part of the rumor, but does not prove the runner knew its nature or that the guard lawfully seized everyone aboard.

The prize is institutional power. A search that finds nothing need not make the sponsors withdraw: they can claim suspects escaped because customs obstructed them. The party can challenge the guard's account of the tortured runner, negotiate limits on the emergency, or force a public review. Exposing how the report was obtained and exaggerated changes political leverage even if some form of cargo settlement is still required.

## Bery and the fragment

The Bořiči's preferred night attempt targets access to the rumored weapon, potentially through Varek and portable cargo. The hidden powder stays aboard only while no one finds or moves it. **Searches are possible outcomes.** Plan a cargo search and a personal search separately, including what each can find and who receives seized property. Finding the crystal can begin a pursuit of its custodian, a custody dispute, or an early recovery offer. Keeping it from discovery until a scripted date is not required.

**Corruption — proposal to develop:** the crystal provokes possessiveness and conflict. A person who takes it might disappear with it under suspicious circumstances. Set onset, resistance, symptoms, the person's motive and opportunity to leave, and evidence of their route before using this outcome. Disappearance is not automatic: containment, vigilance, or player intervention can prevent it. Preserve actual custody even if it shortens the planned recovery job. Detection limits and search scope can give the opening room to develop without guaranteeing the stone remains hidden.

**Council hearing — likely route:** the closed council first sits on Day 3 at noon, after a full day of separate political approaches. Its session could resolve the competing civic claims and introduce Oto and the monarchists. Set what the advocates submit, what the council can decide, and whether its ruling binds the religious claim. Decide Benedikt's favor before play; he retains the Just's claim until it is completed. The earlier noble / purkrabí job remains undeveloped; a hearing need not require completing that job first.
