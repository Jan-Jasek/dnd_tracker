# Dragon Islands — Alastor's return: encounters and problems (WIP)

> Part of: [Alastor's return](dragon-islands-alastor-solo.md) | See also: [Hanser's introduction](dragon-islands-hanser-intro.md), [[Places/Saltmarsh]]
> **Session prep, not canon.** Holds only what the DM has approved, item by item. No stat blocks; the DM brings Dragonbane stats.

## Frame (approved)

- **Three sessions.** 1: Saltmarsh. 2 and 3: the road east and the mountains; the rite ends the solo line. How the road splits between sessions is open.
- **The Gannera** (_Gannera_) runs through Saltmarsh.
- **The road east**, from the bottom up: **Splav** (_Splav_), the raftsmen's and loggers' village on the Gannera and the Dwarven Company's landing, where ore and timber go from wagons onto rafts for Saltmarsh; **Ruda** (_Ruda_), high in the mountains by the mines, from which ore is carted down to Splav; **Hraničná** (_Hraničná_), the hunters' settlement highest up, the last place before Moxan land. Travel times open. What happens in each is open.
- **Lorenzo Vasari** — a Triskalan templar of the island chapters, now a renegade, leads a band on Xendra's trail; Dukas knows him. When Dukas burned the Empire's decree outlawing the Order and proclaimed atonement and protection of the people, Vasari called it heresy. He left and took two things from the chapter: its **Mlčoun shackles** (_mlčounová pouta_; Severite), made for carrying Weave users to the pyre, which would strip Alastor's half-change if they closed on him; and the **Book of Names** (_Kniha jmen_), the chapter's list, kept under the Empire, of people on the islands suspected of the Weave. Vasari means to work through it. He holds to the old ways: whoever carries the Weave belongs on the pyre. The blow the Just's authority has taken eats at him.
- **Vasari's band** — four or five: Vasari, two or three deserters, and a bounty hunter who is also the tracker.
  - **The deserters** ran from the Republic's front near Ansir and face the rope. Under a templar they pass as a holy man's armed escort, not fugitives. Vasari despises them and needs them.
  - **Wiranto**, called **Wiru** by the colonists — a light Moxan who used to catch dark ones for the slavers. He knows the mountains and Moxan ways, speaks their tongue, and reads a trail even on stone. Vasari needs him more than anyone. The dark Moxané hate him; the light ones call him a traitor for serving foreigners.
  - **The tension:** the bounty is the band's cover and income, but Vasari wants the pyre. The hirelings want gold and Xendra alive, as the warrant pays for her alive; Vasari wants her burned.
- **Vasari's head start.** He turned up in Saltmarsh as soon as the warrant was posted and set out after Xendra a day before Alastor's ship arrives. One of Dukas' men glimpsed him at the market; the watch on Hoolwatch saw the band ride east on the miners' road. Dukas knows from them. Alastor does not meet Vasari in town; he hears of him first.
- **Marie's contact** among the Moxané belongs to a clan far from Aditya's.

## Session 1 — Saltmarsh

### Dukas

Dukas learns that Alastor is heading east. He gives his information up front: what his men felt (flickers around the hospice and Xendra's house, the shadow on the east road) and that Vasari's band rode east a day ahead. He asks Alastor to bring back the Mlčoun shackles and the Book of Names. He does not need Vasari alive. The reward stays vague for now: it would certainly be worth Alastor's while.

### The squatters in Xendra's house

**Who.** Four addicts moved in after the search; the Ashen Brotherhood broke the door down to search the house.

- **Šlupka** — Covel veteran with the shakes; drinks at The Anchor, so Hanser's name means something to him. Panics at loud noise and smashes things.
- **Rezka** — young, a knife, trusts nobody. Leads the group and does the talking.
- **Kmotr** — older, a former hospice orderly. He is why they picked this house: he knows it and knows what used to be where.
- **Malej** — Rezka's brother, still a boy; keeps watch at the door.

**Pryska** (_pryska_). A yellow resin they heat in Xendra's lab glass — flasks and retorts Kmotr knows how to handle — over a candle, drawing the smoke through the neck or a reed. Xendra's laboratory is gone into their pipes; a fight in the house means broken glass everywhere.

**At the door.** The house is their first roof in a year and they fear the watch will throw them out, so anyone knocking has come to evict them. Malej raises the alarm; the door stays barricaded with Xendra's operating table; Rezka negotiates through it — threats, curses, finding out what the visitor wants. Force makes all four fight, a desperate brawl rather than a soldiers' fight. Ways in without force: Hanser's name (works on Šlupka), food or pryska, or a promise that nobody will evict them.

**What they have and know.**

- **Rezka** — the half-burned letter from the fireplace, kept for its red wax seal. She trades it, never gives it.
- **Šlupka** — the child's drawing found behind a bed. The dragon reminds him of something he saw burn at Covel; he gives it to whoever listens to him talk about Jimmy.
- **Kmotr** — came right after the search and knows what was left: all of Xendra's personal things, nothing of the assistant or the children. The ash-men "sniffed along the walls like dogs", longest in the assistant's room and in the cellar.
- **Malej** — Xendra's amulet of [[Factions/Vilmar#Vilmarion|Vilmarion]], found in her room: a silver star with a lens. He keeps it because he likes it. Whoever knows the sign sees that Xendra keeps the old faith, not Saybar's church; Nesir recognises it.

### Sněhobílý

Alastor is Sněhobílý's man. If he reports to him after the return, Sněhobílý sends him after Xendra: bring her back alive for trial, and the warrant's reward is his. It gives Alastor official cover: the right to ask questions, and the watch's help.

### Jeod

Jeod notices Alastor's withered face but says nothing this time; he marks Alastor for later. Whether he sits in on the audience with Sněhobílý is open.

### Von Telten in jail

The watch takes Telten off the ship to Kraddok's jail, where he awaits trial and almost certain execution for smuggling the weapons. Whether Alastor visits him is up to the player. Telten still believes Xendra holds his wife and children. He begs Alastor to find them; he can do nothing himself any more.

- **The description of his wife and children.** Alastor learns that the "hostage" matches Xendra's assistant, who walked freely about Saltmarsh, served beer, and talked to Hanser.
- **His wedding ring**, so that his wife knows Alastor comes from him.

### Torvald Hallsson, the guard captain

Honest and stubborn, a former gladiator and soldier, loyal to Sněhobílý since the war. He takes the warrant as an order, not as politics, and dislikes bounty hunters for the mess they make in his town. Alastor is Sněhobílý's man, which opens his door; he stays wary of him until Alastor proves useful. He has nothing to do with Xendra's notes: what he has is what the watch on Hoolwatch saw, and his door to Sněhobílý.

### Xendra's case notes

- The Ashen Brotherhood took them when they searched the house. **Dukas** keeps them and will not simply hand them over.
- They are in Vilmaran. Alastor recognises the script but cannot read it, and Dukas cannot read them either; the only other Vilmaran in town is Urian Ab Nesir, so Nesir is still needed. With the Palm on his door, which he believes the Brotherhood drew, going to read in their camp terrifies him.
- **What they say**, as Nesir reads them aloud — short clinical notes from the first day:
  - _„Pacient první: prsty černé až po druhý článek. Odumřelá tkáň se šíří od rukou. Nehoří, nehnisá. Odumírá.“_
  - _„Ostatní v krčmě: první příznaky asi šest hodin po prvním. Jen ti, kdo s nimi pili a dotýkali se jich. Vzduchem se to nešíří.“_
  - _„Tohle není nemoc. Něco podobného jsem naposled viděla, když přišli prorokoví muži, doma, ve Vilmaru…“_

### Kupferbart

Kupferbart knows his men's supply wagon took the women and children from the miners' road to **Splav** and left them there; where they went next he does not know. He protects Xendra from the gallows: an exile with a brand on his face, he knows what it is to be hunted for what others decided. He lies politely first; pushed, he turns cold and calls his men. He gives way only to proof that Xendra is in danger from her companion, not just from the warrant: the letter, the drawing, the nurse's testimony, Pepa Kudla's "hand like ice". Telten's name does not help; Kupferbart holds Telten responsible for sending her to the gallows.

### Pepa Kudla

**Situation.** Pepa lies wrapped in rags in Mára's tool shed behind the hospice. Mára, the grave digger, feeds him with a long spoon and talks to him like a dog he has grown fond of. He wants nobody to see Pepa; he knows the Ashen Brotherhood would burn the shed. He will not kill Pepa himself, and will not stop anyone who does it cleanly. The player has to decide alone and do it with his own hands.

**What Pepa says**, besides the lines in [Alastor's return](dragon-islands-alastor-solo.md): the lady hired them at night at the hospice back door, hood up.

**How the players learn of him.**

- **The hospice.** A nurse says Xendra treated the first of the three first and cut off his fingers. Next day Mára announced he had died and took the body away. There is no new grave in the cemetery; whoever checks, or asks Mára where he buried him, is on the trail.
- **The Anchor.** Hanser or his veterans: _„Mára teď nosí do kůlny dvojí porci, prej má psa. Mára nikdá psa neměl.“_

### The old seer of the Grove

**Who.** An old dark Moxan, known in town as the weirdo by the grove (_pošuk od háje_); one of the few who know the truth of the blessing of Ilimox. A little cracked as well, so his riddles read both as caution and as madness. Name open.

**Why the town lets him stay.** He was there before Saltmarsh. The Grove is an old Moxan sacred grove, and the Skeld settlers built the town around it. He refused to leave. Among the old Skeld families (the Kesters) a superstition holds that the town will not fall while he lives in the Grove; when the wave tore away half the harbour and left the Grove untouched, the belief grew. Whoever tried to drive him out would have the old families and the fishermen against him.

**What frightens him.** Marie asked him straight out how the curse of the dark ones can be lifted, and spoke as if she knew it is no curse: nobody who has only heard colonists' talk asks that. And when she touched him he felt what she is: the dark ones carry dragon blood, and he sensed in her blood that belongs to someone else. He fears that someone means to break the seal, and what that someone is. He speaks in riddles because he does not know whom to trust and will not give away how the seal is broken.

**What he says.** Not much more than this:

- _„Draci spí. Ať spí. Kdo je probudí, ten je nakrmí.“_
- _„V krvi je síla. Velká síla. Nebezpečná síla.“_
- _„Nesmí se to. Nesmí.“_

### Agáta

One of the nurses Xendra trained, nearly a physician herself, who runs the hospice in Xendra's absence. She knows two things:

- **The flight.** On the second night she saw Marie hurrying them; Xendra was terrified and kept saying she would be hanged.
- **Pepa Kudla.** Xendra treated him first and cut off his fingers; next day Mára announced he had died and took the body. There is no new grave.

### Urian Ab Nesir

Since the warrant, Nesir is the only Vilmaran left in town and under suspicion. Someone smashed his shop window and drew [[Organisations/Order of the Just#Rank & Symbol|the Palm]] on his door; it points at the Ashen Brotherhood, which frightens him more. Who drew it is open. His price for translating the notes: an interview with Hanser in the weekly to clear his name. Hanser would gladly take it — readers and votes — so Alastor only has to bring the two together.

## The road east

### Splav

Kupferbart's wagon left the women and children here. Vasari's band came through a day before Alastor.

- **Old Vondra** (_starý Vondra_) — head of the raftsmen; speaks for them all, and his word holds.
- **Vavřín** — the raftsman who would not talk; the band broke his fingers.
- **Toník** — the boy whose crushed leg Xendra tended.

- **Xendra could not help herself.** She treated a raftsman's boy whose leg was crushed between logs, and begged the raftsmen not to tell anyone about her. They cover for her.
- **Vasari got here first.** The band broke the fingers of a raftsman who would not talk. The raftsmen are now hostile to anyone asking about the woman.
- **The way on.** Empty ore wagons return from Splav up to Ruda; the women rode one. The raftsmen know which carter.
- **Sent the wrong way.** Out of spite the raftsmen told Vasari the women had been rafted across the river to the loggers' clearings. The band crossed and lost a day; on the far bank Wiru finds no trail and knows they were fooled.
- **Winning the raftsmen.** The first talk is cold. The players can win them by tending the beaten raftsman's broken fingers; by telling the truth, that they want Xendra away from a dangerous companion, not burned (the child's drawing or the letter helps); or with Hanser's name, since there are veterans among the raftsmen and the weekly is read here too. Won over, the raftsmen name the carter and stand with the players when Vasari returns; otherwise they shut themselves in.
- **Vasari returns** while the players are there, to punish the liars. He has no time to linger over the raftsmen. As an "apology" for the deceit his deserters demand a few young girls of the village, and Vasari grants them as Splav's punishment for the lie; the rest of the reckoning can wait until he comes back down with Xendra. It wins him his men's favour and their respect. With the raftsmen on their side the players have the numbers; without them they face the band alone. They can also stay out of it and simply leave before the band does.

## Open

- **The date of the rite.** Alastor's return falls in PS 1 M12; whether the rite is tied to the winter solstice is open, for a later session.

Everything else in Saltmarsh, the road (villages, order, events), and the mountains is to be decided step by step.
