# Bořiči — proposed names and short histories

> Part of: [[Campaigns/Orestor]] | [[Organisations/Bořiči]] | See [the preparation questions](orestor-preparation-questions.md) and [the opening leads](orestor-opening-side-quests.md).
> **First cast revision for discussion.** Hans and Lyanna's given names, the Judge's established role, and Ilrusk's patronage are agreed. New surnames, other names, origins, histories, and duties below are candidates until reviewed. List titles, nicknames, and aliases with names. Surnames and public roles need not reveal secret affiliations.

## Six local members and one hidden patron

### Matyáš Vorel — The Judge

**Birth:** common-born, Orestor. **Public position:** magistrate. **Private position:** revolutionary leader and Ilrusk's Veil disciple.

Current history proposal: a miller's son placed in charge of the mill's workers, he settled their disputes and continued to do so wherever he went. He has a calm, imposing presence and natural authority; people look to him to resolve problems without his needing to raise his voice. His nickname predates his judicial appointment. The city now says he can tell guilt simply by looking at someone. This is a reputation, not an established supernatural ability. His judicial career showed him how entrenched power protects itself. He believes democracy requires the Great Change and accepts destructive revolution as its price. His restraint of manner does not establish restraint of policy.

### Captain Hans Keller

**Birth:** son of a soldier or guard, western Meredia; exact legal estate open. **Public position:** senior city-guard officer under Sir Lothar. **Private position:** operational coordinator and candidate for the Judge's right hand.

Military service has been his whole life. He worked in military counterintelligence and has tortured many people. He is dangerous, experienced, and committed to cleansing the old order by fire. He could take control of Bořiči if given the opportunity. His intention that guards survive the raid is a specific operational condition, not proof of general mercy. Develop his father's service, his own postings, the cause of his revolutionary conviction, and his relationship to the Judge. He is distinct from **Sir Hans**, the former Just templar hunting escaped Ellesmere mages; no relationship is established.

**Shipment chain:** Jindra “Šňůra” brings him Lyanna’s report, including Mělčinář’s name. Hans approaches Zbožný Zdenda to commission the theft. He closely monitors investigations into Bořiči, learns in time that Jožka has named Jindra, and warns him before the watch reaches him. The privacy, payment, and terms of Hans’s dealings with Zdenda remain open.

**Independent firm approach:** Hans approaches the players on Day 1 to carry out his obligation to Zdenda. The request requires unlawful interference; an evidence-room account book, pressure on witnesses, and bribery or threats over a property purchase remain alternatives. His offer, disclosures, deadline, and fallback require development in [the Day 1 questions](orestor-preparation-questions.md#day-1-questions--hanss-independent-commission).

### Lyanna Varga — Lady Alynn's assistant

**Birth:** common-born; Imperial, specific province open. **Public position:** Alynn's assistant. **Private position:** political and commercial intelligence source.

Proposed history: her family's workshop was lost to **Brauer & Hess**, its creditors. Her command of accounts and correspondence later earned Alynn's confidence. She admires and fancies Alynn but recognizes that a merchant republic financed by the same bank can become dependent on its creditors. Her revolutionary loyalty and personal attachment conflict. She enjoys kink in her adult private life; preferences, partners, and whether Alynn reciprocates remain open. Specific province and surname need confirmation together.

**Shipment intelligence:** she overhears Alynn agreeing to collaboration with the bank representative who later hires the firm. He promises a weapon that could turn the tide of a war, names Mělčinář, and asks how to deliver it. She may conceal the Imperial bank's involvement when informing Jindra “Šňůra”. What she passes on, and whether she hears Alynn's delivery instructions, remain open; see [the source conversation](orestor-opening-side-quests.md#lyannas-source--alynn-and-the-bank).

**The bank and Great Change:** Ilrusk uses the revolutionary project to introduce democracy and remove the bank's leverage. Republican borrowing for separation can leave Orestor dependent on Isthar and Imperial interests without the republicans recognizing it. The exact debts, relationships, Ilrusk's information, and planned financial measures need development. Removing the bank does not guarantee the revolution's outcome.

### Lord Oldřich z Vřesova

**Birth:** highborn, Meredian. **Wealth:** inherited townhouse, limited ready cash. **Public affiliation and court role:** a gentleman of Lord Oto's household, handling contacts with lesser noble families and civic petitioners; publicly supports the monarchist cause. **Private position:** proposed chair of secret Bořiči meetings.

His earlier opening of family grain stores remains a history candidate, not proof of selfless motives. His court role could give him access to noble correspondence and patronage while his city contacts make him an effective meeting organizer. Develop why he turns against Oto's programme, what he expects to gain or surrender in the Great Change, and whether his generosity builds loyalty as much as it relieves suffering. His household position is agreed; the detailed relationship to Oto requires discussion.

**Darker side — proposal:** Oldřich treats assistance as a purchase of loyalty. People he feeds or protects become clients who owe him obedience. In Oto's household he can advance a supporter's petition, delay a critic's request, or withhold aid from someone who refuses to cooperate. He believes ordinary people should have a political voice, but expects gratitude and deference toward himself. His public generosity and revolutionary speeches can serve an ambition to build a personal following in the new order. Which abuses he has actually committed remains to be discussed.

### Brother Dietrich Falk — templar within Orionův hněv

**Birth:** common-born; German-inspired western Meredian origin proposed. **Public position:** Hněv templar. **Benedikt's understanding:** a trusted Just informer monitoring Hněv. **Private allegiance:** Bořiči.

History candidate: he enforced punishments and requisitions while treating obedience as moral authority. Recognition of his own complicity convinced him that the institution must lose its power. He sees Hněv as its dangerous final attempt to preserve that power. Benedikt believes him loyal and does not know his revolutionary affiliation. Benedikt requesting the infiltration remains the proposed placement story; reporting route and terms need discussion. Dietrich can supply truthful reports while working to dissolve the institution Benedikt hopes to reform. His actions under Hněv orders and the limits of his loyalty remain open.

### Bishop Edvard Lindholm — Apostolic Church of Orion

**Birth:** highborn, Valdic. **Public position:** prominent bishop within Jeroným's local church; jurisdiction open. **Private position:** proposed second meeting figure alongside the nobleman.

He grew up in exceptional luxury. The disparity between his own abundance and other people's deprivation humbled him and drew him toward charity and political change. His concern for people with little is sincere. He also has a concealed predatory sexual interest in boys. Whether he has acted on it, what others know, and what consequences or evidence exist remain unresolved. His sincere concern for the poor coexists with this concealed predatory interest. Develop his house, arrival in Orestor, relationship to Jeroným, and role at secret meetings. His knowledge of the church's founding lie is not established.

### Rewyn Ilrusk — the Weaver; public name Antonín

**Birth and ancient legal estate:** unresolved. **Public position:** librarian and volunteer in the town hospital. **Private position:** hidden patron and the Judge's Veil teacher.

**Rewyn Ilrusk** is the historical figure's name. **Antonín** is his ordinary public identity; the townspeople do not recognize the ancient disciple. Ilrusk connects to Bořiči through the Judge alone and does not attend its local meetings. The Great Change serves his attempt to introduce democracy, displace bank leverage, and preserve his own influence over Orestor. The Judge believes they share his revolutionary purpose. Ordinary recruits can mistake several local leaders for the Weaver. The Judge's knowledge of his teacher's ancient identity and the other members' understanding remain open.

## Minor contacts — name proposals

- **Jindra “Šňůra”** — minor Bořiči messenger; Šňůra is his nickname, and his surname remains open. An Orestor local with family history in the city, he owns a small house in the crowded suburbs near the port. Neighbors notice hooded visitors and infer one is a guard from exposed chain mail; they infer some are women from body size. He and his childhood friend Étienne (Eti), provisionally Étienne Durand, work in a shared port rope workshop and also fit and operate cargo rigging. They take discreet unloading jobs for Zdenda; Jindra separately sells stolen metal fittings to Václav Riedel. He takes Lyanna's shipment intelligence directly to Hans but has no view of the full command chain. Hans's urgent warning makes him obey and follow to a safehouse. He subsequently doubts Hans and considers escaping to a friend; Hans leaves a trusted city guard and Bořiči member to prevent him leaving. The watcher, provisionally Rudolf “Rudi” Seidel, is his former military subordinate who fled punishment for looting; Hans found him work and helped him settle in Orestor. Jindra is kept above a guild warehouse by reluctant keeper Vít Sýkora, a sympathizer afraid to join the movement. Age, family history, household, precise home location, and later outcome require discussion; the detailed friend, watcher, and warehouse arrangements are in the opening leads. See [the home and fate questions](orestor-opening-side-quests.md#jindras-home-custody-and-fate--open).
- **“Tupoun” Jožka** — chewing-addicted petty thief outside Bořiči, without a surname in the cast. The nickname mocks his reputation for thoughtlessness. He steals to fund his next chew and partly overhears Jindra and the hooded Lyanna at the covered riverside market. The patrol knows him and takes him to the guardhouse to beat him; there he offers the rumor to bargain for relief. He is withdrawing when visited. Exact theft, patrol members, receiving officer, and guardhouse location remain open. The duty sergeant reports his account through ordinary watch channels; Hans monitors the report and spots Jindra’s name.

## Convictions and danger

The leadership is a revolutionary coalition, not a roster of uncomplicated benefactors. Conviction, ambition, coercion, hypocrisy, personal attachments, and private wrongdoing coexist. Judge, Hans, nobleman, bishop, and Dietrich need distinct limits and disagreements. The movement's promises are not proof of what it will do after gaining power.
