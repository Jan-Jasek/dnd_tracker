# Orestor — the amethyst, separation, and shared ascension

> Part of: [[Campaigns/Orestor]]
> **Parked proposal, not active campaign preparation.** This document preserves the earlier idea of using the amethyst to separate and distribute essence through sacrificial rites. The current [Herald rites](orestor-herald-rituals.md) operate independently of the stone. Timing, bank suspicions, first-victim assumptions, and tasks below belong to this archived design and must not be used as current campaign facts. Any future fragment interaction needs fresh discussion. Current references: [Selight's motives](orestor-selith-motives.md), [the recovery commission](orestor-amethyst-commission.md), and [the timeline](orestor-sacred-timeline.md).

## Established foundations

- According to [[Cosmology]], the seal collapsed at the Shattering / Roztříštění and crystallized into fragments containing **Orion's and Erathon's essence**. The amethyst is not an ordinary gem with a spell placed inside it.
- Selight is proposed as a Veil being capable of absorbing its power under particular conditions. He intends to gather Orion through the fragments and continue his work.
- He recognizes corruption in the essence. His idea of purification need not match the actual effect.
- Mlčoun suppresses the Veil in canon. The box's precise effect on a fragment, detection, and an ongoing rite remains undecided.
- The DM's setting direction is that much advanced magic requires blood or sacrifice. Existing Maegi creation lore already connects blood, concentrated life, and Veil. The particular method below is a campaign proposal, not a universal spell rule.

## Proposed physical nature

The amethyst holds both essences in a tightly bound arrangement left by the original seal. **Influence leaking into its surroundings and complete absorption are different processes:** it can affect a carrier without being safely opened and consumed.

At close contact, Selight may recognize something familiar from Orion's work alongside an alien pressure. This grants neither a complete conversation with Orion nor omniscient memories. Range and manifestations need choosing. Direct absorption can threaten Selight's coherence and will.

## Developing combination — separation and distribution

Selight cannot contain all the power himself. He believes he can **share Orion's essence among his followers and make them demigods**, while transferring Erathon's corruption into people he condemns as betrayers. His own growth and his followers' elevation form a single vision of Orion's return.

This requires distinguishing the **source**, the **cost**, and the **recipients**:

| Role                    | Proposed function                                                                                   | Open questions                                                                              |
| ----------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Amethyst**            | Source of the mixed essence being released and divided.                                             | Access, amount transferred, depletion, and whether meaningful separation is possible.       |
| **Sacrificial donor**   | Blood, life energy, or a follower's sacrifice powers a working that Selight cannot perform unaided. | Whether death is necessary; willingness, coercion, and what the donor believes will happen. |
| **Condemned recipient** | Receives the portion Selight identifies as Erathon's essence.                                       | Method of access, onset, effect, duration, and whether intervention is possible.            |
| **Blessed recipients**  | Selight and surviving followers receive what he regards as Orion's essence.                         | Capacity, actual gifts, residual corruption, and changes to identity or obedience.          |

These are different roles. A proposed rite can sacrifice one follower and afflict a separate target who leaves alive. It need not produce the condemned person's corpse at the ritual site, and no fixed ratio between donors and targets is established.

### The follower's sacrifice — candidate method

The offered life supplies the force for separating and directing a portion of the mixed essence. Selight's existing imbuement of that follower may help him conduct the working; this is a possible explanation to develop, not a rule already established. The follower's essence pays for or enables the transfer; the unwanted Erathon essence originates in the amethyst.

Whether this requires death, blood loss, permanent depletion, or another sacrifice remains open. If death is required, decide why lesser costs are insufficient. Voluntary sacrifice is possible; cult control means apparent willingness need not establish independent choice.

Selight may consider the donor's sacrifice part of the faithful's shared elevation. His sincere affection can coexist with that demand. Do not assume the donor actually lives on in the recipients merely because Selight promises it.

### The condemned person's fate

The DM's proposed direction is a devastating affliction whose eventual outcome is largely sealed after a successful transfer, but whose form varies. Candidate outcomes include delayed death, bodily transformation, loss of control, or a persistent dangerous condition. These are possibilities, not a universal effect table.

Set the decisive point before play. If the transfer is irreversible, players need discoverable opportunities to prevent it beforehand. A later rescue might protect others, contain the affliction, or preserve the person's agency for a time without guaranteeing a cure. If interruption or treatment can reverse it, define what makes that possible. Do not decide reversibility only after players attempt a rescue.

In the timeline, record the donor's fate, transfer, target's symptoms, transformation or death, and discovery as separate events. Different delays can make the order of public incidents differ from the order of rituals.

### What the faithful receive

Demigodhood is Selight's intended gift and promise. Actual recipients might acquire powers, memories, or altered perception; precise benefits remain open. Distribution alone does not prove purification. Decide whether separation is partial, selective but imperfect, or fundamentally mistaken before its effects become evidence in play.

The shared essence might deepen followers' connection to Selight. Whether it strengthens obedience, gives them independent power, or introduces competing impulses must be chosen; these outcomes are not interchangeable.

## Custody and the opening — unresolved constraint

The opening currently places the amethyst on Milbern's person, separately from the bank's cargo, with its later movements open. **Actual transfer of Erathon's essence from this stone requires access to it.** The bank's suspicions and the stone's presence somewhere in the city do not provide that access.

Two compatible routes are available, neither chosen:

- **Preparation before acquisition:** early rites prepare donors, targets, or the cult's network. Actual extraction begins after Selight gains the stone. Jeroným's first public incident must then have another established mechanism, such as Selight's own imbuement; it cannot silently count as an amethyst transfer.
- **Access before the first transfer:** explicitly resolve how Selight gains custody or temporary access before that incident. The bank's later recovery job follows the resulting location. Do not assume Milbern hands it over or that a theft succeeds.

Remote extraction is not established. Adding it would require explicit limits and would change the significance of the bank's containment box.

## Component A — diverting corruption into another vessel

**Obstacle:** releasing Orion's share also releases Erathon's. The unwanted essence needs somewhere to go.

**Preparation:** Selight prepares vessels, potentially altered bodies or objects bound to extracted life energy. Their form, preparation, and cost are open. Canon establishes living beings as concentrations of Veil; this technique is a new proposal.

**Interaction:** with the amethyst present, he tries to accept or distribute Orion's essence while routing corruption into those vessels. Preparation can precede acquisition; actual extraction cannot.

**Observable effects:** used vessels contain a real threat. Bodily vessels could explain some abominations. Unused prepared vessels need a distinct, discoverable sign of preparation.

**Player intervention:** securing, disrupting, or rescuing prepared vessels obstructs the transfer. Taking the amethyst prevents further extraction. Destroying a filled vessel might release its contents; that consequence needs discoverable warning.

**Implication:** purification has a cost and leaves dangerous remnants. A perfect, repeatable method would have consequences for all fragments; its actual precision remains open.

## Component B — distributing power among followers

**Obstacle:** Selight alone cannot bear the fragment's strength and alien will.

**Preparation:** he deepens connections among imbued followers so they can carry the power together. Any blood or sacrifice used to establish this capacity needs a specific function. Distribution and sacrificial extraction are related tasks, not automatically the same act.

**Interaction:** power enters the connected followers and Selight in portions. The combined direction uses this for their promised elevation. On its own, distribution does not separate Erathon's essence.

**Observable effects:** after actual extraction begins, linked recipients may share fragment-specific dreams, memories, or behavioral changes. Their connection can exist before the stone is acquired.

**Player intervention:** freeing people or severing particular links reduces collective capacity. Abrupt interruption during a transfer could endanger others; safer methods must be discoverable. Mlčoun is a candidate tool for interrupting a connection, not an automatic cure.

**Implication:** the followers matter to both Selight's ambitions and his ability to bear the power. This exposes the conflict between his affection and the danger he imposes on them.

## Alternative C — reshaping Selight after Orion's imprint

**Separate alternative, not an additional requirement of the developing combination.**

The obstacle is incompatibility between Orion's essence and Selight's current arrangement of Veil. Selight alters himself using memories of his creation, seeking properties that can receive Orion's imprint. Shared origin offers a possibility, not safe absorption by default.

The role of life energy, other people, or death would need a concrete explanation. The symbolism of betrayal alone cannot establish a magical requirement.

With the amethyst present, a prepared Selight attempts to receive the recognizable Orion imprint. Selectivity and simultaneous transfer of Erathon remain open. A familiar gesture or memory does not establish purity.

Each completed preparation would produce a concrete change in Selight's abilities or manifestations. Players could discover and disrupt particular prerequisites. His earlier use of Orion's appearance would not prove prior absorption. This alternative strongly expresses his desired transformation but needs observable changes rather than an unexplained claim of greater attunement.

## Betrayal and the target list

Selecting betrayers can be Selight's emotional and religious justification. No option establishes that adultery or a broken oath creates a physically distinct essence. The magical function, accusation, and target's actual circumstances are separate design questions.

Tillibor remains a possible later target, perhaps ninth. His role awaits discussion of his character and templar origins and abilities. No roster, order, fixed count, or one-donor-per-target requirement is established.

## Containment and consequences

Decide whether the closed Mlčoun box interrupts transfer, suppresses detection, or both. Already transferred essence in people or vessels need not return to the stone when the lid closes. Suppression need not undo completed changes.

Recovery can therefore stop new transfers while leaving existing afflicted people and empowered followers in play. The exact effects must follow the selected rules rather than being invented to preserve a planned sequence.
