# Orestor — the violet seal fragment and the number of fragments

> Part of: [[Campaigns/Orestor]] | See also: [[Cosmology]], [[People/Orion]], [[Organisations/Order of Maegi]]
> **Discussion draft, not canon.** The seal's fragment count and any correspondence to the Art remain open.

The object [Bery](orestor-ship-dossier.md) carries is a **seal fragment crystallized at the Shattering**, not a gemstone formed in the earth. Its working appearance is dark violet. The color and the way it affects people can be described at the table without giving characters its true origin. In public speech it may be called a purple crystal, a jewel, or a cursed stone; none of those names establishes its substance.

Established cosmology says that the broken seal crystallized **Orion's and Erathon's soul and essence together**. The fragments are not pieces of the nine disciples. The Art has ten powers: Orion mastered them all, gave one to each of nine disciples, and kept the tenth. Maegi rings retain a degraded echo of that division. The number of physical seal fragments, the number of seals, and their relationship to the powers are still open.

## Possible structure

| Model                    | What it would mean                                                                                                                                | Strength and cost                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Nine major fragments** | The nine disciples' channels shaped where the broken seal split; Orion's tenth binding may have been spent or distributed through them.           | Echoes Selith's nine betrayers and planned deaths, but risks making his rites look mechanically tied to the stone when they are independent. It also needs an answer for Orion's tenth power.          |
| **Ten major fragments**  | The seal fractures along the ten channels of the Art: nine associated with the disciples' specialties and a tenth with Orion's reserved power.    | Makes the recurring ten meaningful and gives an unknown tenth a strong mystery. It can make the world feel like a fixed collection quest unless minor splinters, losses, and uneven power are allowed. |
| **Irregular fracture**   | The seal breaks into an unknown number of mixed pieces. A fragment can resonate with one or more Art powers without being assigned a unique slot. | Best preserves the catastrophe's disorder and freedom to add fragments later; the nine and ten remain thematic patterns rather than an exact inventory.                                                |

**Working recommendation for discussion:** use **ten major resonances** in the original seal but leave the number of surviving, recoverable stones open. Some fragments may contain more than one resonance, some may have split again, and some may be lost. This makes the Art's structure matter without requiring Selith's nine deaths or the party's search to map one-to-one to physical crystals. It remains a proposal until the seal's construction and the other known fragments are reconciled.

The violet fragment could have a **shadow affinity** recognizable to Isthar's Maegi lineage. That would explain why Jeod considers it unusually valuable or dangerous once he learns of it. Its color does **not** prove that it belongs to Jeod, contains the original disciple Isthar, or grants a defined shadow ability. Nor does it establish that all fragments have simple color codes. Birger's shadow-lord fragment already exists elsewhere in the setting; decide whether it is this same resonance, a different aspect, or evidence that the affinity model is too tidy before assigning powers.

## What the Orestor timeline needs now

- Bery carries one physical violet crystal on his person; cargo searches do not move it.
- Its actual essence is mixed Orion and Erathon. Corrupting influence and direct use are distinct; their onset and limits remain open.
- Jeod, Ilrusk, Selith, and Tillibor learn about it only through established reports, searches, or witnesses. Its arrival alone gives none of them knowledge.
- Selith's oath-breaker rites have an independent purpose and timetable. A later encounter with this fragment can change his ambitions without retroactively powering those rites.

Before canonizing a nine- or ten-fragment structure, list the other known stones, including Birger's, and determine how a seal built from ten powers could break into that number. The Orestor opening only needs the violet fragment's custody and observable effects.
