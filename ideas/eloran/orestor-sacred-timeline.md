# The Shoalrunner Incident — sacred timeline, first three days

> Part of: [[Campaigns/Orestor]]
> **Counterfactual prep, not canon.** This is what happens **if the legal consultate and player characters do not exist**. In the actual campaign the firm exists, has its history and debts, and enters on Day 1 through [the bank's offer](orestor-opening-job.md). The players interrupt this sequence; do not preserve an event once their actions remove its cause.
> **Review boundary:** The ship arrives on Day 1. The council does not meet on Day 2; its first sitting is Day 3 at noon. A Bořiči raid is possible on the first night only if the vessel remains reachable. The baseline raid takes Rauk’s equipment and captain’s valuables and kills Varek; its logistics and loot handoff remain open. The vote and Day 3 aftermath remain working proposals.

The baseline gives each faction an independent course. It is neither a script to read to players nor a prediction of their choices. **Day 1 is the arrival of _The Shoalrunner_** in **PS 2 M02**. Fix exact dates, the council review hour, and the next practical unloading or sailing window before play. The [city state and venues](orestor-opening-city.md) show pressures already in motion, the [city and ship cast sheet](orestor-opening-cast.md) records actor knowledge, and [the violet fragment note](orestor-seal-fragments.md) keeps its nature separate from the public description of a purple crystal. Each daily block covers roughly six hours: **morning 06:00–12:00, day 12:00–18:00, evening 18:00–00:00, night 00:00–06:00**. A day's night block is the early hours after its evening block.

## Starting conditions before Day 1

- Varek's familiar coastal trader, **_The Shoalrunner_ / _Mělčinář_**, approaches Orestor with grain, other declared goods, and sealed southern Imperial powder hidden in a dry compartment. He knows the compartment and the planned handoff to Matěj. Matěj knows the lot is powder in this working version, but not its hiding place or final reserve custodian.
- Lady Alynn and other merchant republicans accumulate weapons against a loyalist or monarchist takeover and to strengthen their capitalist-republic project. Lyanna passes the weapon rumor and Mělčinář's name to Jindra “Šňůra”, who reports to Captain Hans; Hans approaches Zbožný Zdenda. “Tupoun” Jožka, a chewing addict outside Bořiči arrested in the evening two days before arrival, noticed Jindra meeting a hooded woman and overheard only fragments about a weapon and an Imperial ship. He cannot identify Lyanna and heard no ship name. Hans learns of Jindra's exposure that evening, warns him urgently, and takes him to a safehouse that night. Day 1 is Jindra’s second day in hiding. A trusted Bořiči member watches him under Hans’s orders to prevent him leaving, using force if necessary. Jindra doubts Hans’s intentions and considers escaping to his childhood friend and coworker. After a few more days he may escape or Hans may arrange to kill him first; that later outcome remains open and depends on player actions. Torture of Jožka adds nothing. Alynn and Severin Varro know its nature through private correspondence; Lyanna hears only the political offer and delivery discussion. Her meeting with Jindra takes place under the covered riverside market shortly before closing on an evening before the ship arrives; the exact interval remains open. See [the preparation questions](orestor-preparation-questions.md).
- Bery carries a **violet seal fragment** on his person. He acquired it weeks earlier and intends to stay with his sister Eva, then seek a buyer. The bank, Ilrusk, Selith, and Tillibor have no lead to the stone at the start.
- The closed council has Alynn, Oto, Lothar, Konrad, Benedikt, and Jeroným. Purkrabí Jan is a hostage and cannot preside. A Just patrol is the working religious intervention; Hněv's direct ship search is an alternative to develop separately, not an additional default patrol. Kate is elsewhere on Jeod's business; he has no reason to summon her yet.
- Outside the quay, Jitka Křížová helps newly gifted Bořiči commoners control their magic. Einar Halden keeps an old shrine and tells a disputed legend of Orion. Fenris's informants and ordinary trade clerks observe different pieces of harbor traffic; none starts with knowledge of Bery's packet.

## Day 1 — arrival and detention

**Morning (06:00–12:00) — arrival.** Konrad's harbor launch hails _The Shoalrunner_ in the approach channel, records Varek's Imperial connection, declared freight, and passengers, and directs him to a berth where the newly enacted levy can be assessed. Varek first hears of the decree at this hail. **Chief Customs Officer Anežka Koutná** takes the papers; harbor admission gives her service control of the vessel and ordinary cargo process, but does not clear the goods for sale. Anežka begins ordinary customs inspection immediately, before the city watch arrives. The declared cargo is straightforward and largely tariff-exempt, including the urgently needed grain; only any non-exempt freight receives the applicable levy. The city watch has orders to look for an Imperial river trader, not a ship name. Once _The Shoalrunner_ has tied up, **Captain Jaromír Zima** treats it as the likely match, posts a landward cordon, and presents Lothar’s emergency claim to confiscate the ship and all cargo into guard custody, halt the passengers, and search the vessel exhaustively, dismantling where necessary. Anežka refuses him access to the hold and summons Konrad, who backs her, retains ordinary customs authority, and requires payment of any remaining levy on non-exempt freight before its clearance. No merchant advocate or templar has arrived yet; no passenger is individually accused. Jeod learns nothing about Bery's crystal from the hail.

**Day (12:00–18:00) — levy and competing claims.** The bank asks the Merchants' Guild to serve as its proxy. The guild pays any remaining Imperial levy on non-exempt freight on the bank’s behalf and demands that free trade be honored. Konrad now lacks a levy-based reason to hold the declared freight. He offers an extraordinary harbor-customs search if the guild agrees; it refuses. Konrad will authorize that search only on guild consent or a closed-council execution order, while routine customs powers remain his. He points to the council's first sitting on Day 3 at noon and a possible ruling that the emergency does not displace harbor jurisdiction: Alynn and Konrad would support it, Lothar would oppose it, and two more of Oto, Benedikt, and Jeroným would be needed. Jaromír keeps the city-guard cordon and seeks confirmation from Lothar; customs keeps the vessel and hold. In the afternoon a Just patrol detects a diffuse Veil trace and cites an old church edict granting it authority to investigate magical corruption. Jaromír refuses church questioning inside his cordon, and Anežka refuses church access to the ship. The seekers cannot identify a person or object. The guild, guard, and customs each have reason to approach Benedikt about withdrawing the templar claim.

**Evening (18:00–00:00) — stalemate.** The ship remains in harbor custody; city guards hold the landward exit; Just templars watch for corruption. All three forces post overnight shifts around the detained passengers and watch one another. The Just retain their separate legal claim. No passenger has yet been singled out as the cause of the trace. Alena Novotná asks for care for Ondřej, Claire offers treatment, and Rauk demands a witnessed inventory of the tools left aboard; these requests do not settle custody. Varek and his full crew remain aboard under harbor observation. The guild maintains its protest and the bank's cargo cannot be handed off. Konrad and Alynn want the templars called off, while Lothar wants their competing claim removed too. The Bořiči can observe the water approach, but a ship with its crew aboard and three alert forces ashore is harder to take. The day closes without a settlement.

**Night (00:00–06:00) — possible hired raid.** If the ship remains reachable, Zbožný Zdenda's three Skeld brothers board through an opening coordinated by the Bořiči guard officer. Harbor and Just shore watches remain separate. Most crew sleep; at most two patrol aboard. Set how those men are handled and how the captain's cargo-hold key is obtained. The brothers take Rauk’s equipment and selected captain’s belongings, leaving the grain and concealed bank lot aboard. Other passenger valuables remain optional inventory candidates. The captain deliberately points the brothers to Rauk's mechanical inventions, crossbow mechanisms, new alloys, and Veilbreaker to protect the bank's lot; Claire's tools, Piere's equipment, and other valuables need a full inventory. After obtaining the key and Varek’s misleading answer, the brothers kill him to eliminate a witness. Exact boarding, carrying capacity, magical aid, other targets, and retreat remain open. The officer intends no guards be hurt. The brothers do not know the powder compartment. Zdenda receives the haul from the brothers before the supposed weapon goes to Hans. Whether they first store it at their own hideout or deliver directly, and whether the handoff is that night or later, remain open pending berth, hideout, receiving location, and boat choices. Track actual theft separately from Matěj’s belief, and adapt to player interventions.

Bery repeatedly asks for lawful release but makes no escape attempt in this baseline: flight would draw a personal search to the fragment hidden in his boot. Lukáš watches the service exit and considers slipping out during a shift change; the three watches make him wait. A party-created gap could change either choice. Selith’s followers continue their separate Herald activity elsewhere in Orestor.

## Day 2 — theft investigation and political pressure

**Morning (06:00–12:00) — petitions and leads.** The first-night raid has taken Rauk’s equipment and selected captain’s belongings; the concealed bank lot remains aboard unless an intervention exposed or moved it. The brothers have murdered Varek after questioning him and taking his key. Harbor customs secures the ship, and the city guard investigates the theft and any deaths. Specific crimes provide grounds for a focused joint search without settling authority over the port in general. A thorough search can discover the concealed lot. Jaromír keeps the city-guard cordon; his original weapon intelligence remains Jožka’s secondhand account. Lothar orders a search for Jindra “Šňůra” or the unidentified speaker; Alynn's guild prepares its challenge to the emergency declaration and asks for the detention record. Konrad makes clear that ordinary customs work continues, but an extraordinary security search requires guild consent or a closed-council execution order. The bank's guild advocate petitions Jeroným, the one councillor receiving petitions this office day, on the bank's trade loss and the passengers' confinement. He is surprised that neither the guard nor Benedikt's patrol briefed him. He takes the petition but cannot issue a six-member execution order alone. The Just's vague Day 1 report goes to Benedikt. The passengers remain held, despite the guild's demand that unaccused travelers be released; officials may consider individual medical leave, but there is no general release order. Ondřej's fever and Růžena's illness make delay costly. The bank's consignment cannot be handed to Matěj while the ship is held.

**Day (12:00–18:00) — separate audiences, no council sitting.** Alynn seeks votes to preserve harbor jurisdiction and release the passengers. Lothar seeks support for an emergency search and works his rumor source. Konrad will accept a council execution order or the guild's consent, but will not surrender the port to the watch. Benedikt keeps the Just's claim in place and tells petitioners he will withdraw it only after they complete a separate favor that would anger Tillibor; its exact task remains open. In actual play he can offer that job to the firm. In the no-firm baseline, no petitioner completes it today. Jeroným is not invested in the freight dispute, but presses for the release of travelers against whom nobody has stated an individual charge. Oto distrusts both an Imperial-linked shipment and Lothar's claim to take over the harbor. He withholds a commitment while asking what evidence supports the threat and what limits a proposed order would place on either side.

**Evening (18:00–00:00) — the cost of delay.** Each camp counts possible votes for the Day 3 noon sitting and circulates its preferred account of Jožka's statement. Without a fresh agreement, the harbor keeps the ship, the guard keeps its cordon, and the Just keeps its legal claim. Passengers still need food, water, care, and a place to sleep; ordinary requests to leave become leverage in the arguments over detention. The crew’s circumstances depend on the raid and subsequent investigation; Varek is dead in the baseline, and his murder is part of the investigation. Develop how Bořiči and Zdenda assess the stolen equipment, and whether Matěj mistakenly reports the bank lot stolen.

**Night (00:00–06:00) — aftermath to develop.** Establish the factions’ next moves from the first-night theft and Varek’s murder, and the findings of any Day 2 investigation. No second raid is scheduled in this baseline.

## Day 3 — first council sitting, to vet

**Morning (06:00–12:00) — investigation follow-up or mounting costs.** If the raid killed Varek or others, harbor customs and the city guard continue their investigation of the deaths. They can conduct a joint, logged search on specific criminal grounds without settling who commands the port in general. The first pass can cover bodies, crew quarters, the baggage locker, and visible cargo; a deeper structural search could discover the powder. A fresh Veil trace might bring the Just back with stronger grounds. If there was no raid, the original legal dispute persists and the continuing detention strengthens Jeroným's call to release unaccused travelers. Bery remains among those held unless someone grants him an individual release; a later personal search could find his packet.

**Day (12:00–18:00) — closed council's first hearing.** The six Konšelé finally sit with a Day 2 record of petitions and, if the raid occurred, the investigation record of theft or killings. Alynn and Konrad favor protecting harbor jurisdiction; Lothar presses for emergency guard authority. Oto's vote depends on the evidence and limits placed on the proposed order, not on a settled alliance with either camp. Jeroným asks the council to decide the passengers' status separately from ship and freight custody. Benedikt has not withdrawn the Just's claim merely because the council convenes; in this baseline his demanded favor remains undone. The council can now issue a narrow execution order, refuse one, or fail to reach four votes. Its civic ruling does not automatically settle the church claim.

**Evening (18:00–00:00) — consequences of the hearing.** Record the order actually made, any separate passenger release, the scope of lawful searches, and who may board or unload. If no four-vote ruling passes, custody remains contested and the factions must decide whether to continue the hold, negotiate a narrower order, or risk unilateral action. Release Bery only if the actual order or an individual decision permits it. Build later events from the raid result and this hearing, rather than fixing a disappearance or transfer in advance.

## Using this after actual play

The **real** firm exists and receives the bank's offer on Day 1; [three separate leads](orestor-opening-side-quests.md) can take it toward Jindra, Oto, and Benedikt. As soon as the players act, replace affected baseline events with what occurred at the table. Rebuild each faction's next move from its surviving resources, information, and priorities; do not force Varek's abduction, the council's compromise, Bery's release, or any later hunt. Record physical custody, separate beliefs, official orders, and outstanding obligations at each session end.

The bank's later [fragment recovery commission](orestor-fragment-commission.md), Tillibor's possible [investigation of an independent Herald attack](orestor-herald-rituals.md), and a candidate council-seat vote begin on their own evidence and dates. Selith's intended nine deaths echo the nine betrayers, but their schedule, targets, and mechanism remain separate from the number or custody of seal fragments. Set their deadlines on the campaign calendar when those threads are prepared.
