# Orestor — sacred timeline (WIP)

> Part of: [[Campaigns/Orestor]]
> **Draft, not canon.** Companion to [the seized-ship opening and task list](orestor-opening-job.md).

A day-by-day sheet of **what happens in the city if the players sleep**. After play, rewrite the next day from what they did.

## Scope

Track threads with their own triggers:

1. **Ship opening:** debt consolidation and the bank's assignment to secure release and delivery of its consignment, including recovery if stolen. Bery may carry the amethyst personally; the bank's cargo contract does not concern it.
2. **Amethyst hunt:** an incident, still to be chosen, alerts competing hunters. Isthar commissions recovery through the bank; Ilrusk's Mlčenliví and Selith's followers also pursue it.
3. **Herald rites and investigation:** the cult asserts its presence and punishes perceived oath-breakers independently of the stone. Jeroným's proposed square incident may prompt a separate commission from Tillibor.
4. **Council vote (candidate job):** the firm may be asked to influence an open-council vote filling a closed-council seat, potentially Jeroným's if his death leaves it vacant. Patron, candidate, method, electoral procedure, and date remain open.

Use relative triggers until dates are chosen. The bank offer and the square incident have no required shared day or order. Selith's working plan is nine deaths echoing Orion's nine betrayers, on an intended schedule whose dates and intervals need setting. The hunt and council vote have their own deadlines. Record what factions intend to attempt, not guaranteed outcomes.

**Opening baseline:** Alynn knowingly participates in Imperial support carried as undeclared powder from the Empire's south. ## Stone column

Track, each day:

- **Where the stone actually is**
- **Who physically carries it**, and what event could change custody
- **Who knows it entered**
- **Who is hunting**, and **where they think it is**
- **How they learned**, and what evidence the players can find
- **Containment:** where the bank's box is, who carries it, whether it contains the stone, and whether it is open, locked, or damaged. Apply only the suppression properties established for play.

Desired opportunity: it spends some time with someone **who is not hunting**, allowing the party to investigate before an organized faction secures it. This depends on actual custody; do not force a transfer to preserve the hunt.

If the party secures the stone early, adapt the bank offer and hunters' actions to that result.

## Discovery incident and faction knowledge

The hunt begins when an incident provides actionable information about the stone. The bank's proposed lead is a description of the opening passenger seen with the crystal, recognizable to the party as Bery. The sighting, reporter, and route to Jeod still need defining; the other hunters need their own sources. The [recovery commission](orestor-amethyst-commission.md) records these questions.

| Actor                  | Information to establish                                                                                                                                                |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Bank / Jeod**        | What report arrives after the stone enters Orestor, its reliability, and why it prompts recovery. The bank has no knowledge linking the stone to the rites.             |
| **Party**              | The bank's identifying lead, or an earlier discovery in play. Meeting Bery does not itself identify his possession.                                                     |
| **Mlčenliví / Ilrusk** | How Ilrusk learns, what he orders, and what Konrad or other agents know.                                                                                                |
| **Selith / Heralds**   | How they learn and recognize a fragment, and who can pursue it while other cult activity continues.                                                                     |
| **Hněv, if involved**  | Its own information trigger and what it actually identifies. A shipboard intervention remains optional; Tillibor's investigation brief alone does not reveal the stone. |

Record separate times for physical arrival, the incident, each report, and each faction's response. Hunters can hold incomplete or wrong information. None automatically knows the current custodian when custody changes.

## Independent cult actions

The [Herald rites](orestor-herald-rituals.md) establish the cult's presence and punish perceived broken oaths or vows. Their beginning and continuation do not require amethyst arrival, possession, or extraction. Their mechanism and reason for acting now remain open.

**Jeroným's proposed square incident** belongs to this thread. Prepare the threat, evidence, and possible interventions before scheduling it. If he dies, Tillibor may approach the firm about who caused his transformation and death. The commission has its own terms and does not require the bank job to be active or finished.

Record preparation, intended target, cult resources, warning signs, attempt, outcome, and public response against Selith's intended nine-death schedule. If a chosen method separates a rite from later symptoms or death, track those separately. Interrupted rites and rescued targets change his options; any substitution or delay needs a defined cost and method.

## Consequences across threads

- **Party secures or delivers the amethyst:** update custody, hunter actions, containment, and debt. The independent rites can continue.
- **Party exposes or interrupts the rites:** update surviving targets, cult resources, and faction responses. The amethyst remains with its actual custodian.
- **Selith obtains the stone:** his acquisition creates a new opportunity; any use or absorption needs defined mechanics. It does not retroactively explain earlier rites.
- **Hunters and investigators encounter the same people:** allow connections supported by evidence. Selith's involvement in both threads can emerge without making one case a prerequisite for the other.
- **Party declines or delays a job:** advance each faction only through its available information and means. Leave leads and consequences the party can discover later.
- **Jeroným dies:** his vacant council seat and a possible Kajícníci response require their own political follow-up. If play prevents the death, update those plans accordingly.

At each session end, carry forward actual custody and completed events, then revise pending actions. The older [amethyst-powered ritual proposal](orestor-amethyst-ritual.md) is archived and supplies no active timing or dependency rules.
