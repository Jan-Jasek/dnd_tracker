# Dragon Islands — Alastor's return to Saltmarsh (WIP)

> Part of: [[Campaigns/Dragon Independence]] | See also: [[Places/Saltmarsh]], [[Factions/Vilmar]], [[People/Jeod Isthar]] | Table intro: [Hanser's introduction](dragon-islands-hanser-intro.md)
> **Discussion draft, not canon.** Solo/duo follow-up played in Dragonbane. Records agreed answers and a parked queue.

## Frame

- **When:** around **PS 1 M12**, right after Act I of Dragon Independence. The same player then plays [[Campaigns/Orestor]] with other characters. Dragon Islands part 2 follows after Orestor.
- **Who:** Alastor returns to Saltmarsh alone. One extra player may join, from outside the original group.
- **Two threads.** The main line is the hunt: **hunt → truth → peak**. The crystal thread (Matyáš, the templars, the plague) runs in the background in Saltmarsh and lays the foundations for a crystal hunt — possibly a second act a few weeks after Alastor returns, and part 2.

## Alastor

Sixty-year-old bounty hunter. Searching for a cure for his wife, he reached the [[Factions/Vilmar|Vilmar Sultanate]] and one of [[Factions/Vilmar#Prophet Saybar|Saybar's]] blood mages. The mage began turning him into a disciple and told him to feed his wife his blood after the change. She died of it. The ritual did not fully take: Alastor is half-changed, half his face withered. He hunted the mage for years, gave up, and came to the islands for a new life. There he ran into Saybar's people again and took it as a sign to pursue vengeance. He knows he must grow stronger before he can stand against them.

## The Truth (agreed)

- **Marie von Telten** (_Marie Teltenová_) — Meredian-born, married into the German noble Von Telten family — is a **Požehnaná**, made by Saybar while Telten served on the Sultanate border. Only Saybar creates Požehnaní. She planned the islands operation with **Hilua**.
- **Telten is innocent.** His family lived in comfort on **Meros**. After the wave he believed them dead, until a **Požehnaný**, **Zahir Nuamet**, came aboard claiming they were held hostage. He gave in, smuggled the weapons aboard the **_Sea Ghost_**, and could not search for his family because the Požehnaný stayed aboard. There were no letters: the Požehnaný spoke by magic with someone he called Xendra and relayed messages from Telten's wife that rang true, and at landings Telten learned that Saltmarsh had a councilwoman Xendra, who had come to town with a woman and two children matching his family. When the party took the ship the Požehnaný escaped; Telten told them all this.
- **Marie hid in Saltmarsh** under a false name as a refugee widow with the children, living in **Xendra's** house and working as her assistant.
- **Xendra is innocent.** Vilmaran-born physician (_lékařka_), in Saltmarsh only a short time; a good woman who cared for refugees and the wounded through the war and wanted to make the town better. She won the council's health and quality-of-life seat. The Požehnaný named her as the captor to point at her — a public figure, and Vilmaran.
- **The Saltmarsh incident** (see [[Places/Saltmarsh#The Incident]]). Marie, sensing something powerful, sent three hirelings to mug Matyáš. He fought them off; they sickened, drank the night away at The Snapping Line, and by morning several people there showed necrotic symptoms. Xendra was called and was mortified. Sněhobílý called in the Ashen Brotherhood, whom he had turned away a few days before; they burned the tavern with everyone inside. Option: the men went home to the Crab Cliffs instead, and the Brotherhood burned part of the poor quarter.
- **Marie's way inland.** She volunteered to treat and help the Dwarven Company's miners, which took her into the mountains toward Kanbar and let her make contact with the local natives. Hilua and Marie each worked their own angle and knew little of each other's details; Hilua's was Aditya's group near Dras Wyn, three weeks' travel from Saltmarsh.
- **The flight.** Marie fed Xendra rumours that accusations were gathering against her and that Sněhobílý meant to use them to eliminate her as opposition, perhaps staging something to make it convincing, and proposed escaping to the mountains, where she knew someone who would hide them. In the nights after the incident Marie tried again and again to reach Zahir by magic and got no answer; she judged it time to vanish. On the second night after the incident, the night after the Ashen Brotherhood came into town, Xendra fled with her; they left on foot at night — carts do not travel the roads in the dark, and wheels leave an easy trail. Marie keeps Xendra alive on the road: a dead Xendra in town would raise questions, a vanished one confirms her guilt.
- **The destination** is a sacred site on a peak in the **Ganner Mountains near Kanbar**, east of Saltmarsh: a native mountain-top city, shrine, or ruins the natives are forbidden to enter, tied to dragons, half-dragons, or draconic magic. Marie means to use her children in a rite there.
- **The rite** inverts the seal Orion's mages laid on the peak over the dark natives' dragon blood (see [[Places/Dragon Islands#The Blessing of Ilimox]]), powered by the children's old blood. It would return the dark their power. They would be grateful to Marie, and Vilmar becomes their ally; the natives take back their land, the colonies fall into chaos, and Vilmar gains its base. **The dragon under Meros is a separate story**, Finnigan's — a handful of Požehnaní could not control it.

## Saltmarsh When Alastor Returns (agreed)

- **The council** (six seats by declared focus) governs both town and state: Gellan Primewater (trade, mayor), Sněhobílý (war), Manfred Kupferbart (dwarf; mining and smithies; Dwarven Company mines toward Kanbar), Edwin Kester (Skeld old families), Ondřej Andr (Meredian newcomers), and Xendra's empty health seat, which [[People/Hanser|Hanser]] runs for. Gellan negotiates with Sněhobílý to split the executive powers.
- **Von Telten is a prisoner.** The watch takes him off the ship to Kraddok's jail, where he awaits trial and almost certain execution for smuggling the weapons. Whether Alastor visits him is up to the player. **Sněhobílý** has put an official bounty on Xendra, so other hunters are on the trail.
- **Hanser and part of the town believe Xendra was framed** by Sněhobílý. They are right that she was framed, wrong about who did it.
- **The surviving thug.** **Pepa Kudla**, one of Marie's three hirelings, the first to show symptoms and the first treated. **Mára**, the hospice grave digger, keeps him hidden and feeds him; nobody else dares go near. He is still alive when Alastor arrives: the necrosis has taken most of his fingers, his ears, his lips. He asks to be killed in exchange for what he knows:
  - _„Říkala, že je to obyčejnej kořen. Udělit mu lekci, okrást ho, naučit ho, jak se chovat k dámě. Kurva. Obyčejnej kořen. Nikdy jsem nic takovýho…“_
  - _„Měla ruku jako kus ledu… vzpomínám si, že jsem klukům říkal, že mít ji na seně, tak si ho radši vyhoním vlastní… eh… bolí to.“_
- **Marie worked alone in Saltmarsh.** Anyone she left behind could give her away if caught, and she leaves no witnesses. She did have dealings with the head of the miners, **Manfred Kupferbart**, the dwarven councillor of the Dwarven Company.
- **Hanser felt something wrong about Marie** — cold hands, a gaze that went through him — and cannot explain it; he puts it down to drink. He and Xendra were friends.
- **The Just** — the island chapters, outlawed by the Empire after the Shattering, cut off from the mainland, rebranded under new leadership, and offering their services to the Republic to prove themselves against real threats — sent a squadron after the incident. Lost and insecure: since the wave they sense the Weave in far more people. They are the **Ashen Brotherhood** (_Bratrstvo popela_; behind their backs _Popelníci_, _Popeláři_), led by **Valerián Dukas**. They sense active Weave use clearly and its **Weave shadow** for hours afterwards; passive Weave drowns in the noise except for powerful users and strong artefacts like the crystal (the player does not know this). Alastor at rest probably passes unnoticed; using his abilities leaves a trail. With Jeod at court, Sněhobílý has two rival advisors who want each other dead. They are in town when Alastor arrives. [[Organisations/Orion's Wrath|Orion's Wrath]] belongs to Orestor and does not appear here.
- **Jeod Isthar** arrived a few days after the incident on Finnigan's trail, offered Sněhobílý his services, and became his advisor (how exactly is open), eager to shape the coalition his way. Nobody in town knows he is a mage. Alastor can meet him there. Jeod's control of the world through [[Organisations/Brauer & Hess]] debts stays hidden for Orestor; he travels by the Paths.

## The Crystal Thread (agreed)

- **One crystal**, an Orion–Erathon fragment tied to corrupting bodies and raising the dead; a different fragment from Orestor's.
- **Matyáš** (working name), son of a Meredian family from **Covel** who moved to Ansir during the war. He found the crystal after the tsunami on the coast around Covel — perhaps simply fished it out, though some drama is welcome.
- Two of Rotgar's men robbed the refugee caravan Matyáš travelled with and fought over the crystal. It sickened them, and the sickness spread to the room at Dirden's farm where they were sealed in. The crystal never reached the farm; Matyáš took it back.
- The party met him on the road as they set out from Saltmarsh at the start of Act I: alive, himself, unnaturally likable (Wisdom save or charmed), heading to Saltmarsh to sell the crystal for a new life.
- He spent about six weeks in Saltmarsh while the party was away; what he did in the first weeks is open. He fought off Marie's hirelings and fled south toward **Oros** that same night. The town knows he was mugged and vanished. The description of the young man the Brotherhood wants reminds Alastor of the rider.
- **Perin and Charles** carry a mild form of the plague from the sealed room — a punishment for meddling, not the smugglers' fate. It gives Alastor a second reason to chase the fragment.

Since then messengers from Ansir, sent ahead while the _Sea Ghost_ is repaired, have brought Telten's testimony and a warning about Xendra, and announced the party's return; After her flight, on the Brotherhood's report of the Weave around her, Sněhobílý first issued a warrant for her in connection with the sickness; when the messengers came, he issued a second warrant, for high treason, the same day. The Ashen Brotherhood searched Xendra's house first, looking for the source of the sickness because they had felt the Weave around her; they broke down the door and took her papers. When the warrant came there was nothing left to search. Since then four addicts have squatted in the house and defend it as their own.

## The Road East (in discussion)

> Encounters, problems, and village clues: [encounters draft](dragon-islands-alastor-encounters.md).

- **Length:** three sessions, following **hunt → truth → peak**: Saltmarsh, the road east, the mountains. The rite ends the solo line.
  **The road east:** from Saltmarsh east to **Splav** (_Splav_), the raftsmen's and loggers' village on the Gannera and the Dwarven Company's landing, where ore and timber go from wagons onto rafts for Saltmarsh; then up to **Ruda** (_Ruda_), the mining camp, from which ore is carted down to Splav. From Ruda, **Hraničná** (_Hraničná_) lies northeast, around the mountain and lower than Ruda, in the forest where the hunters work: cleaner and quieter than the mines, and the last settlement before Moxan land.
- **Travel times:** Saltmarsh → Splav one day on foot, a long half day by wagon; Splav → Ruda two days of climbing, with the empty ore wagons; Ruda → Hraničná half a day to a day; beyond, two to three days through Moxan land to the peak (rough).
- **Kupferbart's workers** took the women and children from the miners' road to Splav (see the clues).
- **On the road (agreed directions):**
  - _Xendra cannot stop being a doctor._ She treats someone on the way and is remembered kindly; people also remember Marie pulling her away.
  - _Kuba's trap_ in place of an ambush: the miners are hard men, not murderers. Xendra, afraid of the Požehnaní, begged people not to talk about her; Marie asked Kuba, a young miner whose hand she saved, to lead any pursuers off the trail or get rid of them, and he leads the players into the drake's den (see the encounters draft).
  - _Bounty hunters_ after Sněhobílý's reward, with a renegade templar among them, or led by one.
  - _A monster:_ a small, stunted lizard-drake.
  - _The Ashen Brotherhood_ follow the Weave shadow east; Alastor meets them at some point.
- **Marie's contact among the Moxané** — the **Sisik** clan, far from Aditya's: its young chief, who explored the ruins near the summit himself and let Marie in for weapons, without the rest of the clan knowing (name open). Direction: word of Hilua's dealings with Aditya has spread among the natives, and Marie can prove she comes from the same source.

## World Rules Established

- **Seals and breaches:** see [[Cosmology#Seals and Breaches]]. Jeod knows several seals and their inversions; exactly how Orion's Seal was destroyed is unknown.
- **Požehnaní** (the Blessed) are Saybar's blood mages; only he makes them; costly and rare; empowered since the Shattering.
- **Fragments are pure Weave** — the essence of Erathon and Orion, perhaps of the disciples too. Rituals do not need them.
- **Native clans** each have their own shamans and natural sources of power; Vilmar's plan had several entry points among them.

Keep the mechanics abstract. Open: whether he sickens once separated from it, and the slow lich-like change.
