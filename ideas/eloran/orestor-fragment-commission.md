# Orestor — the bank's fragment commission (WIP)

> Part of: [[Campaigns/Orestor]]
> **Draft, not canon.** The DM's proposed commission, with dialogue and preparation questions. Follows [the seized-ship opening](orestor-opening-job.md); timing and custody live in [the sacred timeline](orestor-sacred-timeline.md).

## The offer

**[[Organisations/Brauer & Hess]]** commissions the consultate to find and recover the violet seal fragment. **[[People/Jeod Isthar|Jeod]]** learns of its presence through an incident and information route still to be chosen. The ship commission concerns a separate, undeclared powder shipment; this assignment follows the bank's discovery of the stone, independently of any ritual attack.

The bank does **not know of a connection between the fragment and the rituals**. Its brief contains no murder investigation. Delivery in the supplied case earns cancellation of the firm's consolidated debt.

### Proposed dialogue

> “My employers have learned that an unusual dark-violet crystal has been brought into Orestor. They want you to locate it and bring it to us.”

The representative describes **a passenger the party encountered during the ship job**, reported seen with the crystal. In the working opening this is **Bery**. Write his recognizable features when developing that scene; the party should be able to connect the description to someone it met. The bank need not know his name.

The representative places a metal box on the desk and opens it. A lining of **Severite / Mlčoun** lies beneath dense foam fitted into both halves. Closing the lid presses the contents firmly in place.

> “Its origin is… questionable. Do not touch it directly. Do not wear it, use it, or carry it loose. Put it inside this box, lock it, and bring it to me. Keep it closed.
>
> “Deliver the crystal in this box, and the debt you owe this house will be erased.”

**Working lead:** a sighting of that passenger with the crystal. Who saw him, where and when, how the report reached the bank, and how the crystal was identified remain open. The report establishes possession at the sighting, not his current location or custody.

**Time pressure:** the carrier or the next useful lead may leave the city soon. Define an actual departure opportunity, intention, and discoverable warning when preparing his movements. Do not force a departure merely because the party pursues another job. The bank expects prompt results, while the firm's debt makes refusal or prolonged delay difficult to afford; exact reporting expectations and financial consequences need setting.

## Terms and knowledge

- **Scope:** locate, contain, and deliver the stone. The separate [investigation commission](orestor-herald-rituals.md) has its own client, evidence, and terms.
- **Written settlement:** identify the consolidated principal, interest, and bank-held fees being discharged. Claims outside the consolidation need explicit treatment; the bank cannot erase an unrelated creditor's claim.
- **DM knowledge:** Jeod owns the bank in secret. His information source, intended use of the fragment, and how much his representative knows remain open. The bank has no knowledge connecting the stone to the attacks.
- **Clue in the offer:** the prepared box and unusually large reward show that the employer considers the object dangerous and valuable. They do not identify its owner or establish what the stone does.

## The containment box

**Established lore:** **Severite** is the Imperial / Common name; **Mlčoun** is Meredic. It is a Veil-suppressing metal used for shackles, blades, and armor. See [[Organisations/Order of the Just#Severite / Mlčoun]].

**Proposed prop:** a lockable metal case with a Severite lining and dense compressible foam that prevents the enclosed stone from shifting. Foam secures it mechanically; Severite is the proposed means of magical suppression. Supply a mundane transfer implement, such as tongs, so the no-touch instruction is actionable.

Before play, decide what the closed case suppresses: influence on its carrier, supernatural detection, effects originating from the stone, or some combination. Set whether an open lid, damaged lining, or prolonged containment matters. Containing the fragment does not stop the independent Herald rites. It also does not make the carrier invisible to ordinary pursuit.

## Incident that starts the hunt — open

An incident exposes the stone's presence or significance and prompts competing efforts to obtain it. The incident itself, its location, witnesses, and information routes remain undecided. It need not be a ritual attack.

| Hunter                     | Interest and response                                                                        | Preparation still needed                                                                                  |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Bank / Isthar**          | Obtain the stone through the consultate.                                                     | What reaches Jeod, what he verifies, and the lead supplied with the offer.                                |
| **Mlčenliví / Ilrusk**     | Seek the stone through Ilrusk's network.                                                     | His information source, orders, and the roles of Konrad and other agents.                                 |
| **Selith and the Heralds** | Acquire a fragment for Selith's ambition to restore Orion through himself and his followers. | How they recognize the opportunity, locate it, and divide effort between pursuit and their ongoing rites. |

These factions need not learn simultaneously or know the same things. Hněv's participation in the hunt is an optional further development requiring its own evidence. Tillibor commissioning a murder investigation does not itself give him knowledge of the stone.

### Jeod's agent when the firm is absent

The [three-day sacred timeline](orestor-sacred-timeline.md) removes the firm and PCs as a counterfactual, but Jeod still has agency. In its first three days **he has no report identifying the violet crystal**, so there is no bank hunt. Once a credible report reaches him, the no-firm continuation needs a response.

**Working response:** once Jeod receives a credible report of the crystal, he sends for **[[People/Kate|Kate]]**, his apprentice and established field agent. She is not waiting in Orestor at the ship's arrival and does not begin a hunt before Jeod has learned what arrived. Her current location, travel time, instructions, and first local contact need to be set when the discovery incident is chosen. Without the firm, she can lead Jeod's recovery effort. In actual play, the bank can commission the firm while Jeod summons her as a later backup; her arrival follows the message and journey, not an automatic deadline or a scripted collision with the party.

If Kate and the firm both pursue the fragment, decide what each has been told and how their instructions differ. Neither Kate's presence nor Piere's independent arrival on the ship makes either of them an automatic stone detector or resolves Bery's custody by itself.

Track actual custody and each faction's information in the campaign state once play begins; [the sacred timeline](orestor-sacred-timeline.md) describes the no-firm baseline. If the party already holds the fragment, the offer becomes one of containment and delivery. If it has left Orestor, the hunt follows that outcome. Recovery can succeed while the attacks remain unresolved; the investigation can succeed while another faction holds the stone.
