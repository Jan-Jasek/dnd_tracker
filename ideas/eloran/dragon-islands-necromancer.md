# Dragon Islands — the dormant necromancer (open idea)

> Part of: [[Places/Darkenwold]] | See also: [[Places/Dragon Islands]], [[People/Orion]]
> **Open idea, not canon.** A separate story, unrelated to Alastor's hunt for Marie.

- **Who:** one of Orion's early, failed attempts to create followers — a Prastarý. He went mad. A thanatomancer tormented by the souls he consumed, which share his body.
- **Where:** left dormant by Orion near **Darkenwold**, in the far north-east of the archipelago, close to the coasts where slavers capture Dragonborn.
- **Why it matters:** a death-and-corpses fragment, such as Matyáš's crystal, could pull at him or stir him.
- **Seed for later acts:** an older curse, from before the dragons and tied to the fey and to the necromancer, may lie dormant in the **light-eyed** natives. Folklore only for now.
- **Open:** what wakes him, what he wants, and whether the slave raids on the Dragonborn coasts have already disturbed him.
